# Six new shots for teaser v2 (Google Flow, Veo 3.1 Fast, 720p, 8 s each)
Goal: give the teaser what The King trailer has and v1 lacks: a quiet open, the hero alone and silent, night fire,
one burst of action, the turn (crown), the hero walking alone into the unknown, then silence and the title.
Use "Frames to Video" with one of Tigran's stills as the first frame when it fits; otherwise text-to-video.
Every prompt ends with sound only (no music, no speech): the score is laid in the edit.

## Look line (paste into every prompt)
Muted desaturated palette, overcast diffused daylight, cold grey-green exteriors, warm amber candle and firelight interiors, deep shadows, 35mm film grain, anamorphic 2.39:1 feel, shot on Alexa, natural light, no lens flares, no saturated colours except deep red cloth.

## 1 · OPEN (replaces the 3 s of black)
Dawn on a high Armenian plateau, thin mist over tall dry grass moving in the wind. Out of focus in the background, a row of tall spears with dark red banners stands still. Snow-capped Mount Ararat a faint silhouette on the horizon. Static camera, very slow push-in at grass level. Sound: wind in grass, distant banner cloth flapping, a single far crow.

## 2 · THE WEIGHT (the king alone)
An old Armenian king in his sixties with grey beard and a gold eight-pointed crown stands alone on a stone city wall at dusk, red cloak moving in the wind, looking out at distant mountains. He does not move. Camera behind and to the side of him, slow lateral drift. Cold blue dusk, last warm light on the horizon. Sound: strong wind, cloak fabric, a distant city murmur below.

## 3 · NIGHT FIRES
Night. Seen from the top of a city wall, a whole valley below filled with hundreds of Roman campfires, smoke drifting. In the foreground, embers float up past the camera from a brazier. Slow tilt down from the dark sky to the fires. Sound: crackling brazier, distant horns, far-off hammering, wind.

## 4 · CLASH (one burst for the fast section)
Armoured Armenian cataphract cavalry in scale armour charge through dust straight toward the camera, low angle, horses filling the frame, then impact against a wall of red Roman shields. Handheld, fast, chaotic, motion blur. Overcast grey light, dust. Sound: thundering hooves, metal impact, horse screams, shouting.

## 5 · THE CROWN
Extreme close-up: old weathered hands slowly lift a heavy gold crown set with red stones from a grey-haired head, in dim candlelight. The crown is lowered out of frame. Shallow depth of field, slow. Sound: silence except a soft breath and the faint metallic touch of the crown.

## 6 · WALKING OUT (last image before the title)
Wide shot from behind: the old king, bareheaded, in a red cloak, walks alone on foot out through a huge open wooden gate carved with winged lions, toward long lines of Roman legionaries waiting in the distance under a flat grey sky. Static camera, he gets smaller. Sound: his footsteps on stone, wind, then everything drops to silence.

## Order in v2 (≈45 s)
1 open → tent (v1 2-3) → hoof + procession (v1 4-6) → city (v1 7-8) → throne hall (v1 9-10) → 2 the weight (hold 4 s) → 3 night fires → legion (v1 11-12) → 4 clash (cut in 3-4 pieces, 0.5 s each) → 5 crown → gate (v1 13) → 6 walking out → 2 s black silence → title.
