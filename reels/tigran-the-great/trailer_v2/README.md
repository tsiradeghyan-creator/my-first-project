# Tigran the Great — trailer v2
69.8 s, 1280x720, 24 fps, 2.39:1 letterbox, -16 LUFS. Built by build_trailer.py (EDL.txt = cut list).
Structure after The King trailer: slow world → "THEN ROME CAME" pulse → son in the tent (quiet hold) → fire + 4 fast battle cuts
→ hard cut to silence at the gate → hands: ring, sword, crown (emotional pad, bells) → 2 s black silence → title braam.
Sound: score and SFX fully synthesized in numpy (drone, pads Dm-Bb-F-A, 72 bpm pulse, 140 bpm drums, booms, braam, riser,
footsteps, crackle, creak) + diegetic audio of the Veo/Dreamina clips. The old "Vanguard" music is not used.
Cards are English, factual (empire Caspian→Mediterranean, King of Kings, Rome, the son with Pompey); no fake press quotes.
