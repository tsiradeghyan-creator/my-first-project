# Tigran's own prompts for teaser v1 (Meta Muse, 8 shots) — the house standard
Saved as the reference for every future video prompt on this project. Source: Tigran, 2026-11-02.

## The method (what every prompt has)
1. **ROLES** — who is who, by screen side (LEFT/RIGHT, foreground/background), who speaks, who is absent.
2. **ACTION** — 10 s, exactly two shots, one hard cut; second-by-second beats; the cut is motivated
   (after the line ends, on hoof contact, on a footfall); shot B continues the same action, never resets.
3. **CAMERA** — per shot: framing, lens feel (24/28/35/50/65/75/85/100mm), move (locked / slow push-in / track back
   at subject speed / crane rise), axis and eyeline kept, direction of travel kept.
4. **CONTINUITY** — preserve faces, costume, props, light direction from the supplied image;
   "Skin exactly as in the first frame, do not add spots, pits or blemishes"; subtle 35mm grain; no smoothing.
5. **AUDIO** — voice described by character (age, register, position), dialogue in Armenian with exact lip sync,
   ambience continuous across the cut, sound bridge into the next scene (06 → marching), space left for lines added in the edit.
   No music, subtitles or text.
Instructions in English, dialogue in Armenian.

## Story beats inside teaser v1
- 01 OTS on Pompey: «Ինչո՞ւ պիտի վստահեմ քեզ» (Why should I trust you?)
- 02 CU Tigranes the Younger: «Որովհետև նա իմ հայրն է» (Because he is my father) — "he" = King Tigranes
- 03 hoof · 04 king + four vassal kings · 05 Tigranakert from the wall · 06 envoy before the king (envoy's demand added in edit)
- 07 legion in the mountain pass · 08 gates opening

## The 8 prompts (verbatim)

### 01 · Over Pompey's shoulder — the question
ROLES: Pompey is the older Roman commander in the blurred LEFT foreground, seen from behind. Tigranes the Younger is the dark-haired young man facing him on the RIGHT, wearing the charcoal cloak and bronze brooch. Only Pompey speaks in this clip.
ACTION: 10 s, two shots, one hard cut. 0–2 s natural breathing, slight fire movement, the young man watches Pompey. 2–5 s Pompey asks in Armenian: «Ինչո՞ւ պիտի վստահեմ քեզ»։ At 5 s hard cut. 5–10 s hold on the young man listening in silence, mouth closed, no answer yet.
CAMERA: Shot A reference OTS, 50mm, locked. Shot B tighter OTS, 75mm, very slow straight push-in, a little of Pompey's shoulder left, eyeline screen-left.
CONTINUITY: exact faces, hair, costumes, brooch, tent, firelight direction. Skin exactly as in the first frame, no spots, pits or blemishes. Natural micro-movements, subtle 35mm grain, uneven light, no smoothing.
AUDIO: Pompey mature, low, measured voice from the foreground; young man silent. Quiet fire crackle, canvas movement, breathing. Continuous ambience across the cut. No other dialogue, music, subtitles or text.

### 02 · Close-up of Tigranes the Younger — the answer
ROLES: Tigranes the Younger in the close-up; Pompey off-screen LEFT; King Tigranes absent.
ACTION: 0–2 s holds Pompey's gaze in silence. 2–5 s answers in Armenian: «Որովհետև նա իմ հայրն է»։ At 5.5 s, after the sentence, hard cut. 5.5–10 s restrained expression, one quiet breath, gaze fixed on Pompey. "My father" = King Tigranes.
CAMERA: Shot A supplied close-up, 85mm, locked. Shot B extreme close-up eyes/nose/mouth, 100mm, locked, same side of the axis, same screen-left eyeline.
CONTINUITY: exact identity, dark hair, stubble, charcoal wool cloak, embroidered collar, bronze brooch; reference firelight and background; skin clause; 35mm grain, micro-movements.
AUDIO: one younger male voice, quiet, controlled, firm; exact Armenian lip sync; Pompey silent; low fire crackle and breathing across the cut. Nothing else.

### 03 · The hoof strike
ROLES: the reference hoof in the foreground; other legs keep their background positions.
ACTION: 0–4 s the suspended hoof completes one natural step onto rocky ground, grit scatters. At contact (~4 s) hard cut. 4–10 s same step continues: weight transfers forward, hoof lifts again; background legs in walking rhythm.
CAMERA: A ground-level close-up, 85mm, locked. B ground-level side detail ~35° further around the same hoof, 100mm, locked; direction of travel and contact position matched across the cut.
CONTINUITY: same hoof, markings, colour, ground, sunlight; walking gait, not a gallop; dust from contact, settles; real-time motion, 35mm grain, weight.
AUDIO: heavy hoof impact at contact, small stones, quieter hoofbeats around, faint tack; impact carried across the cut. No dialogue, music, text.

### 04 · Tigranes on horseback and the four vassal kings
ROLES: King Tigranes on the dark horse in the centre; four crowned vassal kings WALK beside him — green and ivory LEFT, blue and brown RIGHT.
ACTION: 0–5 s procession continues from the supplied positions, measured walk, places kept. At 5 s hard cut on a hoof landing. 5–10 s same movement, on Tigranes' composed posture.
CAMERA: A low frontal wide, 35mm, tracking back slightly faster than the group, revealing more ground. B frontal medium of the king, 65mm, tracking back at horse speed, vassals partly at the edges.
CONTINUITY: all five identities, crowns, colours, horse markings, saddle, architecture, mountains; vassals on foot; gait matched across the cut; skin clause; cloth movement; 35mm grain.
AUDIO: walking hoofbeats, individual footsteps, leather creaks, restrained metal; continuous rhythm across the cut; light wind, distant crowd murmur without words. No music or text.

### 05 · Tigranakert from the wall
ROLES: guards on the foreground wall and the city's inhabitants stay part of the established environment.
ACTION: 0–5 s market already under way, small groups walk, fabrics and banners move. At 5 s hard cut. 5–10 s pedestrians in one market street already visible in shot A.
CAMERA: A elevated wide, 24mm, slow vertical crane rise revealing more city beyond the wall. B tighter elevated view of the central market, 65mm, locked, same viewing direction.
CONTINUITY: exact layout, temples, walls, mountains, water, banners, daylight; B is a closer view of the same city; buildings rigid; believable scale; 35mm grain; atmospheric depth.
AUDIO: distant market murmur, footsteps, banners, remote animals; ambience slightly closer after the cut, continuous. No intelligible dialogue or music, no titles or letters.

### 06 · The envoy before Tigranes
ROLES: dark-haired envoy in the ivory cloak LEFT; King Tigranes, older, crowned, burgundy cloak, on the raised steps RIGHT; guards behind.
ACTION: 0–5 s from the open-hand gesture the envoy lets his hand settle and waits. At 5 s hard cut. 5–10 s Tigranes watches in silence, upright, still, withholding his answer.
CAMERA: A low wide two-shot, 35mm, locked. B medium close-up of Tigranes, 75mm, very slow push-in, gaze screen-left, same side of the axis.
CONTINUITY: both faces, costumes, crown, positions, steps, columns, throne, guards, light sources; envoy below the king's level; skin clause; 35mm grain; breathing.
AUDIO: palace room tone, faint fire, cloth, breathing; no intelligible dialogue — space for the envoy's demand in the edit; in the final second faint marching footsteps as a sound bridge. No music, subtitles, text.

### 07 · The legion in the mountain pass
ROLES: the Roman legion marches away through the pass; the mounted officer stays in the column.
ACTION: 0–5 s march continues away toward centre-right, varied steps on rocky road. At 5 s hard cut on a nearby footfall. 5–10 s close view of the nearest boots, lower legs and shield edges.
CAMERA: A low rear three-quarter wide, 35mm, slow track forward behind the nearest rank at walking speed. B low rear three-quarter detail, 65mm, locked, boots and shields move away through frame, travel direction kept.
CONTINUITY: armour, shields, standards, formation, horse, mountains, daylight; same road and speed across the cut; equipment sway, restrained dust, 35mm grain, weight.
AUDIO: layered boots on stones, leather, light armour rattle, occasional hoofbeats, mountain wind; rhythm continuous, closer impacts in B. No speech, music, text.

### 08 · The half-open gates
ROLES: two massive timber gate leaves and four guards as in the image.
ACTION: 0–5 s from the exact partly-open position the leaves keep opening toward camera on fixed outer hinges; guards clear of the timber. At 5 s hard cut. 5–10 s same opening from the exact angle reached; end while still moving.
CAMERA: A low central wide, 28mm, locked. B closer low view of the right leaf, its carved winged lion and the widening gap, 50mm, locked, same side.
CONTINUITY: timber, hinges, iron, carvings, guards, city beyond, sun direction; opening never resets or reverses; light spreads over the floor; 35mm grain, worn surfaces, gate weight.
AUDIO: deep timber strain, hinge friction, iron creaks continuing across the cut; city murmur clearer through the gap; quiet wind. No dialogue, music, text.
