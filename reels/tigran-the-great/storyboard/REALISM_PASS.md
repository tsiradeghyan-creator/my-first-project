# Realism pass for Nano Banana Pro (Tigran's note: stills look like a cartoon)
Two tools:
A) REALISM PASS — attach a finished still (e.g. stills/09.jpg) and re-photograph it as real footage. Same picture, real look.
B) DIRECTOR LOOK — the light/colour/camera block to put at the end of every new frame prompt.
Rules learned: never write "cinematic, epic, majestic, dramatic, hyper-detailed, 8K" (they push the illustration look).
Ask for a frame grab, flat motivated light, imperfections, low contrast, dull materials.

## A) REALISM PASS (attach the still, paste this)
```
Re-photograph the attached image as a real frame grab from a live-action historical film shot on location with real actors, real extras and real costumes. Keep exactly the same composition, framing, people, poses, face, crown, costume, city and camera angle. Do not change the man's face, only make it a real photographed human face.

Remove every illustrated, painted, CGI or video-game quality. Nothing is too clean, too symmetrical or too perfect: armour scales slightly uneven, bent and dented, dull brass not shiny gold; wool cloak heavy, pilled, wet at the hem, mud splashed up to the knees; extras look like tired real people of different ages and builds, unshaven, dirty, some faces half turned away, slight motion blur on the walking extras.

Real skin: lined, weathered, uneven tone, visible pores and stubble, dark eye sockets, matte, no retouching, no smoothing, no shine, no added blemishes.

Light as a cinematographer would light it: one source only, a heavy overcast sky from above and slightly left; soft, low-contrast, directionless daylight; faces partly in shadow; no rim light, no backlight glow, no spotlight on the hero. The fires are far away and light nothing near the camera. Smoke and haze between the planes; the background is paler and lower in contrast than the foreground.

Colour of a dark historical drama: muted and desaturated, cool steel grey sky, olive-brown mud, stone in grey-beige; the cloak is a dark wine colour close to brown, metal is dull brass and iron; skin natural, never orange. Low contrast, lifted blacks, highlights never clipped, fine 35mm film grain, slight softness at the frame edges from an old anamorphic lens, 2.39:1 feel.

One single full-frame 16:9 image. No text, no numbers, no borders.
```

## B) DIRECTOR LOOK (end of every new frame prompt)
```
DIRECTOR'S LOOK: Real live-action frame grab from a historical drama shot on location, not an illustration, not a painting, not CGI. Real actors and real extras, tired, dirty, imperfect; no symmetry, no hero pose, no glamour. Costumes worn, wet, muddy, patched; metal dull and dented; wool heavy and pilled.
LIGHT: motivated sources only — overcast sky as one soft, low-contrast key from above; interiors lit only by real flames; no rim light, no backlight glow, no spotlight on the main character; faces partly in shadow, dark eye sockets.
COLOUR: muted, desaturated, cold grey and olive-brown; burgundy reads as dark wine, gold as dull brass; skin natural, never orange or glossy. Low contrast, lifted blacks, highlights never clipped.
CAMERA: old anamorphic lens character, soft edges, shallow depth where the lens says so, fine 35mm film grain, slight motion blur on moving people.
SKIN: real pores, wrinkles, stubble, uneven tone, matte, no retouching, no smoothing, no added blemishes; faces exactly as in the reference.
One single full-frame 16:9 image, no text, no numbers, no borders.
```
