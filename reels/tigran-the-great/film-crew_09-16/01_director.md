# 01 · Director — block 09–16

## Dramatic core
An old king who has just lost his city wants to stop the retreat; Rome's legion is already crossing the river after him; with one raised hand he turns a beaten army back into an attacking one.

## Subtext
Power is not noise. Everyone around Tigran moves, shouts, bleeds, runs; he is the only still thing in the frame, and the stillness is the power. The block is not about the battle — it is about the moment a decision passes from a face to a hand to two thousand horsemen.

## Visual thesis
**The world moves past the king; the king does not move until his hand moves — and then the whole world moves with it.**
Photographable: in shots 1, 2, 6, 7 Tigran is the only figure who is still while everything else (soldiers, smoke, banners, riders) travels; the first real motion he makes is the hand (6), and the cavalry charge (8) is that motion multiplied.

## Direction rules (from the lens — `styles/the-king-2019.md`, researched 2026-11-03)
1. **The king is still and silent.** No speech, no shouting, no grimace. Decision is shown by eyes, breath, jaw — never by acting big.
2. **War is weight, not spectacle.** Mud, wet wool, tired men, cold river. No slow-motion heroics, no blood on screen; violence is implied by the approach, the volley, the splash.
3. **Flat grey daylight outside, fire only where the city burns.** No golden-hour glamour; the only warm light is the burning city in shots 1–2.
4. **The camera observes, the action moves.** Slow, motivated moves on the king; handheld energy only when the army moves (5, 8).
5. **Wide = painting, battle = eye level.** Geography is a still, painterly wide where armies are dark masses against a pale sky; inside the fight the camera is at human height, close, tight.
6. **Hold, then break.** Slow retreat and decision, a sharp quiet before the signal, then fast battle cuts that end before contact — black.

## Performance notes
- **King Tigran (old, grey-haired):** objective — stop the rout. Obstacle — his army is broken and Rome is in the river. Visible behaviour: slow heavy walk that does not hurry (1); eyes that search then lock, one nasal breath, jaw tightens, chin lifts a few degrees (2); hand curls, tendons tighten, then opens (6); arm held high without trembling, then one hard chop, feet never move (7). Never: tears, shouting, open mouth, looking into the lens.
- **Horse archer:** total focus; draw to the cheek, release, already reaching for the next arrow — a professional, not a hero.
- **Legionary (boot only) and the shield wall:** discipline, rhythm, one body.
- **Cavalry riders:** lean forward, spears lowered, no faces in focus; the banner carries the emotion.

## Edit intent
Two halves. **Hold** (1–2): slow, warm fire, the king's face; same-axis cut-in 1→2. **Hard scene change** to the cold river (2→3) on the king's eyeline to frame-right — he looks toward where the battle is. **Accelerate** (3→4→5): wide → insert → medium, threat then answer. **Stop** (6): near silence, heartbeat, the hand. **Release** (7→8): the hand's chop is the cut point; the war horn bridges into the charge; the charge cuts to black a moment before contact (the trailer v3 act-break grammar: fade to black / hard black).
