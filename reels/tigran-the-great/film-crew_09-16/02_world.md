# 02 · Production Designer — block 09–16

## SET A · Burning city street — EXT · late afternoon, overcast · 1st c. BC
**Floor plan (compass, master looks SOUTH up the street):** a wide unpaved street of deep churned mud runs north–south. The camera stands at the NORTH end looking SOUTH; the king walks NORTH toward the camera. Ruined pale limestone walls on the EAST side (frame-left), a broken colonnaded temple and stone towers at the far SOUTH end (frame-centre/left background), a burning stone fortress block with an arched gate on the WEST side (frame-right background). Smoke drifts from frame-right (west) to frame-left (east). Master side: the camera never goes south of the king.
**Locked set text — A:**
"a wide street of deep churned brown mud in a ruined ancient stone city; pale limestone walls broken and blackened on the left, a collapsed colonnaded temple and square stone towers in the background, buildings on fire with orange flames in the windows, thick black and grey smoke rolling across a low overcast sky, ash and embers falling, discarded spears, torn cloth and broken shields lying in the mud"
**Hero props:** discarded spears and shields in the mud (static) · fire in windows (burns throughout) · smoke (drifts W→E throughout).
**Light sources in set:** overcast sky (soft, top/front-left), fires (warm, background, frame-right and centre).

## SET B · River valley in the highlands — EXT · day, overcast, cold · 1st c. BC
**Floor plan (compass, master looks NORTH across the river):** a fast shallow mountain river runs from the far mountains (north-west) toward the camera's foreground (south-east), dividing the valley. The **WEST bank (frame-left)** is a grassy rise held by the Armenians; the **EAST bank (frame-right)** is a stony, muddy shore where the Roman legion stands in a shield wall facing west. A low grassy **hill** on the west side behind the Armenian line is where the king stands (shots 6–7) with his reserve cavalry. Snow-capped mountains and mist close the valley to the north. Master side: SOUTH of the river line — the camera always looks north, so Armenians stay frame-left and Romans frame-right.

## Palette
- Dominant: cold slate grey (sky, river, steel) and wet earth brown (mud).
- Secondary: desaturated olive-ochre (grass, wool, leather).
- Accent: deep burgundy wine of the king's cloak and the Armenian banners — the only "warm" colour in Set B, so the eye finds the Armenians.
- Danger: dull Roman red of the shields — darker and browner than the burgundy, never the same hue; orange fire exists only in Set A.

## Notes to other departments
- No readable text anywhere (shields and banners carry only emblems: Roman gold sun/wings, Armenian gold eight-pointed star).
- Keep the Roman red and the Armenian burgundy separate in every prompt wording ("dull brick-red Roman shields" vs "deep burgundy wine-coloured banners").
