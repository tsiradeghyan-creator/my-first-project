# 04 · Cinematographer — block 09–16

## Lens plan (meaning)
- **24–35mm — the field and the army.** Space, mass, mud (shots 3, 7, 8).
- **40–50mm — the king among men.** Human perspective, he is one of them (shot 1).
- **65mm — the fighter.** Tracking the archer, compresses the riders behind (shot 5).
- **85–100mm — the decision.** Isolation, shallow focus: face, hand, boot (shots 2, 4, 6).

## Format & texture
Large-format digital look (the lens was shot on a 65mm-format sensor): "large-format digital image, shallow depth of field, smooth tonal roll-off in the grey sky"; no lens flares, no orange-teal; natural grain, no glossy sharpening; shallow depth of field only on the long lenses. Composed for a 2.39:1 extraction from 16:9: heads, crown, hand and banner tip stay inside the central band (no key action in the top or bottom 12% of the frame).

## Light plan
**LIGHT A — burning city (1, 2):** "soft flat overcast daylight from above and slightly frame-left, cool grey; warm orange fire glow from the burning buildings in the background, rim-lighting the frame-right side of his face and crown; smoke-filtered, low contrast, no sunlight"
**LIGHT B — river valley (3–8):** "cold soft overcast daylight from a heavy grey sky, slightly brighter from frame-left, no sun, no shadows on the ground, low contrast, desaturated, mist in the distance"
Light does not change within a set. The warm fire never appears in Set B.

## Axis map
- **Set A:** line = the street (king walks toward camera, N). Camera stays north of the king, on the street axis. Cut 1→2 is a same-axis cut-in (size change WS/MS → CU). The king's eyeline at the end of 2 is **frame-right** — toward the river battle that follows.
- **Set B:** line = the river, running away from camera. Camera always on the south side looking north. **Armenians frame-left, moving/shooting L→R; Romans frame-right, facing L.** The king on the hill (6–7) looks frame-right toward the Romans. No planned crossing.

## Camera grammar
- Moves only when motivated: the king's walk (slow push), the decision (micro push), geography (near locked-off painterly wide, the lens's "small dark army masses against a pale sky"), the archer and the charge (tracking at horse speed, handheld energy), the signal (slight tilt up).
- Battle shots (4, 5, 8) at human eye level or lower, close to bodies and horses, never a god's-eye view.
- One dominant move per shot. No orbits, no drone sweeps, no speed ramps, no slow motion.
- Signature of the lens spent twice only: the long static hold on the face (2) and the hand (6).

## Coverage strategy
Planned montage — one designed shot per beat, each with a still keyframe already approved by Tigran. No alternate coverage generated.

## Grade
Applied in the edit (The King Lab transfer, trailer_v3/ref_stats.npy): Set A exterior warmth from fire only; Set B grey-green, desaturated, burgundy kept as the one strong colour. Prompts describe light, not a grade.
