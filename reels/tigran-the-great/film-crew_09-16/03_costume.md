## LOOK · King Tigran (shots 1, 2, 6, 7)
"an Armenian king in his seventies, tall and lean; deeply lined weathered face, heavy brows, long straight nose, thin firm mouth, grey eyes under hooded lids, retaining real fine pores and skin texture; long wavy grey hair to the collar, no beard, grey stubble; tall dark-gold spiked crown with an embossed eight-pointed star at the front; bronze-gold scale cuirass of round overlapping scales over a burgundy tunic, a dark leather belt with a round gold buckle; deep burgundy wine-coloured heavy wool cloak, worn and muddy at the hem, fastened at the left shoulder with a round gold brooch; engraved gold vambraces with an eight-pointed star on both forearms; a short sword in a leather scabbard at the left hip; mud-caked leather boots"
Signature item: the crown + the burgundy cloak with the round gold brooch.
