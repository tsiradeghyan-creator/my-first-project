# 00 · Producer brief — Tigran the Great, teaser block 09–16

**Project:** "Tigran the Great" (Տիգրան Մեծ) — historical war epic, Armenia, 1st century BC. Teaser/trailer v3 (reels/tigran-the-great/trailer_v3/) gets a new middle block.
**Logline of the block:** the old king retreats from his burning city, decides to fight, and at the river turns the retreat into a counter-attack with one gesture of his hand.
**Deliverable:** 8 generated clips (storyboard frames 09–16) that cut into one 25.5 s sequence: retreat → river battle → the signal → counter-attack.
**Duration (edit):** 25.5 s (4.0 · 3.0 · 4.5 · 2.0 · 2.5 · 2.0 · 3.0 · 4.5). Clips are generated 4–6 s each so the editor has handles.
**Ratio / platform / resolution:** 16:9 generation, finished in 2.39:1 letterbox (picture area 1280×536 — keep heads and the crown inside the central 2.39 band); 720p; YouTube/Instagram trailer.
**Target model:** Seedance 2.5 (Dreamina), mode First and Last Frames with **first frame only** = Tigran's clean stills. Rules of `seedance-25-director` apply.
**Style lens:** *The King* (2019) — the film the whole trailer is graded and cut after (reels/the-king-teaser/ANALYSIS.md, trailer_v3/ref_stats.npy). Card: `styles/the-king-2019.md` (researched for this project). Why: grounded, mud-and-iron medieval realism, a still and silent king, flat overcast exteriors, war shown as weight and exhaustion rather than spectacle — exactly the tone Tigran asked for after v2 ("sounded like a game trailer… take everything from The King").

**Shot budget:** fixed by the storyboard = 8 shots. Check against the lens: 25.5 s ÷ 8 = 3.2 s average; The King trailer averages 139.5 s ÷ 54 cuts ≈ 2.6 s, its battle section runs faster (0.6–1 s). Our block is retreat (slow) + battle (fast) → 3.2 s is right; no extra shots.

**Plan of passes:** Director (beats + thesis) → World (two sets: burning city street, river valley) → Costume (King, horse archer, Roman legionary, Armenian cavalry) → DP (axis: Armenians L→R, Romans frame-right) → Editor (contracts for 7 cuts) → Operator → Sound → storyboard (8 cards) → generation plan → review + validator + independent verifier.

**Assumptions:** no dialogue in this block; diegetic sound only, music/score added in the edit (as in trailer v3); colour grade applied in the edit (The King Lab transfer), so prompts describe light, not a LUT; historical battle violence kept to no blood/no impact on screen (moderation + the lens shows aftermath, not gore).
**Open questions:** none that change the plan.
