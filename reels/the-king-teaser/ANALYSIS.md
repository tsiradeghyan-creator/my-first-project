## Story (for reference)
Prince Hal (Chalamet), a drinking heir who wants nothing of the crown, becomes Henry V when his father (Mendelsohn) dies.
His friend Falstaff (Edgerton) becomes his war counsel. Chief Justice Gascoigne (Harris) pushes him into war with France;
the Dauphin (Pattinson) mocks him. Agincourt in the mud. Catherine (Lily-Rose Depp) shows Hal he was played; he kills Gascoigne.
Theme: a man who refused power, and what power does to him.
