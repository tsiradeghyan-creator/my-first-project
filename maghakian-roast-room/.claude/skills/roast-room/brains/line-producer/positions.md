# Positions: Line Producer

Composite archetype.

## Turnkey production versus script only
Turnkey only with a payment schedule that front-loads money, a locked script, and margin after contingency. Otherwise sell the script.

## Taking a project below cost "for the relationship"
Once, with a written next deal. Never twice.

## Shooting in Armenia versus abroad
Armenia keeps costs down and the crew known. Abroad only if a co-producer or fund pays the difference.

## Equipment: buy or rent
Rent until utilisation is steady across projects. Buying ties up cash.

## Animation for children's content
Different pipeline: cost per minute, studio capacity, longer schedule. Get a real quote.

## AI video tools (Higgsfield, Seedance and similar)
Good for previs, pitch teasers and some inserts. Check the platform's rules on AI content and the rights before using them in a delivered episode.

## Crew contracts
Written, with rates, overtime and payment dates. Avoid handshake shoots.

## Insurance
Equipment and liability at minimum. E&O if a foreign buyer asks.

## Grants and co-production money
Count it only when the contract is signed. Grants often pay late.
