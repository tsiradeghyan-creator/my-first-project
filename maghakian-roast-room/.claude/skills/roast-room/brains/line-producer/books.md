# Books: Line Producer (composite)

Composite archetype: no books of its own. This file lists the reference documents and guides the brain draws on, in place of books. No text is reproduced from any book; the commercial titles below were not fetched and are named only as further reading.

## Guides and documents in the corpus

- **ScreenSkills Producer's Handbook (HETV), 2020**, written for the HETV Skills Fund by a freelance line producer. Producer and line producer duties from script to delivery in UK drama: breakdown, cost report meetings, progress reports, deal memos, deliverables, child licences, the Pact/Bectu working day. Teaches LIN-F11, LIN-F16, LIN-F19. (S018)
- **ScreenSkills line producer skills checklist (unscripted).** Core duties: control expenditure, manage daily schedules with contingencies, compare progress against plan, supervise post. (S019)
- **PGA Code of Credits (feature and episodic).** Defines the line producer as the one person with primary responsibility for budget and logistics, with all below-the-line heads reporting to them. (S016, S017)
- **PMA pay guide (UK).** Crew rates banded by budget size. Teaches that rates scale with the show. (S020)
- **Roku Scripted Production Guidelines.** A streamer's public rules for budgets, cost reports, overages, milestone payments, incentives, QC time and close-out. Teaches LIN-F3, LIN-F5, LIN-F11, LIN-F15. (S022)
- **Netflix Originals Production and Post Requirements v2.1, 2017.** Camera and colour pipeline requirements; secondary cameras need approval. (S021)
- **Prime Video music rights requirements.** Cue sheets and full clearance. (S023)
- **WIPO, Rights clearance: a guide for independent filmmakers.** Chain of title and clearance as a planning and budget question. Teaches LIN-F7, LIN-P26. (S038)
- **A Guide to Child Performance Licensing in England (NNCEE, updated 2024)** and **ScreenSafe NZ, Working with Children.** Licence timing, hours, breaks, turnaround. Teaches LIN-F16. (S043, S041)
- **Government of Armenia Decision 412-N of 10 April 2025.** The cash rebate procedure and eligible expense list. Teaches LIN-F14. (S064)
