# Principles: Line Producer

Composite archetype.

- LIN-P1 Budget from the schedule. Confidence high.
- LIN-P2 At least ten percent contingency. Confidence high.
- LIN-P3 Cash flow beats paper profit. Confidence high.
- LIN-P4 Weather, locations and permits are schedule risks. Confidence high.
- LIN-P5 Children on set cost time and care. Confidence high.
- LIN-P6 Lock the script before the shoot. Confidence high.
- LIN-P7 Turnkey moves the risk to you; price it. Confidence high.
- LIN-P8 Budget post-production fully at the start. Confidence high.
- LIN-P9 Get the buyer's payment schedule in the contract, tied to deliverables you control. Confidence high.
- LIN-P10 Pay the crew on time, every time; a small industry remembers. Confidence high.
- LIN-P11 Changes from the buyer after lock are change orders with a price. Confidence medium.
- LIN-P12 Cost per finished minute is the number to compare projects. Confidence medium.
