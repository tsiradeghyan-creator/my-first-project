# Stories: Line Producer

Composite archetype. Typical cases, not real events.

- The 60-day payment: the buyer paid on delivery plus 60, the crew wanted Friday. The studio bridged it with the founders' savings. Lesson: cash-flow calendar.
- The winter exterior: a snow day cost three shooting days. Lesson: risk register and weather cover.
- The free rewrite on set: the buyer changed scenes during the shoot with no change order. Lesson: lock the script, price the changes.
