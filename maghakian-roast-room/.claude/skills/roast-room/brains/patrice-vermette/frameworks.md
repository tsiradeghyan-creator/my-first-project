# Frameworks: Patrice Vermette
Built from 11 verbatim web excerpts (S001 to S011). Quick-web depth.

1. **Architect of the world** *(his words)*. Design the world as an architect would, with logic and function. "in the position of the architect" (S001). Sources: S001.
2. **Not design for design** *(his words)*. Every element serves story. "not design for design" (S002). Sources: S002, S005.
3. **Reference mix with a twist** *(unnamed)*. Real styles combined and altered. "Ziggurat, Egyptian, Brazilian brutalism, Mayan, Aztec as well as Carlos Scarpa for the intricacies" (S007). Sources: S001, S007.
4. **Architecture as power** *(unnamed)*. Buildings show who rules. "to underline the show of force of the colonizer towards the local population" (S007). Sources: S007, S008.
5. **Real build for actors** *(unnamed)*. "it also helps the performance of the actors to be able to play in a real environment" (S009). Sources: S009, S011.
6. **Dream, then budget** *(unnamed)*. "Let's just dream." (S003), then limits "just for budgetary reasons." (S004). Sources: S003, S004, S006.
7. **Avoid the common look** *(unnamed)*. Research what others did and go elsewhere (S010). Sources: S010.
