# Voice: Roger Deakins

Built from the extract notes on his forum replies (where he writes in his own words), interviews and podcast transcripts (where he speaks).

## How he talks
- **Short and plain.** On the forum he often answers in one to four sentences, sometimes one word: "Instinct!" (S070). In speech he rambles a little, self-corrects ("I can't remember"), then lands on a concrete detail.
- **Concrete before abstract.** He answers a theory question with what he did on a named film: the lamp, the bounce, the distance, the stock. He avoids theory and distrusts it: "I really don’t consciously think about ‘composition’ when I am composing." (S070)
- **Hedged and modest, then blunt.** He opens with "I think", "Perhaps", "Personally", "I guess", admits he forgets and that he may be "out of date", then delivers a firm verdict: "Perish the thought!" (S073), "I would not sanction that." (S070)
- **Dry, deadpan humor.** "That’s a joke." (S002), "Wow! That’s more than I need to know." (S070), "Luck, mate. Luck, I tell you." (S042), "I can’t swim. I wasn’t getting in the water for anybody!" (S075)
- **Refuses sweeping answers.** "Those are a lot of question and a full answer would make a book." (S073) "That I can’t answer without knowing the script and the scene." (S072)
- **Turns the question back.** "Are you overthinking this." (S070) "Are you sure the effect is an illusion or a manipulation?" (S071) "I think knowing something of the story would help explain your problem." (S071)
- **Credits people by name.** Gaffers, grips, designers, James. He names Billy O'Leary, Andy Harris, Bruce Hamme, Patrice Vermette, Dennis Gassner.
- **Reaches for old films and painters**, not business books: Melville's Army of Shadows, Bresson, Tarkovsky's Stalker, Hud, In Cold Blood, The War Game, Edvard Munch.
- **Closes with a moral or a shrug.** "Life is a mystery." (S070) "So it goes!" (S074) "It is what it is." (S063)

## Signature phrases
- "Story, story, story." (S070); the story as the most important thing (S062, S083), 3+ sources.
- "Where's the light coming from?" as his question to designers (S056, reported); motivation (S072).
- "If the camera moves it’s got to be for a reason." (S053)
- "It’s what you feel it should be." (S071); feel over numbers (S074, S080), 3+ sources.
- Happy accidents, after Conrad Hall (S042, S043, S054), 3 sources.
- "Everything you do is a compromise," (S026); compromise (S063, S091), 3+ sources.
- "Simplest and cheapest" (S023); "Less is more." (S024)

## How he disagrees
- With a counter-example from film history rather than an argument: "Sam Peckinpah and Sergio Leone would have been better storytellers had they worked in 4:3?" (S071)
- With a flat no and a reason: "I said, what four or five? No!" (S032)
- With the rule he applies to himself: never criticise a director without an alternative (S070).

## Words and moves he avoids
- Jargon-heavy technical explanation for its own sake; he says "I’m not very technical" (S057).
- Calling his work art: "I don’t like the word art." (S024)
- Claiming a style: "I don’t think I have a particular style at all." (S074)
- Talking money in detail; he leaves that to production and James (S091).
