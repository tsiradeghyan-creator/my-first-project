# Frameworks: Roger Deakins
Built from 20 verbatim web excerpts (S001 to S020). Quick-web depth.

1. **Motivated light** *(unnamed)*. Every light has a source the audience would believe. Use: any lighting plan. Score a plan by how many lights have no in-world reason (guidance: none is the goal). "Where's the light coming from?" (S012). Sources: S010, S012.
2. **The set lights itself** *(unnamed)*. Design practicals and windows with the production designer so the set carries the light. "you spend a lot of time playing with ideas, sketching things out, and working with the art department to figure out how the set will kind of light itself" (S011). Sources: S010, S011.
3. **Minimum rig** *(unnamed)*. Fewer lights, faster setups, more shots in the day. "We had a lot of shots to do, so I wasn't going to put lights inside the rooms." (S006). Sources: S006.
4. **Reasoned camera move** *(unnamed)*. The camera moves only when the story asks. "it's got to mean something" (S001). Sources: S001, S002.
5. **Plan, then discover** *(unnamed)*. Heavy prep and scouting, then the scene is found with the actors. "But, I really believe in planning stuff out." (S008); "I like working a scene out with the actors on the day." (S003). Sources: S003, S008, S009.
6. **Rehearsal schematic** *(unnamed)*. Block in prep, print still frames, brief the grips. "I had still frames of certain moments within each shot" (S013). Sources: S013.
7. **Look from lighting, not grading** *(unnamed)*. One shooting LUT; the look is made on set. "that's lighting and design mostly" (S005). Sources: S005.
8. **Weather as a department** *(unnamed)*. Track it constantly when continuity depends on it. "we had to shoot in cloud, for both the mood and matching shots from one to another" (S013). Sources: S013.
9. **Small crew intimacy** *(unnamed)*. One camera, small crew, better performances. "the intimacy of working with a small crew will allow for both better performances in general as well as more personal imagery" (S007). Sources: S007.
