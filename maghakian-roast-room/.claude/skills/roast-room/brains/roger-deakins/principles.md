# Principles: Roger Deakins

Built from the extract notes on his forum replies as Keymaster (2022 to 2026), his interviews in American Cinematographer and elsewhere (2009 to 2026) and Team Deakins podcast transcripts. 31 decision rules, most-used first. "Sources" counts distinct sources that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Weighted toward the calls a producer or showrunner faces about the image: look, crew, cameras, schedule, money, effects, and the director relationship.

1. **The story decides every image; technique without story is nothing.** "The most important thing is the story," (S062) "Without the story and without actors and a performance and something that affects the audience emotionally, you’ve got nothing. Then you’ve just got technique." (S062) "I have never been attracted to a film because of its visual opportunities. I love stories rather than pictures for their own sake." (S071) Sources: 14 (S026, S042, S043, S045, S048, S062, S070, S071, S073, S075, S083, S096, S097, S098). Confidence: high. "you can't you can't shoot a bad script and make a good film." (S096)

2. **If the audience notices the shot, it failed.** "But I don't want anybody while they're watching the film go 'Oh! that's an amazing image,' because suddenly they're not in the story." (S044) "And you go, 'Oh God, I screwed it up.'" (S062) "Cinematography needs to fit in so it doesn’t stand out," (S081) Sources: 10 (S026, S039, S041, S044, S054, S062, S066, S077, S080, S081). Confidence: high.

3. **Every camera move needs a reason, and the reason can be emotion.** "If the camera moves it’s got to be for a reason. And I will say: watch a Bresson!" (S053) "camera movement can be motivated by something that is not necessarily to do with movement within a frame but emotion." (S073) "Far too often the camera moves in a film with little regard for story." (S073) Sources: 7 (S041, S052, S053, S054, S073, S091, S070). Confidence: high.

4. **Plan hard, then find the scene on the day with the actors.** "But, I really believe in planning stuff out." (S031) "A ‘go-to shot list’!!!! Perish the thought! I like working a scene out with the actors on the day." (S073) "You can do so much prep and think it through, but on the day, things just sort of strike you." (S056) Sources: 12 (S001, S031, S034, S043, S045, S054, S056, S057, S073, S079, S081, S089). Confidence: high.

5. **Make the look on set, not in the grade.** "Make the decision when you shoot not in post." (S072) "For me, grading is only finessing what you have recorded not attempting to ‘find a look’." (S070) "I always control color temperature on set and definitely NOT in post." (S073) Sources: 9 (S014, S041, S070, S071, S072, S073, S075, S077, S054). Confidence: high.

6. **Keep it simple; less is more.** "Less is more. It’s like minimalism, you know, don’t overthink it." (S024) "I like things to be simple and immediate." (S041) "I think my lighting's actually much simpler than the other stuff I see." (S031) Sources: 9 (S023, S024, S026, S031, S041, S053, S063, S080, S089). Confidence: high.

7. **Light comes from somewhere real; design the source into the set.** "You spend a lot of time playing with ideas, sketching things out, and working with the art department to figure out how the set will kind of light itself." (S039) "Like for any scene you need to consider what you want it to look like and what would motivate the light." (S072) Sources: 9 (S012, S033, S039, S056, S072, S073, S079, S091, S041). Confidence: high.

8. **One camera, constructed shots, no generic coverage.** "I believe in constructing a shot that has a purpose within a scene rather than leaving it to the editor to cut something out of coverage!" (S052) "It is an obvious truth that a second camera will compromise the shot that can be made by the first camera." (S052) Exception: a second camera for unscripted or intense moments, or when a retake is costly: "So, I am not against multi camera just don’t think it helps for every scene." (S072) Sources: 11 (S029, S032, S052, S053, S057, S070, S072, S082, S091, S092, S098). Confidence: high. On Skyfall: "there were sometimes where we shot with 11 cameras but that was because Sam and I wanted 11 cameras" (S098)

9. **Small crew, operate yourself, protect the actors.** "I like cameras to be small and intimate. I like shooting with a small crew, and I operate myself." (S017) "Create a quiet and welcoming place for the actors. Without them you have nothing." (S074) Sources: 8 (S017, S031, S033, S052, S074, S079, S080, S082). Confidence: high.

10. **Everything is a compromise; choose which ones, and make them last.** "Everything you do is a compromise," (S026) "there's a place where you have to compromise maybe, but you don't want to do it until the last possible moment." (S091) "it is up to the cinematographer to work within the limitations of a production." (S070) Sources: 11 (S002, S026, S063, S070, S073, S091, S034, S041, S092, S097, S098). Confidence: high.

11. **Spend where the scene needs it, save elsewhere.** "This is where we spent more money than the production thought we were going to spend, but I think it paid off. Then, in other places, like the stage set for the raid, we spent less than expected. You have to choose your battles." (S041) "using simple VFX work can allow money to go where it is really needed" (S071) Sources: 8 (S041, S045, S071, S073, S075, S091, S096, S097). Confidence: high. "you find that balance between what you you know what you can afford and what your wishes are" (S097)

12. **The simplest and cheapest tool that does the job.** "You don’t have to have film lights." (S023) "Simplest and cheapest, yeah." (S023) He bought factory plastic instead of film diffusion (S091) and industrial 216-equivalent roll (S073). Sources: 6 (S023, S026, S042, S073, S091, S070). Confidence: high.

13. **Do it in camera; use effects only with a reason, and shoot the foundation.** "I like to do as much in camera as possible, personally." (S063) "But does that make it a reason to use visual effects?" (S063) "I was always arguing for less." (S075) Sources: 9 (S026, S053, S054, S063, S071, S075, S014, S041, S092). Confidence: high.

14. **Limits feed the work; don't wish them away.** "If you always had everything you wanted you would have no excuses!" (S070) "Your work is what it is because it comes out of the challenges you face." (S002) Rafelson's warning that unlimited means stifled (S081). Sources: 6 (S002, S070, S073, S081, S093, S097). Confidence: high. On Fargo's low budget: "But in a way, that often helps you find your way of doing it, you know, less or less more approach, really." (S093)

15. **Read the weather and schedule by the light.** "I suggest you break down the camera angles and shoot each based on the angle of the sun" (S070) "There is little alternative to building some flexibility within a schedule." (S073) Sources: 13 (S036, S041, S045, S070, S072, S073, S077, S091, S057, S092, S093, S096, S098). Confidence: high.

16. **The film is the director's; speak up, but always with an alternative.** "My attitude is that we are here for the Director." (S089) "Never, and I mean never, be critical of a director’s choice without offering an alternative you might prefer." (S070) "I don’t think I’m there just to keep mum and push a button." (S053) Sources: 6 (S036, S053, S070, S089, S045, S081). Confidence: high.

17. **Choose the director and the story before the visuals; part ways early if you see different films.** "it’s the script that’s paramount to me." (S043) "they may see the script in a completely different way and in that case, it is best to part ways at that point." (S089) Sources: 7 (S031, S043, S057, S081, S089, S029, S062). Confidence: high.

18. **Trust your eye over the monitor and the numbers.** "you should always trust your eye. That means you need to train your eye" (S070) "Best not to get obsessed with numbers!" (S074) Sources: 6 (S070, S071, S072, S073, S074, S017). Confidence: high.

19. **Instinct over rules.** "Instinct!" (S070) "I do not like rules." (S070) "Rules are important, but they don’t define what you do." (S026) "If it feels right it may just well be right." (S072) Sources: 6 (S026, S070, S072, S073, S080, S091). Confidence: high.

20. **Coherence beats any single shot.** "Its always more important to prioritize the fluidity of the film over any one particular shot." (S071) "consistency is key to immersing the audience in a film" (S073) "It's the overall look that is the biggest challenge." (S012) Sources: 6 (S012, S031, S045, S063, S071, S073). Confidence: high.

21. **Test what you don't know before the day.** "I didn’t want to get there on the day and be screwed." (S041) "I think you should run a test to be sure which you yourself prefer" (S074) Sources: 5 (S017, S041, S074, S077, S075). Confidence: high.

22. **Prep time saves shoot time.** "All that time you spend in prep saves time when you’re shooting." (S057) "The more time you spend -- especially scouting and looking at locations and talking about the script -- it's time well spent." (S079) Sources: 7 (S057, S079, S091, S089, S093, S096, S092). Confidence: high. "we managed to catch up and do twice as much work because we'd rehearsed it." (S096)

23. **Performance outranks the purity of the light.** "I was never happy that I was cheating the light as much as I was but performance is important." (S071) "If you haven't got a performance you haven't got a film." (S079) Sources: 5 (S062, S071, S073, S079, S070). Confidence: high.

24. **Hold a shot instead of cutting when tension can build in the frame.** "But this is building tension just by holding a shot" (S053) The Sicario distant two-shot with no overs (S057). Sources: 3 (S053, S057, S070). Confidence: high.

25. **Life experience before technique.** "I think life experience is always more important than technical knowledge." (S048) "I don’t think unless you’ve lived a life, I don’t know what you’ve got to offer, really" (S062) Sources: 5 (S043, S048, S062, S088, S026). Confidence: high.

26. **Do work you feel for, or don't do it.** "I think it’s too much of an involvement of time in your life to work on something that you don’t actually have some sort of passion for, especially at my age." (S057) Sources: 4 (S057, S081, S031, S070). Confidence: high.

27. **Nerves mean you care.** "If you care about what you do, you’re going to be nervous about it." (S080) Sources: 4 (S080, S081, S036, S074). Confidence: high.

28. **Don't copy; find your own way.** "Your film is your film not theirs and you don’t learn by copying." (S072) "You can't learn cinematography and you can't copy it." (S048) Sources: 4 (S048, S062, S071, S072). Confidence: high.

29. **Make the day; every minute is a trade.** "But again, you know, you keep it simple and make sure you as well as possible make the day, frankly." (S094) "Because if you don't make that day, then you're going to lose time on another day where it might be, will be just as important." (S094) Sources: 4 (S094, S091, S002, S093). Confidence: high.

30. **Keep control of the image: avoid second units and multi-unit setups that shoot without the DP.** "When, okay, the second unit director or cameraman or whatever doesn't understand why you want that shot. They give you everything else, but not the one you really need or want." (S098) Sources: 4 (S032, S073, S098, S052). Confidence: high.

31. **Conceive the light, then the means to get it.** "But I think it's really, really important job of a cinematographer to conceive of what the light they want, what they want it to look like, but then conceive of how they can get there" (S098) Sources: 4 (S098, S071, S031, S041). Confidence: high.
