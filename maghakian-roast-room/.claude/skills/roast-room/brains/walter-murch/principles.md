# Principles: Walter Murch

Built from the extract notes on 94 sources (1996 to 2025), with the 2016 Web of Stories life interview (S085 to S104) as the longest single body of his own words. 28 decision rules, most-used first. "Sources" counts distinct sources that state or apply the rule. Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Weighted toward the calls a producer makes about post: length, schedule, staff, tools, money, notes and previews.

1. **Emotion comes first; when a cut cannot do everything, give up continuity, then rhythm, and never emotion.** "The last thing you want to throw overboard is emotion." (S103) "my recommendation is to let go from the bottom of the list up." (S021) It must be the correct emotion for that place in the film (S033, S103). Sources: 5 (S021, S033, S063, S084, S103). Confidence: high.

2. **Treat his rules and numbers as working tools, not law: use them, break them on purpose, never thoughtlessly.** "five layers is a threshold which should not be surpassed thoughtlessly" (S001) "Override this blinking thing if it feels like the right thing to do." (S020) "my understanding of it is about at the level of the way an acupuncturist understands where to put the needles" (S088) Sources: 7 (S001, S002, S020, S021, S024, S063, S088). Confidence: high.

3. **Impact needs contrast: no peak without a valley, no big sound without small sound, no greatness without the adequate around it.** "If there are no valleys, then it doesn't matter how high the mountains actually are; they won't seem high." (S006) "the monotony of greatness would spoil your performance" (S076) Sources: 8 (S001, S002, S006, S013, S076, S090, S092, S095). Confidence: high.

4. **Expect the first assembly to run long, then cut by budget: about 30 percent by trims, beyond that by surgery.** "You measure that length and then say, 'What is 30% of that.'" (S089) "If you want to go further than that, you can, and I have, we have, but to do that you have to actually cut out... do surgery" (S089) Sources: 6 (S021, S067, S089, S094, S096, S074). Confidence: high.

5. **Time the script before anyone shoots, and distrust timings shaved to please a studio.** "I believe that it's my duty as an editor to time the screenplay" (S096) "you know that these aren't real, and then you have nothing to guide you" (S096) Sources: 2 (S021, S096). Confidence: medium.

6. **To add something, take something away; cut even excellent material once it tires the audience.** "If I wanted to add something new, I had to take something else away." (S001) "It's like, you know, a 12-course meal, each course is delicious, but by the eighth course, oh, another delicious meal, you know? Another kind of fatigue sets in." (S096) Sources: 5 (S001, S002, S021, S096, S076). Confidence: high.

7. **A scene that looks essential on paper can still go; judge it in the whole film, and if it was a pivot, find a new one.** "you should be suspicious of those very scenes and wonder if this film can ride the bike without these training wheels." (S029) "a film can actually get more healthy by the reduction of something that ostensibly looked very healthy" (S094) Sources: 6 (S021, S029, S094, S095, S104, S076). Confidence: high.

8. **Protect the first viewing: keep the editor off the set and write down the first reaction.** "I want to protect my innocence so that I can transmit that to the audience unencumbered by my knowledge of how angry everyone was with each other that day." (S015) "I don't really want to know how big the set is, or what it smells like, as it burdens the mind unnecessarily." (S077) Sources: 6 (S015, S020, S021, S024, S029, S077). Confidence: high.

9. **Write down why a take is good or bad, never just a verdict; today's reject can be next month's answer.** "Frequently, what is bad when you first see it may be exactly what you want two months later when the structure of the film has changed." (S020) "the compost pile was always being turned" (S087) The third, "wrong" reading of a line saved the end of The Conversation (S088). Sources: 4 (S020, S021, S087, S088). Confidence: high.

10. **The editor serves the director but is paid to provoke; argue an idea up to three times, then drop it.** "If I've been turned down three times, I will abandon the idea." (S099) "What you’re being paid for is to contribute ideas" (S022) "if the director sees it and overrules it, that's the director's prerogative" (S006) Sources: 6 (S006, S022, S026, S039, S055, S099). Confidence: high.

11. **When the picture will not yield, try sound: it is the cheapest, most flexible lever in post.** "I can't solve this problem visually, but maybe I can solve it with sound." (S024) "If you did something really creative with sound, you could do something and do it relatively inexpensively." (S086) "If you use it creatively, sound can punch way above its weight." (S030) Sources: 6 (S022, S024, S030, S062, S086, S089). Confidence: high.

12. **Give one person the whole sound, as the DP owns the image, so nobody can point fingers.** "if there was a problem, people would say, well, it's his fault or no, that's his fault and so there was this finger pointing which we just wanted to eliminate" (S086) Sources: 4 (S006, S086, S090, S073). Confidence: high.

13. **Fill a story gap with the smallest new element before asking for reshoots.** "the ten unshot pages turned into one shot that we needed" (S088) "I had to make it work, which we did needing only one extra shot, a hand pulling a tape off a reel." (S062) Sources: 3 (S062, S088, S102). Confidence: high.

14. **Show the fix instead of arguing for it.** He recorded the dropped Apocalypse Now narration in his own voice and screened 30 minutes; the room said yes (S090, S009). When The Conversation was to be shelved he asked for one more week to try his ideas (S088). Sources: 3 (S009, S088, S090). Confidence: high.

15. **Cheaper tools do not make post cheaper; spend the savings on people and experiments, and keep more than one vendor alive.** "it was very easy for me as an editor to allow my assistants to have practice editing." (S098) "This is not really a healthy situation, that there is only one system that everybody uses." (S097) Sources: 7 (S006, S022, S028, S069, S097, S098, S100). Confidence: high.

16. **Switch tools by diving in, checking for hidden problems and asking working editors; style belongs to people, not machines.** "I just dive into it. I do a couple of days exploring, just to make sure there aren’t any real hidden snakes in there." (S027) "So I think it's more dependent on the people." (S074) Sources: 5 (S027, S037, S055, S069, S074). Confidence: high.

17. **Judge the cut and the mix the way the audience will meet them: big screen, real room, other people.** "I had to find a way to imagine that screen to be 30 feet wide, rather than being five inches wide." (S028) "what you're working on is not a miniature, but it's a mural" (S099) He remixed THX 1138 after a track mixed to a small video sounded too dense on the big print (S086). Sources: 6 (S001, S002, S021, S028, S086, S099). Confidence: high.

18. **Read feedback as a symptom; fix the cause, not the place people point to.** "the problem is not in the elbow. It’s somewhere else." (S021) "how they articulate a problem is nothing to do with what the real problem is" (S098) Sources: 4 (S013, S021, S087, S098). Confidence: high.

19. **Control what executives see and when; fix the one defect they noticed, because they will extend it to the whole film.** "it was a rule of thumb throughout the industry never to let producers or studio executives hear the final mix unless it could be screened with an answer print." (S001) "If they focus on some aspect of the film in a brief window of attention, and they detect a defect, they will extend that defect to the whole film." (S087) Sources: 4 (S001, S021, S086, S087). Confidence: high.

20. **Music should channel emotion the drama already made; earth it after the scene has built it.** "the music is not creating the emotion it's bringing an emotion and condensing it and bringing it to earth" (S055) "approach music in films the way you would approach administering drugs" (S104) Sources: 4 (S002, S055, S092, S104). Confidence: high.

21. **Leave the audience work to do: show less, imply more.** "And really the last creative act of any film is viewing by the audience." (S029) "the most powerful thing you can do to an audience in a film is not show them something, but make them think that they've seen it" (S096) Sources: 5 (S002, S004, S024, S029, S096). Confidence: high.

22. **Keep the happy accidents and fix only what is obviously wrong.** "I fix what is obviously wrong and preserve the happy accidents, clean up the overlaps, etc." (S021) "one of the things that you have to accustom yourself to in films is taking advantage of the imperfections that happen" (S094) Sources: 5 (S016, S021, S055, S092, S094). Confidence: high.

23. **Prefer the necessary shot to the beautiful one.** "I much prefer a necessary shot to a beautiful shot. If it is necessary and beautiful, so much the better." (S076) Sources: 1 (S076). Confidence: low.

24. **Story beats every rule, including his own.** "the right thing to do is ultimately whatever serves the storytelling, in the widest sense." (S001) "to tell the story in the most appropriate, engaging, economical way" (S076) Sources: 4 (S001, S002, S063, S076). Confidence: high.

25. **In documentary, source every claim, say whose story it is, and test hard material on audiences.** "at every opportunity, made sure that we had multiple sources for everything that is said and had visuals to back it up." (S017) "once you lose an audience in a film about mathematics or physics, you never get them back" (S101) Sources: 5 (S017, S023, S037, S038, S101). Confidence: high.

26. **Hire for compatibility and give heads of department rope; trust given is usually repaid.** "Yes, a lot of trust, but the surprising thing about trust is, if you’re given all of this trust, you repay it" (S029) "Even when I'm hiring somebody and I have a very strong feeling that they're gilding their resume, I don't hold that against them." (S074) Sources: 4 (S020, S029, S030, S074). Confidence: high.

27. **Keep previewing; a film that wants a wide audience needs to know where it stands.** "for a film that wants and needs to be accepted by a large popular audience, it's a good idea to see where you are in that process" (S099) Sources: 3 (S099, S101, S013). Confidence: high.

28. **Leave six weeks between films and make no big decisions inside them.** "it's very dangerous to make big decisions during that six week period, because you're standards of analysis are compromised" (S103) "If it's too short, less than six weeks in my case, you inadvertently infect the next film with the rules of the previous one" (S076) Sources: 3 (S029, S076, S103). Confidence: high.
