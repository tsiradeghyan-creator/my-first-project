# Frameworks: Walter Murch
Built from 11 verbatim web excerpts (S001 to S011). Quick-web depth.

1. **Rule of Six** *(his name)*. Emotion, story, rhythm, eye-trace, the 2D plane, 3D space, in that order of priority. "don't ever give up emotion before story" (S001). Score a cut by the highest item it serves and the highest it breaks (guidance). Sources: S001, S002.
2. **The blink** *(unnamed)*. Cut where the thought ends, a frame or two before the actor blinks. "Just before the blink." (S003). Sources: S003, S004.
3. **Shortest working length** *(unnamed)*. "How short can the film be and still work?" (S006). Sources: S006.
4. **Fresh eye** *(unnamed)*. Stay off the set so the footage is judged as the audience will see it. "it burdens the mind unnecessarily." (S010). Sources: S010.
5. **Random access trap** *(unnamed)*. Search tools give what you ask for, not what you need. "sometimes you don't really know what you want," (S009). Sources: S008, S009.
6. **Worldizing** *(his term, secondary source)*. Re-record sound in real space for "any kind of reverberation or atmosphere around a voice or a sound effect" (S011). Sources: S011.
