# Books: Bela Bajaria

Built from 81 public sources (2012 to 2026).

She has written no books. Her thinking lives in spoken keynotes and interviews. The major talks that carry it:

- **APOS keynote with Vivek Couto (April 2021):** "massive local impact", empowered local decision makers, exporting local stories instead of Hollywood's. (S008, S027)
- **TCA virtual press tour (August 2021):** the one-inch barrier of subtitles is falling; Lupin as proof that local creators win. (S060)
- **Banff World Media Festival keynote (June 2022):** back to basics in a noisy year, the underdog is fine, culturally specific is universally attractive. (S004, S076)
- **UCLA Entertainment Symposium with Ken Ziffren (June 2023):** "tiny myth busters": algorithms do not decide, binge beats weekly, local shows on a global platform. (S006)
- **Fortune Most Powerful Women Summit with Michal Lev-Ram (October 2023):** creator first, 27 local offices, gut over algorithm, storytelling plus discovery. Her fullest transcript in the corpus. (S047, S075)
- **Next on Netflix press days (2024, 2025 Korea):** breadth of tastes, no coastal bubble, Korean content beyond language. (S013, S018, S057)
- **First International Showcase (November 2024):** "great stories can come from anywhere", home country first, best version of every genre, 70 percent subs or dubs. (S062, S064, S067)
- **MIPCOM Variety Vanguard conversation with Cynthia Littleton (October 2026, her last Netflix keynote):** deeply local first, "I don't want it global", greenlight authority in each country, financial discipline. (S001, S002, S009)
