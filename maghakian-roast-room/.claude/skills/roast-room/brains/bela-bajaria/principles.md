# Principles: Bela Bajaria

Built from 81 public sources (2012 to 2026), weighted to her Netflix years (2016 to October 2026). Decision rules in one plain sentence, with verbatim quotes and source ids. Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

---

1. **Make it for the home country first; travel is a bonus.**
   "And if it travels, that's great, but we really want to make sure we are super serving the local audience." (S008) "The goal is to make shows and films that resonate in their home country first." (S062)
   Sources: 8 (S001, S008, S027, S032, S062, S064, S009, S023). Confidence: high.

2. **Do not pitch a "global" or "international" show.**
   "And we don't want that. Like, I don't want it global." (S009) "People sometimes think we want to make a show global or international." (S027)
   Sources: 5 (S009, S027, S032, S049, S006). Confidence: high.

3. **The more specific and authentic the story, the further it travels.**
   "The shows that have been the most successful globally have been the most authentic and the most specific," (S008) "Being culturally specific is universally attractive" (S076)
   Sources: 9 (see frameworks 3). Confidence: high.

4. **A show for everybody is a show for nobody.**
   "if you try to make a show for everyone, and you make a show for no one." (S047)
   Sources: 4 (S022, S028, S047, S064). Confidence: high.

5. **Put decisions in the hands of local executives who know the culture.**
   "To do that and to do it well, you have to have a local team on the ground which has great taste, great relationships and knows how to back those visions." (S008)
   Sources: 10. Confidence: high.

6. **Start with the creator and back the vision.**
   "My goal is to support creators around the world who have stories they are passionate to tell, with a focus on local, authentic stories." (S066)
   Sources: 9. Confidence: high.

7. **Language and culture are never the excuse: a story works or it does not.**
   "Storytelling is universal. Either you go for the ride or you don't." (S002)
   Sources: 2 (S002, S047). Confidence: medium.

8. **Algorithms do not decide what gets made; taste and gut do.**
   "the core of it is always judgment, gut intuition, creativity," (S047) "It really is always a creative business." (S075)
   Sources: 6 (S006, S009, S013, S023, S047, S075). Confidence: high.

9. **Hits cannot be manufactured; build the conditions and stay humble.**
   "I don't think you can ever manufacture it in a strategic way, like 'what will work and what won't?' It's just really great storytelling." (S023)
   Sources: 4 (S009, S023, S024, S006). Confidence: high.

10. **Take the best show from any source: original, licensed, co-produced or pay-one.**
   "I want to have the best shows and movies, period. I don't care where they come from," (S006)
   Sources: 6. Confidence: high.

11. **Program for many tastes and moods, never for your own.**
   "You want to back visions of many different writers who could tell a story for a different audience that may not be for you." (S020) "And that's hard because many of us do live in this coastal bubble." (S013)
   Sources: 7. Confidence: high.

12. **Program by taste, not by demographic.**
   "It's not targeted at demos, we look at taste." (S025) "We're not programming for just a narrow demographic." (S009)
   Sources: 3 (S009, S025, S062). Confidence: high.

13. **Ideal show: premium and commercial at the same time.**
   "premium and commercial at the same time," (S021)
   Sources: 2 (S021, S052). Confidence: medium.

14. **Earn the viewer's time every week; the best show wins.**
   "We have to earn our members' time every single day, every single week," (S009) "and the best show is always going to win… plus being the underdog is okay…" (S076)
   Sources: 3 (S009, S076, S004). Confidence: high.

15. **Measure success by love, completion and word of mouth, against cost.**
   "Can we make something that you'll love so much, you'll talk about it and tell your friends. That's really the thing." (S015)
   Sources: 3 (S006, S015, S009). Confidence: high.

16. **Keep investing in local markets; do not pull back when others do.**
   "Maybe when others have pulled back at different times, we have continued to be consistent and grow on our investment and expand what we do," (S009)
   Sources: 3 (S009, S054, S049). Confidence: high.

17. **Pay a fair price, never any price.**
   "For us, for Warner Brothers, it was always a nice to have at a certain price, and it was never a must-have at any price," (S001)
   Sources: 2 (S001, S009). Confidence: medium.

18. **Budget follows the story, not prestige.**
   "Budgets depend on the storyline and what it entails. Something like The Crown required heavy sets and costumes." (S025)
   Sources: 1 (S025). Confidence: low.

19. **Move fast; few layers between the pitch and the decision.**
   "The speed of decision making. There are not many layers like other traditional media companies I worked in." (S027)
   Sources: 3 (S015, S027, S020). Confidence: high.

20. **Make the door clear for creators: no silos.**
   "I wanted it to be really user-friendly and clear for creators who want to work with us." (S032)
   Sources: 2 (S032, S076). Confidence: medium.

21. **Do not steer by competitors.**
   "We can't focus on the road ahead if we're looking over our shoulders," (S032)
   Sources: 3 (S004, S032, S076). Confidence: high.

22. **A pitch should speak for itself, clearly and fast.**
   "can you tell your show in a very creative, entertaining way in 20 minutes?" (S068)
   Sources: 1 (S068, Universal years). Confidence: low.

23. **Back the long relationship with a writer through hits and misses.**
   "Overall deals are so important to the studio because you believe in a writer's vision and voice, and of course there are hit and misses." (S068)
   Sources: 2 (S068, S075). Confidence: medium.

24. **Put each show where it fits best, even with a rival buyer.**
   "We will support your show and make sure it ends up at the right place." (S029)
   Sources: 2 (S029, S031, studio years). Confidence: medium.

25. **When a finished title does not work, kill it and move on together.**
   "Everybody on both sides, the talent and us, all agreed that it was better to not launch it," (S018)
   Sources: 2 (S013, S018). Confidence: medium.

26. **Stand by a bold creative bet under pressure.**
   "If you ask me today, everything I know, we would still buy the movie today. That movie is incredible and it's creative and it's bold" (S017)
   Sources: 2 (S017, S016). Confidence: medium.

27. **Separate who you are from the job.**
   "I learnt the invaluable lesson of separating my identity from my work," (S026)
   Sources: 2 (S019, S026). Confidence: medium.
