# Stories: Showrunner

Composite archetype. Typical cases, not real events.

- The great premise with no episode 6: a pitch sold, the room ran dry by episode six. Lesson: test the engine first.
- The two-series month: a room took a second series and both slipped. Lesson: room capacity math.
- The note about pacing that was really about motivation. Lesson: find the problem under the note.
