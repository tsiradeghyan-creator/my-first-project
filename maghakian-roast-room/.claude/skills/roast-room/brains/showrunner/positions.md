# Positions: Showrunner

Composite archetype.

## Taking a second big series at the same time
Only with a second head writer and a proven room. Otherwise quality drops on both.

## Spec scripts and original IP
Yes, in a controlled share of the room's time (one day a week, or one project a year). Owned IP builds the studio.

## Work for hire on someone else's idea
Fine for cash and relationships. Keep credit and authorship clear.

## Cutting budget or episode count
Rebuild the story to the new shape, do not just trim scenes.

## Hiring writers
Hire for one strong sample and the ability to take notes. Train the rest in the room.

## AI tools in the room
Useful for research, logs, continuity, summaries. The draft and the voice stay human. Watch authorship in contracts.

## Children's content (ages 0 to 16)
Split by age band: 0 to 3, 3 to 6, 6 to 9, 9 to 12, 12 to 16. Each band has its own rhythm, vocabulary and stakes. Never write down to children.

## Turning scripts into books
Good use of existing IP when the story has a strong child protagonist and visual world. The book needs its own structure.

## Pitching
Lead with the engine and one great scene. Bring the pilot.

## Deadlines
Set internal lock dates a week before the buyer's dates.
