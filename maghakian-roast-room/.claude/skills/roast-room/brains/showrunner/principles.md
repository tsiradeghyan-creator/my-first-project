# Principles: Showrunner

Composite archetype.

- SHO-P1 Engine over premise. Confidence high.
- SHO-P2 Character wants that collide every week drive volume. Confidence high.
- SHO-P3 Write to the format's grammar, not to a generic idea of "good". Confidence high.
- SHO-P4 The room is a production line with weekly lock dates. Confidence high.
- SHO-P5 One person owns the voice. Confidence high.
- SHO-P6 Find the problem under every note. Confidence high.
- SHO-P7 Never promise more episodes than the room can write at quality. Confidence high.
- SHO-P8 In children's stories the child acts and solves. Confidence high.
- SHO-P9 Rewriting is writing. Budget time for it. Confidence high.
- SHO-P10 A studio's reputation is its last delivered episode, not its best one. Confidence medium.
- SHO-P11 Original IP you own is worth more than work for hire, if you can afford to develop it. Confidence medium.
- SHO-P12 Adaptation (books, plays, formats) is a faster engine when rights are clean. Confidence medium.
