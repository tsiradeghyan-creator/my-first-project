# Books: Hagai Levi

Built from the extract notes on 32 sources (2009 to 2026). Hagai Levi has written no books. His body of work is television, so this file lists the series that carry his thinking, what each one teaches about selling a story beyond its home market, and which sources discuss it. It describes the works in plain words; it does not reproduce scripts.

## Short Stories About Love (1996, Keshet)
- **What it is:** His first television work, an anthology he created, co-produced and co-directed. Won the Israeli Academy Award for best drama twice (S005, S006).
- **Lesson for the seat:** He came to TV from film because he lacked the tools for features and saw TV as practice (S045).
- **Sources:** S005, S006, S045

## Love Around the Corner (2002, 270 episodes)
- **What it is:** A daily drama he co-created and produced. He disliked the genre but learned the power of a daily slot (S002, S003).
- **Lesson for the seat:** The strip schedule that soaps own can carry quality drama. This is the seed of BeTipul's format.
- **Sources:** S002, S003, S005

## BeTipul (2005 to 2008, HOT) and In Treatment (HBO, 2008 to 2010, 2021)
- **Core idea:** A therapist sees one patient per weekday, then his own therapist on the fifth day. Each episode is one continuous half-hour session in one room (S010, S038).
- **Business path:** Two pilots and about two years to get HOT to commission it (S002, S003). Days after airing, calls came from abroad; HBO, FX and USA competed and HBO bought the remake rights (S002, S003). An Israeli actress in Los Angeles carried a subtitled disc to her manager, then to Ari Emanuel, then to HBO (S040). The US version aired a year after the Israeli one (S002). Episodes cost about five times less than an average US series (S040).
- **Adaptation:** US season one nearly word for word, season two culturally translated with a local team (S002, S003, S008). Season three was the first US season written from scratch (S009, S039). Versions in about 19 to 20 countries by 2021, Levi executive producer or consultant on most (S023, S027, S038, S045). HBO Central Europe bought rights for six countries from Dori Media Group in 2010 (S038).
- **Lesson for the seat:** The template for a small-market format sale: cheap, bare, archetypal, with one structural device, and a creator who travels with it.
- **Sources:** S002, S003, S004, S008, S009, S010, S023, S027, S038, S039, S040, S045

## The Accursed (2013, HOT)
- **What it is:** A five-hour Israeli miniseries he created and directed; he called it avant-garde and experimental and said it used up his TV credit (S004, S005).
- **Sources:** S004, S005

## The Affair (Showtime, 2014 to 2019, co-created with Sarah Treem)
- **Core idea:** One affair told from two points of view, inspired by Rashomon (S004). Golden Globe for best drama (S005).
- **Lesson for the seat:** He left when the show moved from art toward a commercial machine; Treem, his American partner, took over (S004, S023). A US partner is a "safety net" for a foreign creator (S023).
- **Sources:** S001, S004, S005, S023, S028

## Our Boys (HBO and Keshet, 2019)
- **Core idea:** Ten episodes on the 2014 murder of a Palestinian teenager, co-created with Joseph Cedar and Tawfik Abu Wael; the first HBO original series in Hebrew and Arabic (S020).
- **Lesson for the seat:** A local, painful true story can travel on a premium platform when it is told as a universal question (hate crime) and with a co-producer from home (Keshet) (S014, S019, S020).
- **Sources:** S014, S016, S019, S020, S022, S035

## Scenes from a Marriage (HBO, 2021)
- **Core idea:** A remake of Bergman's 1973 series, five episodes, mostly one house and real time, gender roles swapped (S023, S024, S025).
- **Lesson for the seat:** With Bergman he was on the other side of the deal, the adapter, not the seller (S028). Keep the structure, find a reason that makes it definitive again, work with a local co-writer (Amy Herzog), and send scripts and cuts to the rights holder (S024, S025, S027, S028).
- **Sources:** S001, S023, S024, S025, S027, S028, S045

## Etty (ARTE and European co-producers, 2025)
- **Core idea:** Six episodes from the diaries of Etty Hillesum, set in an unplaced, modern-looking Amsterdam; started as a film and grew (S030, S034, S041).
- **Lesson for the seat:** European co-production money (France, Germany, Netherlands), a public broadcaster (ARTE) and a Venice premiere as the route for arthouse television (S030, S032, S034).
- **Sources:** S030, S032, S033, S034, S041, S043
