# Principles: Hagai Levi

Built from the extract notes on 32 sources (2009 to 2026). 20 decision rules, most-used first, weighted toward the calls a small studio makes when it wants a foreign buyer: what to sell, how to price the risk, how to protect the work in a remake. "Sources" counts distinct sources. Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **Make the small personal thing first; travel is a result, not the brief.** "Everything that happened with In Treatment was never my intention." (S002) "It was born as a show for friends... really." (S004) Sources: 4 (S002, S004, S008, S030). Confidence: high.

2. **Build cheapness into the concept, so the price itself is a selling point.** "My intention was always to make it on the cheap...two actors, one location." (S008) "It's also cheap, which helps." (S003) Sources: 5 (S002, S003, S004, S008, S040). Confidence: high.

3. **In a small market, drama only pays through the overseas business.** "You can live very well in Israel off of reality and game shows, but not drama." (S008) "If it wasn't for the overseas business, I would have lost money." (S008) Sources: 2 (S008, S038). Confidence: medium.

4. **Use archetypes, not local types, if you want the story to cross borders.** "It was very important for me to show archetypical stories that represent something that everybody can identify with" (S002) Sources: 3 (S002, S023, S040). Confidence: high.

5. **Translate the problem, not the lines; a near-literal remake is a first draft.** "After the first season we thought it was too close, and that the adaptations should be culturally translated." (S002) "We all felt that the second season requires a much deeper adaptation to make it more culturally relevant" (S008) Sources: 4 (S002, S003, S008, S040). Confidence: high.

6. **Abroad, always attach a local writer.** "When people don't do that, the output is very generic because you're not rooted in that culture." (S027) Sources: 3 (S023, S025, S027). Confidence: high.

7. **Find one structural device that makes the format unlike anything else.** "When I realised during planning for the original series that he would go to his therapist on a Friday I knew I had something." (S003) Sources: 3 (S003, S023, S025). Confidence: high.

8. **Prove a hard concept with a pilot when buyers doubt it.** "I produced two pilots and I think when people watched it they were convinced that it could work, but it took me around two years to start production" (S002) Sources: 2 (S002, S003). Confidence: medium.

9. **Keep the intimate form; refuse notes to "open it up".** "When I first came to America everyone told me they wouldn't let me leave it like it is, that we'd have to open it up and that it couldn't stay in the room." (S003) "Everyone said there was no chance they would leave 'In Treatment' the way it was." (S004) Sources: 3 (S003, S004, S025). Confidence: high.

10. **Choose buyers who give freedom over buyers who pay for volume.** "I wouldn't imagine myself working for a network like CBS or NBC, making it more commercial and telling you what to do." (S003) "I did this one with HBO, which I feel so at home with and [with whom] I have the most creative freedom." (S025) Sources: 4 (S003, S004, S025, S030). Confidence: high.

11. **Know whether a job is for money or for art, and leave when it drifts.** "if the answer to the question 'Why am I doing is this?' is foggy, I'm left in the position of frustration and confusion" (S004) Sources: 2 (S004, S030). Confidence: medium.

12. **Protect the writing by holding the other jobs.** He directs and produces to protect his writing from reinterpretation (the interviewer's summary, S001). "I started as a a writer. I came from a world of words with an image in it" (S001) "And the fact that I directed it myself and wrote it myself." (S025) Sources: 2 (S001, S025). Confidence: medium.

13. **Do not remake without a reason that makes it definitive again.** "I had to have an answer for why I should do it and why I was the one to do it." (S024) "So I felt that if you do it today, you have to make it definitive again." (S028) Sources: 2 (S024, S028). Confidence: medium.

14. **The audience must be able to identify with someone; if you cannot, flip the character.** "I cannot put on a screen a character that [audiences] cannot identify with." (S025) "I couldn't live with Johan. I couldn't identify with him." (S023) Sources: 3 (S001, S023, S025). Confidence: high.

15. **Fewer hours; no season without unfinished characters.** "Every time, I wanted to do less." (S024) "I want to feel that I'm not done with a character at the end of a season, so I have to write a new one." (S001) Sources: 3 (S001, S002, S024). Confidence: high.

16. **Do not try to preserve success; do not repeat a form you are tired of.** "I'm not trying to preserve success." (S004) "I didn't feel interested in doing it again and again in the same way." (S002) Sources: 2 (S002, S004). Confidence: medium.

17. **A moral dilemma sits at the heart of every project.** "The main thing that would interest me is some kind of moral dilemma at the heart of everything." (S001) Sources: 3 (S001, S030, S035). Confidence: high.

18. **For real events, truth beats balance, and never use pain as entertainment.** "It wasn't about being balanced, it was about being true to what happened" (S019) "It was very, very important and crucial for us... not to use the conflict for entertainment" (S016) Sources: 4 (S014, S016, S019, S035). Confidence: high.

19. **Old stories need a new language to be sold again.** "I believe one cannot tell another Holocaust story, nowadays, without charging it with universal, contemporary relevance... and telling it in a new language." (S034) "You cannot just do it the same. It's been done" (S041) Sources: 4 (S030, S034, S041, S043). Confidence: high.

20. **A creator who sold a format should still guard it.** On a weak Dutch version: "While it's true that we were the ones who sold them the format, I do still care." (S040) He served as executive producer and consultant on most versions (S027, S038). Sources: 3 (S027, S038, S040). Confidence: high.
