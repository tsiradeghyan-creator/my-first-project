# Stories: Platform Buyer

Composite archetype. Typical cases, not real events.

- The beautiful bible sent to the wrong desk: great writing, wrong slot, no reply for six months. Lesson: map the buyer first.
- The daily drama that sold on reliability: a studio won the slot because it could deliver five episodes a week without slipping. Lesson: volume is a feature.
- The all-rights deal that blocked a remake: a small fee for worldwide rights stopped a later format sale. Lesson: rights are the real price.
- Three buyers in one week: parallel pitching turned a single low offer into a better one. Lesson: three buyers make a market.
