# Principles: Platform Buyer

Composite archetype. Rules of thumb from TV and streaming sales. Confidence reflects how widely the practice holds, not a source count.

- BUY-P1 Slot before story. Know the hole in the schedule you fill. Confidence high.
- BUY-P2 Price to the buyer's revenue, not your cost. If the slot earns them little, they cannot pay much, however good the script. Confidence high.
- BUY-P3 Volume and reliability are selling points. A studio that has delivered 2,300+ episodes on time is lower risk than a brilliant newcomer. Confidence high.
- BUY-P4 Find the need first. Ask what they are short of this year before pitching. Confidence high.
- BUY-P5 Rights are the real price. Read territory, term, exclusivity and format clauses before the fee. Confidence high.
- BUY-P6 Short packages win first meetings: logline, one-pager, pilot. Bibles come after interest. Confidence high.
- BUY-P7 Never depend on one buyer. Design projects at least three platforms could buy. Confidence high.
- BUY-P8 Make the buyer look safe to their boss. Confidence high.
- BUY-P9 A "maybe" with no date is a "no". Ask for the next step and the date in the meeting. Confidence medium.
- BUY-P10 Language widens or narrows the market. Armenian-only limits buyers; dubbing, subtitling or a Russian-language version can open more, at a cost. Confidence medium.
- BUY-P11 Library value is real. Old episodes sell again as catalogue if you kept the rights. Confidence medium.
- BUY-P12 Payment terms matter as much as price. Late payment by a platform is a cash risk for a small studio. Confidence medium.
