# Frameworks: Tigran (line producer, Maghakian Scripts)

Built from S001 (his own interview, 2026-11-03) and S002 to S007 (repo records of his decisions, paraphrase only). None of these has a name he gave it; all are marked *(unnamed)*. Scoring lines are guidance drawn from his numbers, not his words.

## 1. First read of a script *(unnamed)*
- **What:** the first pass a line producer makes on a new script, before any talk of quality.
- **Steps:** (1) how many shooting days are planned; (2) the cost drivers: props, cars, houses and what the script demands; (3) the rough average cost per day; (4) whether the money offered covers what is written.
- **When:** every new script, every new episode block, every rewrite that adds sets or cars.
- **Score with it:** a plan that cannot state days and cost per day is not ready to price. If the money does not cover the script, the answer is a cut list, not a yes.
- **Quote:** "ռեկվիզիտները, մեքենաներ, տներ, ինչ պահանջները, հասկանա մոտավոր միջինում օրեկան ինչ է նստելու" (S001)
- **Sources:** S001; the season and day-sheet tools he asked for carry the same counts (inferred from S003).

## 2. Comparable pricing *(unnamed)*
- **What:** judge a budget against what similar productions cost.
- **Parts:** similar houses, cars, locations, celebrities, director and writer put the price in the same range. From practice he knows that range, so he knows if this money can make this film.
- **When:** the go or no-go on a budget, before any quotes exist.
- **Score with it:** no comparable in memory means the estimate is an assumption; say so and name who must quote it.
- **Quote:** "նմանատիպ տներում, նմանատիպ մեքենաներով, նմանատիպ լոկացիաներով, նմանատիպ սելեբրիթիներով, նմանատիպ ռեժիսոր սցենարիստով այս գինը մոտավոր կնստի" (S001)
- **Sources:** S001.

## 3. Market price map *(unnamed)*
- **What:** the line producer carries the market price of every link in the chain.
- **Parts:** car hire, a taxi from point A to point B, a 40 km drive to a location, a tech truck per day, food, actors, costumes.
- **When:** any estimate; any time a department head names a number.
- **Score with it:** a line in the budget with no market price behind it is a risk line.
- **Quote:** "ծախսերը ամեն ինչը ինքը գները շուկայական պետք է իմանա" (S001)
- **Sources:** S001; his price list sheet in project.xlsx holds the numbers he has given so far, the rest blank (inferred from S003).

## 4. Relationship pricing *(unnamed)*
- **What:** every item has a market price, and skill, relationships, friends and your circle bring it lower.
- **When:** locations, cars, actors' fees: anything negotiated.
- **Score with it:** the same house can be $100 or $1000. A plan that pays list price everywhere is a weak plan.
- **Quote:** "քո ծանոթներին, ընկերներին, մտերիմներին, քո շրջապատը օգտագործելով կարող ես ավելի մատչելի և ավելի հարմար գներ առաջարկել մարդկանց" (S001)
- **Sources:** S001; at Gate 1 the key condition, two foreign-language actors for 3 to 4 days, was met from his own circle (inferred from S005).

## 5. The 1 percent question *(unnamed)*
- **What:** a contact may have thousands of houses and contacts in their base. You need one percent of it. Ask exactly that.
- **When:** working a fixer, a location scout, a transport contact.
- **Quote:** "քեզ պետք է իր ինֆորմացիայի 1%-ը" (S001)
- **Sources:** S001.

## 6. Daily cost report *(unnamed)*
- **What:** every department reports every spend to the line producer, every day.
- **Parts in the studio's tools:** costs per day, week, month and whole project; receipts logged as they come (inferred from S003).
- **When:** every shooting day.
- **Score with it:** a day with no report is a day the money is not under control.
- **Quote:** "ամեն օր պետք է իրեն զեկուցեն բոլոր ծախսերի մասին" (S001)
- **Sources:** S001, S003.

## 7. Early warning on hidden spending *(unnamed)*
- **What:** on set you see spending happen, and you are told it costs nothing. You know it will come back as a bill at the end, and you will face the fact. React at once.
- **When:** any spend on set that is not in the plan.
- **Quote:** "դա վերջում որպես ծախս պալուբոմ ներկայացնելու են, եւ քեզ փաստի առաջ կանգնացնեն" (S001)
- **Sources:** S001.

## 8. Second director's time plan *(unnamed)*
- **What:** the second director owns time: the call, the moves, the papers ready per breakdown, with texts and hours.
- **Parts:** for a field location, six cars from A to B within the hour, where they park, unload, eat, maybe tents, clothing; everything thought through.
- **When:** every day's plan; every location move.
- **Quote:** "զբորը ճիշտ ժամանակին լինի, ճիշտ ժամանակին շարժվեն, ճիշտ ժամանակին դակումենտները, թղթերը պատրաստ լինեն ըստ ռազմբիվքի" (S001)
- **Sources:** S001; the day sheets he asked for count working days per person and drop the actors' scene lists (inferred from S003).

## 9. The not-today test for the director *(unnamed)*
- **What:** when the director's wishes go past what was planned, discussed and set as the producing task, or today has no budget for it, the answer is no for today.
- **When:** any new ask on set.
- **Score with it:** inside the agreed plan, price it; outside it, refuse it for today and move it to a planned day if it is worth it.
- **Quote:** "այսօր չեն կարող այդ ծախսն անենք այս ինչ բանի համար" (S001)
- **Sources:** S001.

## 10. Ask first, then make only what is needed *(unnamed)*
- **What:** before producing documents, ask who they are for, for what, and for which period; then make only those. Never dump every file.
- **When:** any report, pack or paperwork request.
- **Sources:** inferred from S003 (his rule for the documents menu, 2026-11-03), S007 (folders must say who they are for).

## 11. Studio season format *(unnamed)*
- **What:** the studio's own planning sheet: one sheet per episode, columns for episode and scene, location, mode, actors, pages, props, story day and flashback mark; green row = shot. The director or admin copies scenes into a day sheet in the same columns.
- **When:** planning any series block.
- **Sources:** inferred from S003, S004. Interior and exterior are marked, with exterior evening and night scenes flagged for light and cost (inferred from S003).
