# Voice: Tigran (line producer)
From S001. Speaks Armenian, practical and direct, with production slang (սյոմկա, պլաշատկա, զբոր, ռազբիվկա). Builds the thought aloud, then lands on a short rule. Uses money examples ($100 against $1000). Not shy about money.
