# Principles: Tigran (line producer, Maghakian Scripts)

Built from S001 (his own words, 2026-11-03) and S002 to S007 (repo records, paraphrase only). Confidence counts sources: with one interview, most rules rest on S001 alone. They are still high-trust as his method, because they are his own words; they are low on breadth.

1. **Count the shooting days before anything else.** "քանի սյոմշնի օր են պլանավորել նկարեն" (S001). Sources: 1. Confidence: low breadth, his own rule.
2. **The money must fit what is written.** If the offer does not cover the script, the script or the offer changes. "կբավարարի այս նկարագրություններին ու այս ամեն ինչին" (S001). Sources: 1.
3. **Price by comparison.** Similar houses, cars, stars and team mean a similar price (S001). Sources: 1.
4. **Getting the low price is the job.** "հենց այդ է գծային պրոդյուսերի լավ լինելը" (S001). Sources: 1.
5. **Know the market price of every link.** "ամեն ինչ ունի իր շուկայական գինը" (S001). Sources: 1.
6. **Every spend must be reasonable.** "ցանկացած գնում, ցանկացած գումարի ծախս պետք է լինի խելամիտ" (S001). Sources: 1.
7. **Use your circle to lower prices.** Friends, relationships and personal skill make houses, cars and actors cheaper (S001). His Gate 1 condition was met from his own circle (inferred from S005). Sources: 2. Confidence: medium.
8. **Ask contacts for exactly what you need.** "շատ կարևոր է հարցնել ինչ ինֆորմացիա է տիրապետում" (S001). Sources: 1.
9. **Be easy to talk money with.** "դու պետք է լինես այն մարդ, ում հետ չեն ամաչում փողից խոսեն" (S001). Sources: 1.
10. **Every spend is reported to you, every day.** "ամեն օր պետք է իրեն զեկուցեն բոլոր ծախսերի մասին" (S001). He wants costs per day, week, month and project, with receipts (inferred from S003). Sources: 2. Confidence: medium.
11. **React early to on-set spending that people call free.** "պետք է շատ շուտ արձագանք" (S001). Sources: 1.
12. **Big props for one effect need thought and coordination.** "քիչ մտածելով մեծ ռեկվիզիտներ են վարձակալում, այդ պետք է խելամիտ անեն" (S001). This is a spending rule; he said it is not the costliest mistake (S001, part 3). Sources: 1.
13. **The whole team works as one system, or overspend happens.** "Համակարգված պետք է աշխատեն բոլորը միասին" (S001). Sources: 1.
14. **Say no to the director when an ask goes past the agreed plan or today's budget.** "այսօր նախատեսված չի նմանատիպ ծախս" (S001). Sources: 1.
15. **The second director owns time.** "թայմ մենեջմենթը իր պրոցեսն է" (S001). Sources: 1.
16. **There is no single costliest mistake; the irreplaceable losses are people and key equipment.** "ամենաթանկ սխալ հասկացողություն չկա" (S001). Sources: 1. A risky scene needs a safety plan before it is priced (inferred from S001).
17. **Only days count on a day sheet.** Who is on set and which working day it is for them; not each actor's scene list (inferred from S003). Sources: 1. Confidence: low.
18. **Ask who it is for before making documents, and make only those.** (inferred from S003, S007). Sources: 2. Confidence: medium.
19. **Use the tools on the live series before building new ones.** He asked for one door instead of more tools; the agreed advice was 3 to 4 weeks on the current series first (inferred from S006). Sources: 1. Confidence: low.
20. **The board advises; he decides.** He follows a board's verdict when its condition is met, as at Gate 1 (inferred from S005), and he ruled that councils run the tested Roast Room protocol unchanged (inferred from S005, S006). Sources: 2. Confidence: medium.
21. **Quality bar is real.** He rejects work that looks bad or falls far below what he needs, even when the information is good (inferred from S007). Sources: 1. Confidence: low.
