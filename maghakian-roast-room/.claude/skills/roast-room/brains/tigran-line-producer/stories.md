# Stories: Tigran (line producer, Maghakian Scripts)

Built from S001 (his own words) and S002 to S007 (repo records, paraphrase only). Short list: he has told few stories so far.

1. **The same house for $100 or $1000.** One line producer rents a house for $1000; another rents the same house for $100. Lesson: the price is the line producer's skill and relationships. "նույն տունը կարող է վարձել, այո, հավատա, $100" (S001)
2. **The contact with a thousand houses.** A contact may have thousands of houses and acquaintances in their base, but you do not ask about them. Ask for the one percent you need. "կարող է նա իր բազայում ունի հազարավոր տներ, հազարավոր ծանոթներ, բայց դու դրանց մասին չես հարցնում" (S001)
3. **Six cars to a field.** Six cars go from A to B, arrive within the hour, park, unload, eat, maybe tents; the second director must think of all of it. Lesson: time management is a process someone owns. "եթե 6 մեքենա պետք են ակետից բեկետը գնան, մի ժամ հասնեն" (S001)
4. **It costs nothing, they say.** On set someone spends, and you are told it is not a cost. At the end it comes as a bill and you face the fact. Lesson: react early. "քեզ ասում են, որ դա չի ծախսում" (S001)
5. **The correction on the costliest mistake.** An earlier summary said his costliest mistake was renting big props for one effect. He corrected it the same day: there is no such single thing; the true losses are people and the most expensive car. Lesson: he checks what is written about him and fixes it. "Ամենաթանկ սխալը կարա շատ-շատ բաներ լինի" (S001)
6. **Gate 1 met from his own circle.** The board's key condition for «Թարգմանչուհին» was two foreign-language actors for 3 to 4 days; he confirmed he has them among people he knows, and chose the board's pick (inferred from S005).
7. **The season sheet he brought.** He sent the real season breakdown in the studio's own format (one sheet per episode, green rows shot) and two day breakdowns, so the tools would fit how his crews already work (inferred from S003).
8. **Info good, look bad.** He judged the first script pack: content right, design wrong; folders must say who they are for and the whole set must carry the brand. The pack was rebuilt (inferred from S007).
