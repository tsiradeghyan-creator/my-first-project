# Books: Jason Blum

Built from 97 public sources, 2013 to 2026. He has written no business book. His published work is editorial and opinion:

- **The Blumhouse Book of Nightmares: The Haunted City (2015, editor).** An anthology of original scary stories by filmmakers (Eli Roth, Ethan Hawke, James DeMonaco and others), each tied to a city. Contributors kept the film and TV rights so they would write free of budget limits. He frames it as a way to build a community around scary stories. Taught in S023, S058, S066.
- **New York Times op-ed (2022).** Argues for a "scrappier" streaming model: little money up front, a real stake in success, and an end to paying everyone as if every project were already a hit. Excerpted in S077; referenced in S018 and S062.
- **Vassar commencement address, "Lessons You Need to Survive and Thrive in a Horror Movie" (2020).** Six lessons: be true to who you are; friendship; sometimes jump scares are just jump scares; don't celebrate too early; speak your mind; capitalize on luck. Reported in S089, S090, S099.
- **Horror's New Wave (2025).** A coffee-table book surveying the Blumhouse library, mentioned in S005; no content in the corpus.
