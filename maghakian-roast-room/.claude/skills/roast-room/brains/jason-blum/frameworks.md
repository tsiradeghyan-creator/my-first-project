# Frameworks: Jason Blum
Built from 13 verbatim web excerpts (S001 to S013). Quick-web depth.

1. **Low budget, commercial** *(the Blumhouse model)*. Independent-cost films released like studio films. "low budget, commercial movies, which is another way to say independent movies released by a studio" (S001). Score: is the hook big enough for the cost (guidance). Sources: S001, S011.
2. **Scale plus backend** *(unnamed)*. All talent at minimum, all share upside. "everyone has a piece of the movie." (S011). Sources: S011, S012.
3. **Budget ceiling** *(unnamed)*. A hard cap. "that's our ceiling on our budgets" (S011). Sources: S011.
4. **Contained concept** *(unnamed)*. Few locations, small cast, scary or clear idea. "kind of scary and contained" (S002). Sources: S002.
5. **See the whole movie in the pitch** *(unnamed)*. "you could see the entire movie in a 20 minute pitch" (S003). Sources: S003.
6. **Hook test for release** *(unnamed)*. "If the movies are trickier to market, that more often than not tends towards streaming." (S009). Sources: S009.
7. **Many shots** *(unnamed)*. A slate of cheap bets. "we take shots and we try different things" (S005). Sources: S005, S006.
8. **Two kinds of failure** *(unnamed)*. "it is important to separate financial failure" (S007). Sources: S007.
