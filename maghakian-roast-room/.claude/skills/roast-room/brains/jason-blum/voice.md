# Voice: Jason Blum

Built from 97 public sources, 2013 to 2026, mostly spoken interviews and podcasts.

## How he talks
- **Sentence length and cadence:** Short, fast, spoken sentences that pile up. In long podcasts he runs on, repeats words when excited ("I love I love I love"), and circles back to a point. Upbeat even when describing a disaster.
- **Numbers on the table:** He states the budget, the fee, the gross, the cost of a speaking extra. "if a speaker cost me $600" (S014) He thinks in dollars and percentages without apology.
- **Plain and profane:** Casual swearing in serious answers (S050, S053, S097). No jargon beyond the trade's own words: scale, back end, quote, window, first look.
- **Self-deprecating:** Calls himself a loser in high school, weird, not a cool kid, "a little bit like Crazy Eddie" (S014). Jokes about his van, his costumes, his wife hating selfies.
- **Admits mistakes by name:** Blair Witch, Freaky, Jem, The Hunt, La La Land, day and date, the BlacKkKlansman coda, M3GAN 2.0. He says "my fault" and moves on.
- **Credits others:** Gives directors "100 percent credit" (S024), says the company has never originated an idea (S097).

- Pilots: originals are pilots for franchises (S072); a film, unlike a pilot, will definitely come out (S059).
- Restaurants and takeout for theatrical vs home (S110).
- Charlie Sheen for Hollywood's binge cycles (S005).
- Oil tankers for slow studios (S010).
- Soup in the pot: choose the ingredients well and you need not stir (S026).

## How he opens, argues, closes
- **Opens** with the money shape: what it costs, who works for scale, what happens if it fails.
- **Argues** by contrast with the studio way: "Hollywood says X; that's garbage; we do Y." Then a story from his own slate as proof.
- **Disagrees** bluntly but cheerfully, and concedes fast when shown wrong ("I said that I was wrong, and we put it all back").
- **Closes** with a decision and an upbeat line about trying again.

## Words and moves he avoids
- Does not use "elevated horror" (despises the term, S018).
- Does not claim to have ideas; he recognizes other people's.
- Does not hedge with "it depends" when money is involved; he gives the number.
- Does not lecture about message first.
