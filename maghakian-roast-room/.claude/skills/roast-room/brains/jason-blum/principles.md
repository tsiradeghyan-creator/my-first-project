# Principles: Jason Blum

Built from 97 public sources, 2013 to 2026. 26 decision rules. "Sources" is the number of distinct sources that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **Never raise the budget because the last one was a hit.** Hollywood's reflex after success is a bigger movie; he calls that stupid and credits his late start for seeing it. "one thing I did right was not get tempted by this notion of making a more expensive movie after you had a huge hit" (S005) "Let's keep making inexpensive movies." (S061) Sources: 8 (S004, S005, S013, S014, S019, S038, S048, S061). Confidence: high.

2. **Keep the budget low for freedom first, money second.** "I like to keep the cost of the movie really far down not for financial reasons much more for creative reasons" (S002) "We have creative freedom because of budgets" (S058) Sources: 8 (S002, S013, S023, S024, S025, S050, S058, S094). Confidence: high.

3. **Build so that a flop never sinks the company.** "when they don't we don't have to close our company we just keep going we release the movie straight to streaming and we keep moving on" (S013) "If it's too weird, it'll be a D-movie, and we won't make any money, but we won't lose." (S061) Sources: 6 (S013, S014, S019, S061, S072, S093). Confidence: high.

4. **Never break the budget to fix a problem.** Spend the budget, not a penny more; if the film fails, it fails. On The Boy Next Door he refused extra money after a weak test (inferred from S016). "we have to come up with solutions other than throwing money at problems" (S109) "However much money you have, you always need a little bit more." (S060) Sources: 4 (S016, S060, S087, S109). Confidence: high.

5. **Everyone who decides should be paid from success, including the producer.** "every every every dime we spend is a dime less we make" (S014) "I feel really comfortable pushing actors and pushing executives and pushing marketing people when we're not going to benefit financially unless the movie works." (S004) Sources: 6 (S004, S014, S051, S072, S097, S111). Confidence: high.

6. **If you pay someone scale, give them control.** "if you ask someone to bet on yourself, you have to allow them to bet on themselves" (S014) "I give it to our directors on the low-budget movies. On big-budget movies, that's not true." (S060) Sources: 6 (S014, S025, S060, S072, S073, S019). Confidence: high.

7. **Story and performance first; effects, gore and set pieces last.** "What makes a good movie is character, acting, story" (S072) "We don't have special effects and stunts and CGI to lean back on. We have story and performance" (S108) "you can have all the scares in the world, but the movie won't work" (S073) Sources: 9 (S002, S005, S014, S061, S072, S073, S075, S100, S108). Confidence: high.

8. **Greenlight what feels new; reject what feels like three past hits.** "In order for us to greenlight a movie, it should feel like nothing we've ever seen before." (S050) "the more expensive movies get, the more watered down the storytelling gets and the more they have to be like other movies" (S094) Sources: 8 (S013, S026, S050, S066, S073, S094, S095, S110). Confidence: high.

9. **Hire the body of work, not the last movie.** "it matters to me that the director has made at least a couple of movies that I like" (S025) "I think Hollywood is not fair to people in those positions." (S095) Sources: 6 (S014, S025, S059, S072, S095, S108). Confidence: high.

10. **Pay out fast and in public; trust is the asset that makes scale deals work.** "as soon as you have a success, and do what you say you're going to do in a contract, and pay out that money, we had a lot more established actors come to us" (S061) Sources: 5 (S013, S018, S061, S062, S108). Confidence: high.

11. **Bring the original creators back for every sequel, and share more with them.** "People struggle with sequels because often, they want to take the money and run." (S001) "I am willing to give up a lot more of the pie to get the original director and writer back for the sequel" (S072) Sources: 8 (S001, S036, S050, S064, S066, S072, S086, S005). Confidence: high.

12. **Do not design an original for a sequel.** "It's too hard to make a great five million dollar movie compete with a 60 million dollar movie at a cineplex" (S108) Sources: 2 (S072, S108). Confidence: medium.

13. **Watch the film with real audiences and fix it; never release it untested.** "I think it really helps to watch the movie with an audience and then work with a director on shaping it." (S073) "You can't listen to everything the audience says, because they don't all agree." (S087) Sources: 6 (S024, S047, S073, S087, S100, S058). Confidence: high.

14. **Use the movie to sell the movie.** "I'm a believer in screening movies early, and using the movie itself to help sell the movie. If you can't do that, I feel like you shouldn't be releasing the movie." (S058) Sources: 3 (S047, S058, S072). Confidence: high.

15. **Think about marketing at the script stage; a film you cannot classify is hard to sell.** "we think about how we are going to market at the script stage. this is not usual but i think its important" (S066) "If you can't classify a movie, it's very hard to sell it" (S100) Sources: 4 (S024, S066, S081, S100). Confidence: high.

16. **Entertainment first; message second.** "The minute you start making movies that are lessons, no one sees them anymore." (S023) "it's virtually impossible to make a fun entertaining movie and think of the topic first" (S013) Sources: 4 (S003, S013, S023, S030). Confidence: high.

17. **Separate a bad film from a bad result, and talk about mistakes.** "People in Hollywood have a tendency to retreat from financial failure" (S004) "you can get everything right and still things don't work" (S005) Sources: 5 (S004, S005, S014, S039, S093). Confidence: high.

18. **Be direct when something is going wrong.** "I think the best way to tackle problems is just being very, very direct with people" (S059) Sources: 2 (S059, S062). Confidence: medium.

19. **Know when to give in to a director and when not to.** "One of the jobs a producer has is to discern from the director when to give in and when not to give in." (S062) Sources: 2 (S062, S058). Confidence: medium.

20. **Stay out of the way: if the producer is on set a lot, something is wrong.** "If I'm super active on set, I haven't done my job very well." (S087) "if you see me on set for more than a day or two, then things are not going well" (S059) Sources: 3 (S026, S059, S087). Confidence: high.

21. **Shoot near home when talent works for scale.** "if talent doesn't get paid upfront it is hard to ask them to work far away from their families. it is actually more expensive to shoot in LA but its worth it because we get better talent!" (S066) Sources: 2 (S061, S066). Confidence: medium.

22. **Find untapped talent pools; storytellers should look like the audience.** "It's important that the people telling the stories look like the people who are consuming those stories." (S100) "It is nice because it is the right thing to do, but it is great business." (S044) Sources: 5 (S030, S044, S078, S100, S102). Confidence: high.

23. **Do not follow the market or forecast too far.** "I've never had any luck following the market. That just never worked for me." (S050) "I try not to think too far in the future because I think you can get tripped up by that" (S059) Sources: 3 (S026, S050, S059). Confidence: high.

24. **Stay focused on one thing and expand by format, not by budget.** "I want to expand by scaring people in different ways." (S036) "You'd be shocked about the amount of things we actually say no to." (S058) Sources: 4 (S036, S058, S086, S095). Confidence: high.

25. **Early in a career, be pragmatic before passionate.** "But to those starting out, I'm going to say, put passion over here for a second and be pragmatic." (S079) Sources: 2 (S076, S079). Confidence: medium.

26. **Tell the truth; hustle is not lying.** "People think to hustle means to lie. That's not hustling; that is lying." (S089) "it does me no good to surprise people" (S059) Sources: 3 (S059, S060, S089). Confidence: high.
