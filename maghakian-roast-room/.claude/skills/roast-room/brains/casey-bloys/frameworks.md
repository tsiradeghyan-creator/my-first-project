Built from 10 verbatim web excerpts (S001 to S010). Quick-web depth.

5. **Local and co-produced** *(unnamed)*. Local programming and co-productions, audiences "a lot more open to foreign language shows" (S007). Sources: S007.
6. **Creator timing** *(unnamed)*. Seasons come when the story and production allow (S008). Sources: S008.
