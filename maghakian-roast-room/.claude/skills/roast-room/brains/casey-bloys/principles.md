# Principles: Casey Bloys

Built from 129 public sources (2016 to 2026). Decision rules in one plain sentence, with verbatim quotes and source ids. Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

---

1. **Start from the script in front of you, not from a plan for how many shows you need.**
   "Is it a good script? Do we think it's interesting creatively? Do we think it makes sense as a show?" (S011)
   Sources: 10 (S004, S011, S030, S052, S057, S062, S069, S111, S117, S132). Confidence: high.

2. **Bet on talent you believe in, and back them once you do.**
   "If you believe in the creators, and you believe if your vision, you back them," (S043) "we have a long history betting on our talent" (S077)
   Sources: 9 (S008, S043, S077, S103, S112, S120, S123, S006, S067). Confidence: high.

3. **Let the creator decide how long a story runs and when it ends.**
   "We always will defer to our showrunners," (S088) "It starts with the creative team. I am never the one driving it." (S051)
   Sources: 17 (see frameworks 3). Confidence: high.

4. **Never trade quality for volume, even when money and pressure push for more.**
   "we have not sacrificed quality just to get the numbers up." (S056) "We weren't just filling hours to fill hours," (S049)
   Sources: 12. Confidence: high.

5. **Protect what the brand stands for; it is the reason people pay.**
   "the HBO brand should mean something: quality, exciting, something different, but always, always, always well done and of high quality." (S056) "Kids, tween shows, food, lifestyle…was viewed as too big a stretch for the brand." (S009)
   Sources: 9 (S008, S009, S028, S038, S047, S056, S058, S062, S121). Confidence: high.

6. **Do not test, and do not let data or AI pick or write the stories.**
   "I've been at HBO for 19 years and I have never tested a pilot to decide whether to pick it up or not." (S095)
   Sources: 6 (S009, S025, S095, S100, S108, S088). Confidence: high.

7. **Make shows people will talk about, even if some of them hate it.**
   "And even people who dislike it talk about why they dislike it. For a show to arouse that kind of feeling, that's what we want." (S028)
   Sources: 7 (S025, S028, S073, S095, S103, S112, S125). Confidence: high.

8. **Ask whether a struggling show can go from good to great; if not, spend the money elsewhere.**
   "If I have limited resources, there are other things I want to do." (S030)
   Sources: 7 (S030, S032, S036, S071, S119, S141, S129). Confidence: high.

9. **Take your time in development; do not work backwards from an air date.**
   "What I'm not doing is working backwards by saying" (S132) "the team does take their time developing shows, probably a good amount more time than other places." (S009)
   Sources: 6 (S009, S043, S056, S132, S142, S029). Confidence: high.

10. **Develop many options so the one you make is the best one.**
   "You try a lot of things, try a lot of different stories," (S040)
   Sources: 6 (S004, S029, S040, S062, S069, S144). Confidence: high.

11. **Never build the slate on one franchise or one hit.**
   "I don't want to build a programming lineup that is dependent on the next Game of Thrones franchise coming," (S074)
   Sources: 6 (S011, S029, S058, S074, S112, S004). Confidence: high.

12. **Continue a franchise only if the new story is good and different.**
   "We're going to continue that franchise only if it's good," (S074) "we're not trying to replicate the same show." (S132)
   Sources: 6 (S003, S052, S074, S095, S101, S132). Confidence: high.

13. **Think about the year as a slate: what are we missing?**
   "I like to think about what we're putting out as a slate" (S008)
   Sources: 9. Confidence: high.

14. **Spend for talent and treat it well; good treatment is a recruiting tool.**
   "You go out of your way to spend for talent." (S112) "Other people see that we treat talent well. And then they want to work here." (S112)
   Sources: 3 (S078, S092, S112). Confidence: high.

15. **Make deals that feel fair, and deliver bad news straight.**
   "I'm a big believer in just being as straightforward as possible, even if it's unpleasant" (S008) "They may not always be the best deals, but they feel like they've gotten a fair deal and they have been treated respectfully, and that's important." (S092)
   Sources: 2 (S008, S092). Confidence: medium.

16. **Give notes, not a blank check; the best creators want feedback.**
   "I'm not a proponent of saying 'Great, go do whatever you want.' ... I think the best creators want the feedback" (S112)
   Sources: 3 (S008, S112, S118). Confidence: high.

17. **In a corporate storm, keep your head down and let the programming do the talking.**
   "all you can do is continue to focus on the programming and let that do the talking." (S067) "There's nothing you can do but just continue to do your job." (S101)
   Sources: 8 (S007, S010, S059, S060, S063, S067, S101, S055). Confidence: high.

18. **Keep your eyes on your own work, not on competitors' schedules or money.**
   "A good operating principle is not to worry too much about what other people have airing and to keep your eyes on your own work." (S054)
   Sources: 5 (S009, S054, S055, S103, S112). Confidence: high.

19. **Awards are welcome but are never the reason to make or shape a show.**
   "It's nice to be nominated, it's really nice to win but it's not why we do the shows," (S090) "We're not making decisions based on how to win or not." (S059)
   Sources: 5 (S035, S059, S090, S092, S119). Confidence: high.

20. **Nobody knows what the next hit will be; do not predict, spread the bets.**
   "Anytime anybody tries to tell you what the next whatever is, nobody knows." (S008) "It's a reminder every year that none of us know anything." (S063)
   Sources: 5 (S008, S027, S063, S029, S112). Confidence: high.

21. **Make international shows for their home audience; you cannot engineer a global hit.**
   "You can be aware of what travels historically, but you can't engineer it." (S061)
   Sources: 5 (S061, S085, S100, S108, S002). Confidence: high.

22. **Judge the material, not the announcement or the idea in a press release.**
   "I hope that people will judge the actual material, not how it could be or might be. We will rise and fall based on the material," (S077)
   Sources: 3 (S033, S077, S120). Confidence: high.

23. **When you are wrong, say so in public and fix the process.**
   "If I had to do it again, I would have rolled it out with the producers talking on the record so people understood where they're coming from." (S120)
   Sources: 4 (S031, S047, S082, S120). Confidence: high.

24. **A marketing campaign cannot carry a show; the creative has to stand on its own.**
   "A marketing campaign can only do so much; the show and the creative have to stand on its own." (S004)
   Sources: 1 (S004). Confidence: low.

25. **Every era must re-prove the brand; compete with your own legacy first.**
   "There's always going to be something you have to kind of re-prove. That's the nature of the job." (S008) "I do think, to some extent, our biggest challenge is living up to what HBO has done in the past and continuing that." (S112)
   Sources: 5 (S008, S025, S061, S068, S112). Confidence: high.

26. **Profit is what pays for risk; know how the model makes money.**
   "Historically, HBO has been incredibly profitable and made a lot of money. It allows us to take chances and take big swings." (S008) "What everybody is trying to figure out is, 'How do you make money doing this?'" (S058)
   Sources: 5 (S008, S018, S058, S061, S088). Confidence: high.
