# Principles: Children's Publisher

Composite archetype.

- PUB-P1 Design for the adult who buys and the child who keeps reading. Confidence high.
- PUB-P2 Know the age band. Confidence high.
- PUB-P3 Unit cost decides the price. Confidence high.
- PUB-P4 Small first run, fast reprint. Confidence high.
- PUB-P5 Pre-sell where you can. Confidence high.
- PUB-P6 A series beats a one-off. Confidence high.
- PUB-P7 Pay for illustration and clear the rights. Confidence high.
- PUB-P8 Original Armenian content is the edge over cheap imports. Confidence medium.
- PUB-P9 Edit hard; children's text is short and every word counts. Confidence high.
- PUB-P10 Build a direct audience (parents, teachers) before the third title. Confidence medium.
- PUB-P11 Cash is tied up in stock; count copies as money on a shelf. Confidence high.
- PUB-P12 Translation rights abroad are extra income once a title proves itself at home. Confidence medium.
