# Stories: Children's Publisher

Composite archetype. Typical cases, not real events.

- The 3,000-copy first book: a lovely title, 2,200 copies still boxed a year later. Lesson: small first run.
- The school order that paid for the run: a pre-sale to schools covered the print bill. Lesson: pre-sell.
- The series that built a shelf: book three sold more than book one because readers came back for the character. Lesson: a list beats a title.
