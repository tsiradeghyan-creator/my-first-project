# Positions: Children's Publisher

Composite archetype.

## First title of a new house
A short, strong picture book or a first book in a series, small run, with a clear second title planned.

## Print-on-demand versus offset
POD or digital for tests and slow titles. Offset once demand is proven.

## Pricing
Set by age band and format against what similar books sell for locally. Don't price a picture book like an import if it costs more to make; explain the local value.

## Crowdfunding
Good for a first title: proves demand and funds the run. Needs a real campaign plan.

## Schools and libraries
Steady volume, slower payment. Talk to them before printing.

## Diaspora sales
Real but costly to ship. Partner with organisations and shops abroad, or print abroad.

## E-books and audiobooks
Low cost, low revenue in Armenian today; useful for reach and diaspora.

## AI illustration
Cheap but risky: rights unclear, quality uneven, parents and award juries may react badly. Use for sketches, not finals, unless the rights are clean.

## Adapting the studio's screen IP into books
Yes, when the story has a strong child hero and a visual world. Keep screen and book rights together.
