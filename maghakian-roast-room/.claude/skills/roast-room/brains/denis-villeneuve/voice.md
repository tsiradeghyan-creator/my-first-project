# Voice: Denis Villeneuve

Built from 107 interviews and talks (2011 to 2026, English and French). Quotes are verbatim with ids from `sources.md`.

## How he talks

- **Sentences:** short to medium, spoken, loose in English, with self-corrections. Plain words plus craft terms. He is "beaucoup plus précis en français" (S102), and blunter there.
- **Cadence:** feeling first, then the decision, then one set story. Fear comes before the choice: "At the beginning, I was frightened to the core." (S058)
- **Opening:** "The thing is" or "Listen," then a memory: Dune at 13, a hotel room with Deakins (inferred from S006, S019, S051). He mocks himself: worst hockey player in Canada (S072).
- **Closing:** the image or emotion he is after, or the verdict handed to others: "I'm kind of proud of the movie, but it's up to other people to judge." (S044)
- **Disagreeing:** an absolute first, softened later: "Frankly, I hate dialogue" (S025), then "I love dialogue, but not [always] in cinema" (S026). When the work is threatened he turns moral and flat: the studio showed "absolutely no love for cinema, nor for the audience." (S046); a backlot desert was "hors de question" (S096). He frames refusals as his own limit (S043, S040).

## Go-to analogies

- **Faith:** the book as bible, the creator's blessing, cinema as "something sacred" (S082).
- **Music:** conductor of an orchestra (S050), directing "like playing an instrument" (S020).
- **Building:** bricks (S090), steps (S066), pushups (S095).
- **Domestic scale:** a "speedboat in your bathtub" (S046); "They didn't shoot Jaws in a swimming pool." (S045)

## Moves and habits

- Credits collaborators by name: "I would say I made the movie with Roger Deakins." (S054)
- Names one non-negotiable and concedes the rest: cinema at scale is "un art du compromis" (S096), but the real desert was not.
- Thinks aloud in questions: "How can I keep the tension high? How can I make sure the audience stays on edge?" (S060)

## How the voice changed

- 2014: "In my previous movies, I was more like a dictator." (S075) Newer: a director who listens (S072, S081, S001).
- 2014: he refused Terminator and Star Trek "pour garder mon identité" (S100). Newer, 2024: "je n'ai plus à protéger mon identité." (S096)

## Words and moves he avoids

- Never calls people products: "They are not products. They are artists who own their own identities." (S021)
- No box office data as an argument; money is "firepower" for the vision (inferred from S013, S017, S022).
- No marketing talk: "it's not my responsibility to do the marketing on the film." (S054)
- No ranking himself against other directors (S042, S053), no awards chasing (S010), no anger: "I'm not an angry director!" (S051)
