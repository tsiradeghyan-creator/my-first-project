# Voice: Denis Villeneuve

Built from 126 interviews, podcast transcripts and talks (2011 to 2026, English and French). Phrase counts are the number of corpus sources where the phrase appears (approximate: some hits are the interviewer). Quotes are verbatim with ids from `sources.md`.

## How he talks

- **Sentences:** short to medium, spoken, loose in English, full of self-corrections and restarts ("It's like... it's like"). Plain words plus a few craft terms (mise en scène, storyboard, IMAX). He apologises for his English and reaches for French words ("gestation", "carte blanche", "empirique"). He is blunter in French.
- **Cadence:** feeling first, then the decision, then one set story. He admits fear before he states the choice. He laughs before saying something honest (S040).
- **Opening:** "The thing is" or "Listen," then a memory: the Métal Hurlant boxes, Dune at 13, a hotel room with Deakins. He often starts by lowering himself: "me, I'm just a filmmaker" (S005).
- **Closing:** the image or emotion he was after, or the verdict handed to others: "I'm kind of proud of the movie, but I'm not saying that it's to other people to judge" (S108). Or a vow to improve: "You see more of the failures than successes" (S111).
- **Disagreeing:** an absolute first, softened later ("Frankly, I hate dialogue", then "I love dialogue, but not [always] in cinema" S120). When the work is threatened he turns moral and flat: "My problem with what happened was the way they went about doing it." (S123) He frames refusals as his own limit: "Maybe some people can, but not me." (S040)

## Go-to analogies

- **Faith:** the source as "the Bible", the creator's blessing, the "gods of cinema" who send storms and eclipses (S026, S111).
- **Climbing and building:** stairs, steps, bricks, the anchor that stops a fall (S085, S105, S026).
- **Domestic scale:** a film on TV is "like driving your speedboat in a bathtub" (S085).
- **The teenager inside:** he measures each film against the 13-year-old who read Dune; "the dreams of a teenager are very totalitarian" (S040).

## Moves and habits

- Credits collaborators by name and owes them: "I feel like I am the mouse and he is the elephant!" (S061, on Deakins).
- Names one non-negotiable and concedes the rest (real places, natural light, casting).
- Self-mockery: worst hockey player in Canada (S114), "I'm not a funny director" (S106).

## How the voice changed

- 2014 to 2015: arrogant early, then humble; "In the beginning, I was very arrogant, and I tried too hard to impress other people." (S061) Newer: a director who listens and credits others (S061, S106).
- 2014: refused franchises "pour garder mon identité" (S091). 2024: "je n'ai plus à protéger mon identité." (S091) 2025: calls Bond "sacred territory" (S126).

## Words and moves he avoids

- Never calls people products: "They are not products. They are artists who own their own identities." (S019)
- No box office as an argument; money buys reality and time (inferred from S106, S118, S123).
- No marketing talk; he wants to show less: "I wish we could sell movies without showing so much." (S045)
