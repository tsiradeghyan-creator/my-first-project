# Principles: Denis Villeneuve

Built from the extract notes on 126 sources (interviews, podcast transcripts, festival Q&As and profiles, 2011 to 2026, English and French). 26 decision rules, most-used first. "Sources" counts distinct sources that carry the rule. Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Lines marked "inferred" are a reading of several sources, not his words.

---

1. **The image carries the film; dialogue is the last resort.** If a line can be replaced by an image, replace it.
   - "I'm not someone who remember movies because of their lines. I remember movies because of their images" (S105)
   - "If I could have made movies with one - without any dialogue, it would have been paradise." (S105)
   - Sources: 6 (S017, S023, S026, S105, S120, S048). Confidence: high.

2. **A great film should work with the sound off.** Test the cut, the board and the scene silently.
   - "a great movie, you should be able to watch it without sound. And that's the ultimate goal, yeah." (S105)
   - "The ultimate goal is to make a movie without dialogue and try to do something that is as cinematic as an early silent movie" (S026)
   - Sources: 4 (S023, S026, S105, S120). Confidence: high.

3. **Find your intimate door before you accept someone else's story.** If you cannot find one, do not take the job.
   - "I always try to find an intimate way to get into the story to I have to create an intimate link" (S005)
   - "To shoot something that you don't know is a very bad idea for a film director." (S104)
   - Sources: 7 (S005, S026, S045, S104, S107, S110, S115). Confidence: high.

4. **The project must call you; images must come.** Turn down strong projects that do not inspire you.
   - "It's just that I feel I'm not the right filmmaker to do it. And it doesn't inspire me. The images are not coming out." (S107)
   - "I feel as though a project chooses me, I feel a deep desire to explore. But there is no plan." (S120)
   - Sources: 6 (S106, S107, S116, S120, S129, S019). Confidence: high.

5. **Storyboard everything, then rewrite the script from the boards.** The mise en scene is born in the drawings.
   - "When I finish a screenplay, the first thing I do is to storyboard, not just to define the visual element of the movie, but also to rewrite the movie through images" (S025)
   - "Then, I go and re-write the screenplay from the storyboards because there's always a massive difference between them." (S122)
   - Sources: 7 (S005, S025, S046, S048, S106, S107, S122). Confidence: high.

6. **Prepare so well that you can throw the plan away.** Precision is what buys freedom on the day.
   - "it gives you more freedom at the end of the day. It allows you to improvise because you're so well, you're ready to shoot." (S107)
   - "we love to tear everything apart the storyboards and start from scratch, because we have a better idea on the day." (S048)
   - Sources: 5 (S005, S048, S104, S106, S107). Confidence: high.

7. **Shoot reality: real places, built sets, natural light.** Effects only extend what the camera photographed.
   - "Doing it live makes it real. There is a weight and a presence to it." (S045)
   - "It needs to look as real as the face of my actor." (S025)
   - Sources: 9 (S005, S013, S025, S040, S045, S048, S105, S106, S111). Confidence: high.

8. **Believe the actor first; protect the intimacy around the camera.** Scale is noise.
   - "if you don't believe the actor, then you won't believe anything." (S025)
   - "I have a strong capacity to forget about the scope of things and focus on the intimacy with the actors." (S105)
   - Sources: 6 (S025, S061, S105, S106, S122, S026). Confidence: high.

9. **Casting is the one decision with no compromise.**
   - "The most important part of the film process is casting. You need strong actors." (S045)
   - "I love actors who are good listeners." (S111)
   - Sources: 5 (S045, S106, S111, S118, S108). Confidence: high.

10. **Leave space for the chaos of life.** Plan the structure, then let actors and nature surprise you.
    - "I call it the chaos of life you know" (S005)
    - "instead of avoiding them, we started to chase them" (S026, on storm clouds)
    - Sources: 5 (S005, S026, S048, S111, S106). Confidence: high.

11. **Suggest rather than show.** The unseen threat is stronger than the seen one.
    - "I absolutely love how it's more frightening to not seeing the beast than actually seeing it." (S105)
    - "It's the power of suggestion. I think those are cinematic images, because it's evocation." (S061)
    - Sources: 4 (S048, S061, S105, S046). Confidence: high.

12. **Adapt by transforming, not transcribing.** Destroy the form, keep the author's spirit, and go back to the source when in doubt.
    - "I don't believe in direct adaptation. I think that it's a violent process where you transform, it's like alchemy." (S048)
    - "when we do an adaptation it's a very violent process you need to there's a brutal moment when you need to destroy the original" (S005)
    - Sources: 8 (S005, S040, S048, S085, S108, S110, S118, S119). Confidence: high.

13. **Kill darlings for the hero's point of view.** Cut side stories; give each kept scene several jobs.
    - "We have to kill a lot of darlings. That's the truth." (S118)
    - "trying to find scenes like that that will convey as much as possible information, not going away from the dramatic arc of the characters and trying to make sure that it will not feel like a lecturing or over-exposition." (S118)
    - Sources: 3 (S105, S108, S118). Confidence: high.

14. **Welcome the newcomer as well as the fan.** Someone who never read the book must feel at home.
    - "To make sure that someone like my mother who didn't read the book would feel welcome in the world" (S119)
    - "to make sure that if someone will not have seen Part 1, we'll still be able to enjoy Part 2." (S110)
    - Prepare the ground before the big scene. Opening Dune cold on the Gom Jabbar left people processing information "instead of entering the scene and receive it viscerally." (S118)
    - Sources: 3 (S110, S118, S119). Confidence: high.

15. **Climb in steps, never a step too high.** Each film adds one new skill; a small film can bridge a big jump.
    - "I believe in steps." (S061)
    - "I needed to slowly build stairs, something solid under my feet." (S105)
    - Sources: 6 (S026, S061, S085, S105, S108, S019). Confidence: high.

16. **Keep an anchor when you enter the machine.** A small personal film protects your identity if the big one goes wrong.
    - "If I ended up getting crushed by the system on Prisoners , I knew my creative identity would still be intact with Enemy ." (S019)
    - "I needed to do something pure, an act of cinema, a love of cinema." (S068)
    - Sources: 3 (S019, S026, S068). Confidence: high.

17. **State your terms to the studio up front, and be willing to walk.**
    - "I put everything on the table, saying I'm ready to do this film if I can do it with my own sensibility." (S068)
    - "I just told the truth, and being straightforward might be why I got the project. I wasn't trying to please them." (S019)
    - Sources: 4 (S019, S068, S123, S120). Confidence: high.

18. **Never walk in your own footsteps.** Return to a world only with a new genre and pace, out of urgency, not nostalgia.
    - "I don't want us to walk into our own footsteps." (S117)
    - "I said to myself that it's a good idea to come back to this world not [with a sense of] nostalgia but of urgency" (S116)
    - Sources: 5 (S106, S116, S117, S128, S129). Confidence: high.

19. **Make peace with failure before you start.** Accepting the likely failure is what frees the work.
    - "when you accept that you accept that fate then you become free" (S005)
    - "You're meant to fail, you're gonna fail. It's like you have to accept" (S106)
    - Sources: 4 (S005, S106, S107, S121). Confidence: high.

20. **Fear is real; go when the excitement is bigger.**
    - "But the excitement of doing it was far more powerful than the fear." (S123)
    - "I have some kind of a self-destructive device inside me to take projects that are out of my reach" (S121)
    - Sources: 3 (S040, S121, S123). Confidence: high.

21. **Choose the key artists at the very start.** Bring the DP, and when the score carries the film, the composer, in before the script is done.
    - "I needed a close partner from the start" (S005)
    - "the score in Dune will be essential, will be absolutely crucial to the success of the movie to bring that kind of sacred quality that I wanted." (S106)
    - Sources: 4 (S005, S048, S106, S110). Confidence: high.

22. **Commit early; burn the boats.** Pick the look and the shot on set instead of keeping options for post.
    - "I love to work with cinematographers that aren't afraid to burn the boat right at the start." (S111)
    - "It's not like you work with a keyboard and can change your mind all the time." (S025)
    - Sources: 3 (S025, S111, S104). Confidence: high.

23. **Protect the crew's rhythm; tired people make worse films.** Fixed hours, no endless nights.
    - "I didn't do that on Blade Runner and I almost died." (S106, on French hours)
    - "not to be so ambitious at this time, because it was too warm." (S111)
    - Sources: 3 (S105, S106, S111). Confidence: high.

24. **Pause when the work is hollow.** Stop, learn, and come back only for something meaningful.
    - "If I go back behind the camera, it'll be for something meaningful." (S019)
    - "I came back with more humility, and the will to be at the service of great stories." (S061)
    - Sources: 3 (S019, S061, S120). Confidence: high.

25. **Judge yourself by the process, not the result.** Reception is out of your hands; learn from the gap.
    - "So for me, it's not about the result, but it's about the process." (S108)
    - "So the success is very intimate. How the world would perceive it later, you have no control over that." (S118)
    - Sources: 4 (S048, S068, S108, S118). Confidence: high.

26. **Keep the world complex, not black and white.** A hero can become the problem; let the film warn.
    - "I don't like black-and-white." (S085)
    - "I wanted her to become a moral compass at the end, so the movie would be perceived as a cautionary tale" (S111)
    - Sources: 3 (S085, S110, S111). Confidence: high.
