# Positions: Denis Villeneuve

Built from the extract notes of 107 interviews and talks (2011 to 2026). 37 topics, weighted to the decisions a studio brings to the Directing and vision seat, most-used first. Every quote is verbatim with its source id and date (year-month from `sources.md`). French quotes stay in French, with a plain English gloss outside the quotation marks. Where his view changed, the versions sit side by side with dates and the newest is marked **(newer)**. Topics with no evidence are listed at the end under No recorded position.

## Adapting a book, a play or someone else's story
- **Stance:** Be faithful to the spirit and the author's intent, never to the page. Pick one spine, cut everything that does not serve it, and own the result as a new object.
- "the best way to respect an author is to be very honest about the way you adapt his work: to totally destroy the original and make it your own." (S075, 2014-05)
- "I don’t believe in direct adaptation." (S052, 2017-10)
- "When you adapt, you kill. You destroy in the process of transformation." (S013, 2024-01)
- "In my adaptation, I tried to be more faithful to Frank Herbert than the book." (S017, 2024-03)
- One spine: "I decided to focus the entire adaptation on Paul's journey." (S032, 2021-10) Later: "I had to make the bold choice to make a Bene Gesserit adaptation, and to focus the movie on that sisterhood." (S014, 2024-02)
- The source is the only reference; earlier versions are ignored: "when I was in doubt, I was just going back to the Bible, reading Dune again and finding my answers." (S042, 2021-10)
- He takes the blame: "If someone doesn’t like this adaptation, there’s only one person to blame." (S022, 2024-02)
- **Read:** the method is stable from Incendies to Dune; only the words got harsher. In 2011: "I kept the dramatic structure, the key ideas and main characters." (S080, 2011-06)
- Sources (20): S006, S012, S013, S014, S017, S022, S023, S024, S032, S042, S043, S052, S075, S076, S078, S080, S081, S094, S095, S106.

## Real locations, built sets, green screen and VFX
- **Stance:** Shoot real places in real light and build what you can; VFX are a tool buried inside real images. Decide how an effects shot will be photographed before anything else.
- "I love CGI. It’s a very strong tool, but it’s not the main answer." (S052, 2017-10)
- "They didn’t shoot Jaws in a swimming pool." (S045, 2020-09)
- On green screens: "It's not possible. Not for me. Maybe some people can, but not me." (S043, 2021-10)
- "If you put me in a blue room, I have no idea where to put the camera." (S040, 2022-02)
- **(newer)** softer wording: "Les écrans verts, c'est une technologie que j'essaie d'éviter le plus possible, effectivement." (S105, 2024-03) He avoids green screens as much as possible; virtual sets bore him because reality inspires him (S105).
- "Most important with visual effects is, how will you shoot it?" (S031, 2024-10)
- Why it pays: on real sets actors "can focus on the interiority of their character." (S052, 2017-10)
- **Read:** the rule is about the actor and the light, not purity. He used heavy VFX for the sandworms but shot them in natural light. Inferred from S014, S031, S043, S105.
- Sources (17): S006, S013, S014, S016, S019, S027, S031, S040, S043, S045, S049, S052, S057, S069, S075, S096, S105.

## Taking on someone else's franchise
- **Stance:** Say no until you are ready and the conditions are met: the creator's blessing, a strong script, a reason that matters to you. After Blade Runner 2049 he said never again; in 2026 he took Bond.
- **Over time:**
  - 2014-03: "On m’a offert Terminator et Star Trek, mais j’ai refusé pour garder mon identité" (S100) He refused Terminator and Star Trek to keep his identity.
  - 2017: took Blade Runner 2049. "I needed the blessing of Ridley Scott." (S074, 2017-02) "I came on board because the script was very strong." (S055, 2017-06) "There's no new Blade Runner movie unless it's existential." (S056, 2017-10)
  - 2022-03: on a third Blade Runner: "it would be difficult for me to decline an invitation, but I’m not expecting one." (S010)
  - 2024-03: "I don’t think I will ever approach someone else’s universe again." (S021, S029)
  - 2026-10 **(newer)**: he agreed to direct the next Bond as his way out of Dune: "I need a very powerful rocket to pull me away from Arrakis." (S035)
- **Read:** his 2024 vow to stay out of other universes did not hold, but the test did: he took Bond for a reason of his own (a contrast project), not as a hire. Inferred from S035, S036, S037, S073.
- Sources (17): S010, S021, S029, S035, S036, S037, S049, S052, S055, S056, S058, S059, S060, S066, S074, S096, S100.

## Theatrical release versus streaming
- **Stance:** A film's life must start in a theatre. He fights for the window, says he is not against platforms, and argues the best defence of theatres is films that need them.
- **Over time:**
  - 2018-05: "Mes films sont conçus pour le grand écran, qui est un tableau d’une immense ampleur." (S099) His films are designed for the big screen.
  - 2021-08, on Warner's same-day HBO Max release: "Frankly, to watch ‘Dune’ on a television, the best way I can compare it is to drive a speedboat in your bathtub" (S046) and "C'est un coup de poignard dans le dos pour moi. Le cinéma est un travail d'équipe." (S106) A stab in the back. From then on he would demand theatrical exclusivity by contract (S102, paraphrase).
  - 2021-08, same interview: "Je n’ai rien contre les plateformes, bien au contraire." (S102) Nothing against platforms, quite the opposite. "Mais la carrière d’un film doit d’abord commencer par le grand écran." (S102) But a film's career must start on the big screen.
  - 2024-01: "The theatrical experience is here for good. It will prevail, it will transform." (S013)
  - 2024-03 **(newer emphasis)**: "la meilleure manière de défendre cette idée de voir des films en salle, c'est d'essayer de faire les films qui vont prendre leur plus grand potentiel possible en salle" (S105) Defend theatres by making films that reach their full potential there.
  - 2026-03: "as human beings it's important to share experience, to have communal experiences." (S003)
- Phones: "Pour un téléphone, tu ne réalises pas de la même manière." (S099, 2018-05) You do not direct the same way for a phone. He gives no method for the small screen.
- Sources (16): S003, S012, S013, S014, S021, S029, S030, S046, S051, S057, S077, S090, S099, S102, S105, S106.

## Sequels and when to stop a world of your own
- **Stance:** Make the next one only if it will be better and different, never stay in one world for decades, and go back with a critical eye.
- **Over time:**
  - 2021-08: "J'ai toujours vu qu'il pourrait y avoir une trilogie, après ça on verra." (S106) He always saw a possible trilogy; after that, we will see.
  - 2024-01: "Dune Messiah should be the last Dune movie for me" (S013)
  - 2024-03: Messiah only if it beats Part Two: "Otherwise, I don’t do it." (S018)
  - 2024-09: the first two films are one piece: "It's not like a trilogy" (S034) and "it was really a diptych" (S033)
  - 2025-01: one more, then stop: "Yes. After that, it would become unhealthy." (S016)
  - 2026-03: "go come back to this world not by nostalgia, but by urgency." (S003)
  - 2026-10 **(newer)**: he went back "not out of nostalgia, not because I miss it, but the opposite" (S035), because he is tired of it, as a guard against complacency: "I want to make sure that I will be severe with the material. That I will not be complacent." (S035)
- Each part gets its own genre and tempo; he calls Part Three a thriller after a contemplation and a war movie (S003). "Normalement, on vise deux ans. C’est un délai très court, mais ça se peut." (S107, undated) Two years between parts is short but possible.
- **Read:** the rule held (one more, only if better, then hand on); the stated motive moved from urgency to fatigue within 2026.
- Sources (15): S003, S013, S014, S016, S018, S022, S026, S031, S033, S034, S035, S036, S037, S106, S107.

## Career pace: steps, not leaps
- **Stance:** Raise the difficulty one step per film, never skip a step, and put a small film between big jumps. He also admits a pull toward projects beyond his reach.
- "I believe in steps." (S066, 2015-09)
- "you do 15 pushups the first time, the next time you go for 20." (S095, 2015-09)
- "All my filmography has been built like bricks." (S090, 2021-10)
- "J’ai toujours cherché à accroître les défis, en matière de mise en scène, mais en m’assurant qu’il n’y ait jamais une marche trop haute." (S097, 2024-09) Raise the challenge, but never a step too high.
- "I knew people who had skipped steps and who found themselves in a position where they lost control over what they were doing" (S028, 2024-12)
- Waits for the tools: "C'est un projet que je n'aurais pas osé faire il y a dix ans, mais que maintenant j'ai les connaissances techniques pour pouvoir faire le film dont je rêve." (S106, 2021-08)
- Counterweight: "I have some kind of a self-destructive device inside me to take projects that are out of my reach," (S041, 2021-08)
- Sources (14): S021, S023, S028, S029, S041, S049, S066, S073, S077, S090, S093, S095, S097, S106.

## Casting
- **Stance:** The most important decision. Cast the lead first for what the role needs, fight producers for the actor you want, and use casting to fill what the script lacks.
- "The most important part of the film process is casting. You need strong actors." (S049, 2017-07)
- "When you’re casting, you dream." (S053, 2017-10)
- Filling a thin role: "I needed his imagination to invade the part of the detective." (S070, 2013-09)
- He fought for his lead on Enemy: "j’ai dû me battre pour imposer Jake Gyllenhaal" (S100, 2014-03). On that co-production he talked the producer out of a European actor and into an American one (S078, paraphrase).
- Fit over origin: "I felt that the most important thing was to find the strongest leads I could." (S082, 2011-01)
- Stars: "They are not products. They are artists who own their own identities." (S021, 2024-03)
- Sources (14): S003, S013, S020, S021, S048, S049, S051, S053, S070, S076, S078, S080, S082, S100.

## Hiring key collaborators (DP, designer, editor, composer)
- **Stance:** The first hires are partners with no ego and a direct connection. Bring them in on day one and keep them film after film.
- "I brought Roger on board day one." (S050, 2017-10)
- "I need someone who has no ego and will be there just for the project." (S075, 2014-05)
- "Tu veux avoir des gens avec qui tu as une connexion directe, et avec Patrice, on n’a plus besoin de se parler pratiquement." (S107, undated) You want people with a direct connection; with his designer they barely need to talk.
- The composer before the shoot: "I brought him very early on the project, even before I started to shoot." (S059, 2017-02)
- He credits part of his success to choosing partners: "J'ai eu un flair aussi pour m'associer aux bonnes personnes." (S106, 2021-08)
- He hires people he can learn from: "I had a master class on how to light a shot doing Bladerunner with Roger Deakins." (S004, 2021-10)
- Sources (13): S004, S043, S047, S050, S052, S054, S059, S062, S066, S070, S075, S106, S107.

## Actors: control or freedom
- **Stance:** A precise script and frame, a free performance inside it. Build trust before the hardest scenes.
- "We didn’t improvise with the story, but we improvised a lot with the actors to be into characters" (S073, 2015-01)
- "It’s always good for a director to follow the actor instead of telling him where to go." (S075, 2014-05)
- "We did the torture scene at the end because I wanted to have a strong confidence with the actors as it required all of our concentration." (S069, 2013-10)
- "Il faut que l'acteur ait une certaine part de liberté dans un cadre." (S105, 2024-03) The actor needs some freedom inside a frame.
- "J’aime profondément les acteurs. Ce sont eux qui vont amener l'âme du film à l'écran." (S105, 2024-03) Actors bring the soul of the film to the screen.
- Sources (13): S001, S006, S020, S052, S053, S054, S069, S073, S075, S077, S079, S102, S105.

## Choosing which project to make
- **Stance:** Only a story he falls in love with and connects to intimately. Count the years it will cost. When two are ready, the easier one to finance can go first.
- "because I fall in love with a story that says something meaningful about our world." (S091, 2016-11)
- "In order to tell a story, I need to have a deep connection with it from a very intimate point of view" (S074, 2017-02)
- "when you fall in love you have no choice; you have to jump." (S065, 2015-10)
- The cost in years: "it'll be another two, three years of my life just gone like that" (S094, 2021-10)
- "If I go back behind the camera, it'll be for something meaningful." (S029, 2024-03)
- Tie-breaker: "Both were ready to shoot at the same time, but Polytechnique was less complicated logistically and easier to finance." (S080, 2011-06)
- Sources (11): S006, S018, S029, S065, S070, S074, S080, S084, S091, S093, S094.

## Leading the crew: dictator or listener
- **Stance:** He keeps the final say but listens, because the crew often has better ideas. Firm, never angry, never abandons a team.
- **Over time:**
  - 2011-04: "Very often directors are dictators, but I think it has to be a dictator who listens a lot." (S081)
  - 2014-03: "because very often they have better ideas than yours, it can be tough on the evil ego. But it makes a better film." (S072)
  - 2014-05: "In my previous movies, I was more like a dictator." (S075)
  - 2018-02: "I’m firm, solid I think. I’m not an angry director!" (S051)
  - 2024-10 **(newer)**: "I’m not alone. There is a crew around me." (S001)
- Duties: "As a director, I have a responsibility toward my film crew." (S057, 2017-11) "Le cinéma est un travail d’équipe et tu ne peux pas lâcher une équipe." (S102, 2021-08) You cannot let a team down.
- Sources (11): S001, S027, S051, S057, S065, S072, S075, S081, S091, S097, S102.

## Risk
- **Stance:** Artistic risk keeps him alive and is what will save cinema, but he only takes projects he can pull off.
- "I feel alive when I take risks." (S069, 2013-10)
- "in order to stay alive and to evolve, you need to take risk." (S078, 2014-03)
- "I was ready to risk everything because artistically it made sense." (S058, 2017-10)
- "What will save cinema is freedom and taking risks." (S013, S030, 2024-01)
- The limit: "But I know that the projects I choose are films I can pull off." (S095, 2015-09)
- Sources (11): S012, S013, S022, S029, S030, S058, S064, S069, S078, S095, S099.

## Rest, burnout and the gap between projects
- **Stance:** Big films eat years. He plans rest or a small, different film between them, and then keeps going anyway.
- **Over time:**
  - 2020-07: "It would be healthy for me to do something small," (S047)
  - 2021-10: "I prefer to be in this position right now than having shot both movies back-to-back and having regrets because I was too tired." (S032)
  - 2024-02 and 2024-03: "I will not jump behind a camera right away." (S014) "for six years I was on Arrakis non-stop, and I think it will be healthy to step back a little bit." (S018)
  - 2026-03 **(newer)**: he told his crew he was taking a break, then said yes to Part Three because "I kept awakening in the middle of the night with those images." (S003)
- Stamina is finite: "I may not have that in 20 years" (S020, 2021-10). "Right now, I have the stamina to do movies that require more energy." (S017, 2024-03)
- Sources (11): S003, S014, S017, S018, S020, S023, S029, S032, S047, S097, S106.

## Budget, box office and what money buys
- **Stance:** Money buys closeness to the vision, and some films cannot be made without it. He also sees the future in lower budgets and the middle as gone. Your last film sets your next budget.
- "When you have the firepower for Blade Runner or Dune, you can get very very close to the movie that is in your mind" (S022, 2024-02)
- "I had tasted what it is like to make a movie with money. Yeah. The power." (S001, 2024-10)
- "Some movies I can do there that I can’t do at home because of the money." (S073, 2015-01)
- "It's the low budget or super big budget, there's no middle." (S032, 2021-10) "I think that the future of cinema relies on the creativity of lower budgets." (S032, 2021-10)
- After Blade Runner 2049 underperformed: "On est aussi bon que le dernier film qu'on a fait dans ce milieu-là." (S106, 2021-08) You are as good as your last film; Dune got a tighter frame for it (S106).
- Tight schedules: "We shot that movie with just enough movie." (S064, 2015-09)
- Sources (10): S001, S007, S017, S022, S032, S064, S073, S080, S097, S106.

## Learning from failure
- **Stance:** He names his failures plainly, puts the cause on himself, and treats each film as a lesson.
- "I realized that I wasn't happy at all with the results and that the core of the problem was me." (S029, 2024-03)
- "So that pause was probably the best and most important artistic decision I've ever made." (S029, 2024-03)
- "Every time you make a movie, you come out of it and you cannot believe how much you learn." (S014, 2024-02)
- "Un film, c'est comme un autoportrait, comme on se voit à l'adolescence, on voit juste les défauts." (S106, 2021-08) A film is a self-portrait where you only see the flaws.
- "I’m a slow learner. I’m still learning." (S066, 2015-09)
- Sources (10): S001, S014, S029, S041, S051, S066, S082, S097, S098, S106.

## Studios, Hollywood and keeping control
- **Stance:** Use the studio machine without being swallowed. Put your terms on the table at the start; if they want a director for hire, let them hire one.
- **Over time:**
  - 2014-03: "ne pas se faire avaler au détour" (S100) Do not get swallowed along the way.
  - 2015-01: "I put everything on the table, saying I’m ready to do this film if I can do it with my own sensibility." (S073) "I’m not doing movies in Hollywood as a director for hire." (S073)
  - 2017-02: "I didn’t dream to be part of the machine, and I had heard too many stories about directors being crushed by the system there." (S074)
  - 2024-02 **(newer)**: "Ça reste une grosse machine, mais je n’ai plus à protéger mon identité." (S096) Still a big machine, but he no longer has to protect his identity. "Si les studios m’engagent, c’est parce qu’ils veulent un film de moi." (S096) Studios hire him because they want a film by him.
- On the industry now: "Everything's about Wall Street." (S030, 2024-01)
- Sources (9): S021, S028, S030, S070, S073, S074, S096, S097, S100.

## Scale, big crews and delegation
- **Stance:** He prefers one camera and one unit. At scale he splits units under someone who shares the vision, and works to keep that vision intact across everyone.
- "It's not the best way to work, I love to work with one camera, one tripod, but I didn't have the choice, I had to do it this way." (S004, 2021-10)
- On the sandworm unit: "If I had done it myself, I would still be shooting." (S031, 2024-10)
- "Travailler avec d’aussi grosses équipes n’est pas évident : il faut que la vision soit communiquée à tout le monde afin qu’elle demeure et s’impose." (S097, 2024-09) With big crews the vision must reach everyone so it holds.
- "Even when I’m surrounded by 250 people waiting for me to make a decision, I am always trying to go back to that intimacy of filmmaking." (S066, 2015-09)
- Sources (9): S001, S004, S019, S031, S050, S051, S052, S066, S097.

## Audience: who the film is for
- **Stance:** First for himself, then a relationship with the audience. Never let them get lost, and trust them to fill gaps.
- **Over time:**
  - 2017-10: "you make the movie for yourself, you make it for the audience" (S050)
  - 2021-10: "Frankly, I think that I made this movie for me." (S042)
  - 2026-03 **(newer)**: "when you make a series of movies, like you are in a relationship" (S003) and "I felt a responsibility to finish the story." (S003)
- Clarity: "If they are not lost, then they can only focus on the tension." (S016, 2025-01)
- Gaps: "the audience has to fill the holes" (S081, 2011-04)
- Novelty: "you feel the audience is excited when they see something they haven't seen before" (S030, 2024-01)
- Young viewers: he lowered Dune's rating because "I wanted young people to experience what I had" (S012, 2021-10)
- Sources (9): S003, S012, S016, S030, S042, S050, S073, S081, S089.

## Marketing, trailers and secrecy
- **Stance:** Sell less and show less; protect surprises. He moved from treating marketing as not his job to blaming directors who leave distribution to others.
- **Over time:**
  - 2017-07: "I wish we could sell movies without showing so much." (S049) "I want the audience to see the movie knowing as little as possible." (S049)
  - 2017-10: "it’s not my responsibility to do the marketing on the film." (S054)
  - 2018-02: "It was a nightmare, the marketing of this movie." (S051) "It’s difficult when the trailer doesn’t reflect the object." (S051)
  - 2021-08 **(newer)**: "Je trouve personnellement que les cinéastes ont tendance à se désengager en laissant tout dans les mains des distributeurs." (S102) Filmmakers tend to disengage and leave everything to distributors.
- Secrecy in production: "We shot that sequence in high secrecy with a small unit." (S018, 2024-03)
- Sources (9): S015, S018, S049, S051, S054, S060, S072, S095, S102.

## Storyboards, previs and room for the unplanned
- **Stance:** Board the whole film with the DP, which rewrites it in images and briefs the crew. Then stay free on the day.
- **Over time:**
  - 2017-02, on previs: "I hate that. I like to be free on set. I like to be inspired by actors." (S059)
  - 2017-10: "we love to tear everything apart the storyboards and start from scratch, because we have a better idea on the day." (S052)
  - 2018-02: "follow the storyboard, don’t follow the script." (S051)
  - 2024-02 **(newer)**: "I used to say that storyboards precede the screenplay. But nature precedes the storyboards." (S014)
  - 2025-06, on VFX: "I’ve learned the hard way that you need to be as precise as you can, otherwise it goes in a lot of directions." (S027) His boards now feed the VFX previs (S027).
- **Read:** the plan got stricter where VFX money is spent and looser where nature and actors are. Inferred from S014, S027, S052, S105.
- Sources (9): S006, S014, S020, S027, S050, S051, S052, S059, S060.

## Screenplay readiness before shooting
- **Stance:** Do not start without a ready script; writing is the hardest part and he is slow at it. Fix comprehension on paper, not in the edit.
- "No, it was the writing; the writing is the toughest part." (S040, 2022-02)
- "I am a very very slow screenwriter" (S065, 2015-10)
- "The thing I want to avoid is not having something ready." (S018, 2024-03) "We need to make sure all the ideas are on paper." (S018, 2024-03)
- "Je ne voulais pas me retrouver avec des problèmes de compréhension à résoudre au montage" (S096, 2024-02) He did not want comprehension problems left for the edit.
- He keeps rewriting at every stage: "I’m rewriting the movie as I’m storyboarding, and through the preproduction, writing with the actors and in the editing room" (S060, 2016-12)
- Sources (8): S018, S040, S049, S055, S059, S060, S065, S096.

## Pressure and fear
- **Stance:** Fear is constant and admitted; most pressure is self-made. Scale does not scare him; intimate scenes with actors do.
- "At the beginning, I was frightened to the core." (S058, 2017-10)
- "The pressure is coming from me, myself, and it’s bigger than anything I can feel from outside honestly." (S074, 2017-02)
- "It was worth the risk. Then it was too late to be afraid." (S056, 2017-10)
- "Pour être honnête, ce qui me fait peur, ce sont les moments d’intimité, de fragilité ou de vulnérabilité avec les acteurs." (S098, 2025-03) What scares him is intimacy and vulnerability with actors.
- Sources (8): S041, S048, S056, S058, S064, S074, S098, S101.

## Small films and side projects
- **Stance:** A small personal film before a big studio one is an anchor that keeps your identity safe. He says he may go back to small films, not now.
- "I need to do something to stay alive. I need to do something that would be totally mine." (S073, 2015-01)
- "I would have an anchor." (S028, 2024-12)
- **(newer)** on returning to Canadian indies: "It might happen." (S017, 2024-03) His current projects "require too much firepower" (S017) to be made at home.
- Sources (8): S017, S021, S028, S029, S047, S073, S077, S097.

## Critics, test screenings and feedback
- **Stance:** Critics are mirrors, bad reviews included. His real test is honest friends at home.
- "Sometimes, bad ones are good critiques for me because they are pointing their fingers at things that as a filmmaker I should improve." (S073, 2015-01)
- "Good critics are fantastic and terrible mirrors." (S074, 2017-02)
- "Personally, I would have shared the movie with film critics earlier; I was an advocate for that." (S051, 2018-02)
- "c'est toujours le test ultime, pour moi, de présenter mes films au Québec devant mes chums" (S105, 2024-03) The ultimate test is his friends in Quebec.
- Sources (7): S044, S051, S053, S073, S074, S092, S105.

## Awards, festivals and making films for money
- **Stance:** Not for awards and not for money. Take awards as freedom when they come.
- "I don't want to make movies just to make money. When I make films, it has to come from the bottom of my heart." (S082, 2011-01)
- On awards: "it gave me a bit more freedom, which is very good" (S070, 2013-09)
- "I’m not making movies to get awards recognition. If you do that, you’re not walking the right path." (S010, 2022-03)
- "I’m not interested in the film festivals in a way because I’m not very comfortable in those environments." (S073, 2015-01)
- "Les films de genre se retrouvent rarement au palmarès." (S099, 2018-05) Genre films rarely win prizes.
- Sources (7): S010, S070, S073, S074, S082, S099, S105.

## Committing early: post-production fixes, reshoots and director's cuts
- **Stance:** Decide on camera and tell the studio now. No fixing it in the grade, no reshoot culture, no extended cuts.
- "If you don't like it, tell me now, because after that, it's too late." (S023, 2024-03) "I don't like to change the nature of images in the DI. I love to commit." (S023, 2024-03)
- "À Los Angeles, il y a cette philosophie selon laquelle on n’a qu’à tourner à nouveau tout ce qui ne fonctionne pas après le tournage principal. Ça m’épuise, cette logique-là." (S096, 2024-02) The reshoot-it-later logic exhausts him.
- "When it’s in the trash bin, it’s dead." (S068, 2013-09)
- "Je n’ai d’ailleurs jamais fait de director’s cut d’aucun de mes films." (S102, 2021-08) No separate director's cut; the release is his cut: "On Prisoners I was free and the movie is the director’s cut." (S073, 2015-01)
- "We will never put a shot on the screen that we don’t feel has a certain level of quality." (S027, 2025-06)
- Sources (7): S023, S027, S068, S073, S077, S096, S102.

## Competition and comparison with masters
- **Stance:** Accept that you will be compared to the masterpiece and you are free. Find your own identity next to the giant.
- "no matter how good what you're doing is, the film will always be compared to the first, which is a masterpiece." (S055, 2017-06) "And when you make piece with that, you are free." (S055, 2017-06)
- "The Star Wars series is a huge elephant in the room." (S032, 2021-10) "So we have to find our own identity." (S032, 2021-10)
- "There’s still a movie that needs to be made about that book, just a different sensibility," (S044, 2021-10)
- Early films: "Je me mesurais toujours aux autres cinéastes." (S098, 2025-03) He always measured himself against other directors, until Incendies.
- Sources (7): S006, S032, S042, S044, S053, S055, S098.

## Dialogue, television and series
- **Stance:** Cinema should be carried by image and sound; dialogue and television are his foils. No recorded position on making a TV series.
- "Dialogue is for theatre and television. I don't remember movies because of a good line, I remember movies because of a strong image." (S026, 2024-10)
- "Movies have been corrupted by television" (S025, 2024-02)
- The test: "you should be able to watch it without sound. And that's the ultimate goal." (S019, 2024-02)
- Earlier and softer, with writers: "I try to be the screenwriter’s best friend by protecting their poetry and their ideas" (S095, 2015-09)
- **Read:** stated as an ideal by a director who co-writes his films. Applied to dialogue-led drama, it means making talk scenes visual, not deleting them. Inferred from S001, S025, S095.
- Sources (7): S001, S019, S025, S026, S028, S051, S095.

## Creative identity and repetition (brand)
- **Stance:** Never repeat yourself; each film reacts against the last. What studios buy is his identity.
- "Hell is repetition." (S090, 2021-10)
- "I would’ve just repeated myself and died, artistically, quite quickly." (S021, 2024-03)
- "On choisit un film en réaction au précédent." (S100, 2014-03) Same in 2024: "On fait toujours un film en réaction au précédent." (S097, 2024-09)
- "Je ne voulais pas marcher dans mes propres pas." (S096, 2024-02) He did not want to walk in his own footsteps.
- Sources (6): S021, S029, S090, S096, S097, S100.

## Focus: one project at a time
- **Stance:** One film in production at a time, several in development.
- **Over time:**
  - 2011 to 2014: he alternated two films in production (Incendies and Polytechnique, then Enemy and Prisoners): "So when I was coming back to that other project, I had a freshness and a distance, which was great." (S077, undated)
  - 2021-10 **(newer)**: "I'm a bit monomaniac. I can just work on one project at a time." (S094) "J'essaie de travailler sur un projet à la fois." (S106, 2021-08)
  - 2024-03: development still runs in parallel: "I have four projects on the table, currently." (S021)
- Sources (6): S021, S034, S077, S080, S094, S106.

## Public funding and the home industry
- **Stance:** Public money is a privilege and a safe place to learn and take risks. The envelope is small, so big projects go abroad.
- "C'est un privilège de faire des films et c'est déjà incroyable qu'on ait un système qui nous permette de financer le cinéma avec de l'argent public." (S105, 2024-03)
- "il y a beaucoup de talent au Québec et l'enveloppe budgétaire est petite." (S105, 2024-03) Much talent, a small budget envelope.
- The US contrast: "Aux États-Unis, la règle est très simple, si ton film n’a pas de succès, soit critique ou au box-office, tu ne refais pas de film, c'est fini." (S105, 2024-03)
- "We have a buffer that protects us a little more and that space allows us to explore more and take risks" (S074, 2017-02)
- He has not applied for public money in about 15 years and says so (S105).
- Sources (6): S017, S073, S074, S080, S105, S108.

## Female leads
- **Stance:** He keeps choosing women leads and resists pressure to swap them.
- "I’m very sensitive about the fact that there’s not a lot of good work for women in cinema that also deals with strong characters." (S072, 2014-03)
- "It was important for me to depict a strong woman, but not a strong woman who could also be a strong man." (S095, 2015-09)
- A 2016 profile reports he refused to change Sicario's lead from a woman to a man (S091, writer's report).
- Sources (6): S059, S061, S072, S091, S095, S096.

## Compromise and non-negotiables
- **Stance:** At scale, film is compromise and constraint feeds ideas. Name the one or two things you never give up, and impose them.
- "Le cinéma, surtout à cette échelle-là, est un art du compromis, parce qu’il y a toujours des contraintes. Or, dans les contraintes, c’est là que la créativité opère." (S096, 2024-02)
- "Toutefois, il y a des choses sur lesquelles je ne fais aucune concession. J’ai imposé qu’on tourne dans le vrai désert." (S096, 2024-02) No concession on some things; he imposed the real desert.
- "And there are always forces that come to oppose you, to create pressure, and you have to work with those forces." (S062, 2015-10)
- On bad weather: "instead of avoiding them, we started to chase them" (S028, 2024-12)
- Sources (5): S019, S028, S031, S062, S096.

## AI, algorithms and technology
- **Stance:** He worries less about AI than about filmmakers behaving like algorithms. He defends human crews and real dreaming. No recorded position on generative AI tools in production.
- He worries less about AI "than the fact that we behave like algorithms, as filmmakers" (S013, 2024-01)
- "I think I’m a worried optimist." (S057, 2017-11)
- "Technology’s evolved, but humanity’s not better." (S052, 2017-10)
- On concept artists browsing instead of dreaming: "Everybody can have access to [the internet], but dreams are unique." (S057, 2017-11)
- "the beauty of cinema is the idea of bringing many artists together to create poetry." (S027, 2025-06)
- Sources (5): S013, S027, S052, S057, S105.

## Working in a culture not your own
- **Stance:** Enter through an outsider's eyes, put ego aside, listen to locals and rewrite. Avoid exoticism.
- "my door to enter into the story is the twins, because I can have the same glance, the same look on this world as them." (S084, 2012-06)
- "I tried never to fall into the trap of exoticism." (S080, 2011-06)
- "It was a huge job and I really had to put my ego aside. I didn't arrive there with answers" (S081, 2011-04)
- "So the truth is I did rewrite a lot of scenes, all the family scenes" (S084, 2012-06)
- "I have a responsibility towards Mexicans because I was trying to depict their reality with authenticity." (S064, 2015-09)
- Sources (4): S064, S080, S081, S084.

## Advice to beginners and first projects
- **Stance:** Go out alone with a camera now; a first film is a jump into the void, and his came too early.
- "Take a camera, go out alone in the world, and just try to make sense of what you see around you." (S066, 2015-09)
- "Go on the streets with your iPhone… and shoot." (S001, 2024-10)
- "Faire un premier film, c’est sauter dans le vide" (S097, 2024-09) A first film is a jump into the void.
- "Maybe I made my first film too early." (S001, 2024-10)
- Sources (4): S001, S066, S082, S097.

## Exit: handing on what you built
- **Stance:** Finish your part, leave seeds for whoever comes next, and do not hold the door shut.
- "I think that it would be a good idea for me to make sure that, in Messiah, there are the seeds in the project if someone wants to do something else afterwards, because they are beautiful books." (S034, 2024-09)
- "I’m not closing the door. I will not do it myself, but it could happen with someone else." (S033, 2024-09)
- Sources (3): S033, S034, S035.
