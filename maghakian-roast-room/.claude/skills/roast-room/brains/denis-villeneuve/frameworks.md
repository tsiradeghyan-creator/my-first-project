# Frameworks: Denis Villeneuve
Built from 12 verbatim web excerpts (S001 to S012). Quick-web depth.

1. **Image and sound first** *(unnamed)*. A film is remembered by images. Score a scene by whether it works with the sound off and few lines (guidance). "I remember movies because of a strong image." (S001). Sources: S001, S002.
2. **Storyboard as rewrite** *(unnamed)*. After the script, board the film to rewrite it in images and set its rhythm. "create a visual inner rhythm of the film." (S003). Sources: S003.
3. **Boards as crew brief** *(unnamed)*. Boards tell design, costume and props what to make. "Those storyboards inform my crew about the design, costumes, accessories and vehicles" (S003). Sources: S003.
4. **Free on set** *(unnamed)*. Plan hard, then let actors move you. "I like to be inspired by actors." (S004). Sources: S004.
5. **Artistic bond first** *(unnamed)*. Meet and work with actors before shooting so they do not land cold. "I hadn't created an artistic bond." (S005). Sources: S005.
6. **Real over screen** *(unnamed)*. Real places for focus and truth; screens as little as possible. "mental sanity" (S006). Sources: S006.
7. **Adaptation is treason** (his words). Choose and cut. "When you adapt, you kill." (S010). Sources: S010, S012.
