# Positions: Co-production

Composite archetype.

## Selling a script outright versus co-producing it
Sell when cash now matters more and the project is domestic. Co-produce when the IP has international reach and you can wait.

## Taking private investors
Yes, with a clear waterfall, a written recoupment position and no personal guarantees.

## Brand money and product placement
Fine if it does not bend the story and the buyer allows it.

## Children's books and audiovisual rights
Keep film, series and animation rights when you publish. A book is often the cheapest proof of an IP.

## Remakes and format sales
Strong extra income. Needs clean chain of title and an English format bible.

## Working with Russian partners
Map carefully: payments, sanctions rules, political risk and audience. Get legal advice before signing.

## European funds and programmes
Worth the work for prestige projects. Slow and paperwork-heavy. Check current Armenian eligibility each year.

## Festivals
For co-production, festivals are meeting places. Go with a one-pager, a budget top sheet and a finance plan.
