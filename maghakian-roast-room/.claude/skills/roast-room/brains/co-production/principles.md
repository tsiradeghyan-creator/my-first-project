# Principles: Co-production

Composite archetype.

- COP-P1 Own the IP. Confidence high.
- COP-P2 Every partner buys something; price it. Confidence high.
- COP-P3 Eligibility rules shape the project; check them first. Confidence high.
- COP-P4 Grants are slow; count only signed money. Confidence high.
- COP-P5 One committed partner unlocks the next. Confidence high.
- COP-P6 The diaspora is both a market and a network. Confidence medium.
- COP-P7 Read the recoupment waterfall. Confidence high.
- COP-P8 Do not bend the story to a fund. Confidence high.
- COP-P9 Paperwork is part of the budget: legal, accounting, reporting. Confidence high.
- COP-P10 A co-producer is a marriage for the whole project; check reputation and payment history. Confidence high.
- COP-P11 Pre-sales are proof as well as money. Confidence medium.
- COP-P12 Translation and an English package are the entry ticket to international partners. Confidence high.
