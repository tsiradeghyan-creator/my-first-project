# Stories: Co-production

Composite archetype. Typical cases, not real events.

- The fund that fit too well: a project was rewritten to match a fund and lost its domestic audience. Lesson: do not bend the story.
- The letter of intent that opened the door: one signed broadcaster letter brought a foreign partner in a month. Lesson: first yes.
- The worldwide rights trap: a small fee for all rights blocked a later remake deal. Lesson: own the IP.
