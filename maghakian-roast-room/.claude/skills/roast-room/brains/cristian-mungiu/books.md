## Tania Ionașcu, bunica mea. O biografie basarabeană (Humanitas, 2023)
- **Thesis (in my words):** The life of his grandmother, who fled Bessarabia after the 1940 Soviet ultimatum while her family stayed and was shot or deported. One fast decision, shaped by attachment to possessions, decided a family's fate; big history reaches small lives near an empire's border. (S044, S081)
- **Origin:** Notes he took with her in the 1990s, organized during the pandemic; the war in Ukraine made it urgent. (S081, S091)
- **What it shows about how he decides:** Some stories suit books, not films ("Filmul e mult mai imprecis" S081 [film is much less precise]); a screenplay and a possible series came from it (S009, S091); advice: talk to your grandparents now (S044).
