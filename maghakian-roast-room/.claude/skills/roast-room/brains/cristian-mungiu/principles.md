# Principles: Cristian Mungiu

Built from the extract notes on 101 sources (2007 to 2026). 24 decision rules, most-supported first. Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Romanian and French quotes stay in the original with an English gloss outside the quotation marks.

1. **Do not tell the viewer what to feel: no score, no cut that points.** "There is no editing in life. You can't cut out the things that you don't like." (S002) "I don't think they have to be told when to be emotional or not." (S101) Sources: 12 (S002, S011, S012, S015, S017, S037, S094, S096, S101, S105, S044, S022). Confidence: high.

2. **Make only personal films about a world you know in detail.** "I spend so many years making a film, it'd feel awful to do something that anyone could do. We have short lives as filmmakers ... I only make personal films." (S018) "If you don't know the context as well as others do, then I think it is better to let others make the film. It is not a question of language, it's a question of details." (S038) Sources: 7 (S018, S035, S038, S045, S096, S108, S068). Confidence: high.

3. **Start from a real case, then fictionalize.** "I don't really make up things." (S026) "My effort is to understand the things happening next to me." (S051) Sources: 11 (S011, S026, S034, S047, S048, S049, S051, S080, S091, S094, S096). Confidence: high.

4. **Take no side; give every character their best argument.** "My position as a citizen on this issue is not in the film." (S010) "If [I was] judgmental with the characters, if I took one of these sides, the other side is not going to watch it" (S048) Sources: 10 (S005, S008, S010, S013, S027, S048, S049, S050, S102, S110). Confidence: high.

5. **Keep final cut, or do not make the film.** "I don't think the way that I work is possible to be translated here, because I make all the decisions." (S032) "Altfel sa fiu doar regizorul unui film in care producatorul face montajul final nu ma intereseaza." (S068) [Being only the director where the producer has final cut does not interest me.] Sources: 4 (S032, S044, S063, S068). Confidence: high.

6. **Choose the kind of film you make over the budget it could have.** "am decis că pentru mine e mai important ce fel de film fac și să mă reprezinte decât ce buget are filmul ăla și ce box office va avea global" (S063) [what kind of film matters more than its budget or global box office]. "For me it is more important to be truthful than to be famous or rich." (S044) Sources: 4 (S044, S063, S068, S098). Confidence: high.

7. **Small industry: the director is also producer, distributor and lobbyist.** "we work with very very little money but we had to become not only the writers, directors of the film but also the producers and after that I will just release my films and sell my films" (S006) "It's a very small industry here in Romania, so we are involved in everything." (S022) Sources: 6 (S006, S022, S032, S066, S105, S044). Confidence: high.

8. **Do not wait for perfect money; start when the story and season are right.** "However, if you have a good project, you find the money. Sometimes the challenges can help you to express yourself." (S038) "We did what everyone does in that situation: start anyway, hoping something comes up. And indeed, the money ran out." (S044) Sources: 4 (S022, S038, S044, S046). Confidence: high. Limit: he admits this left Occident unfinished until the Quinzaine promise freed fund money (S022).

9. **Keep the hard scene even when partners doubt it.** "I have to say, nobody believed in this scene." (S001) "you either have the balls to show how things go on or not. If not you better just do something else." (S015) Sources: 4 (S001, S015, S019, S032). Confidence: high.

10. **Precision first: rehearse, know the text, shoot until it is free.** "this requires a level of precision you never have in theater and you're never going to have again in film, so you need to know the text by heart" (S001) "In order to do this, we rehearse a lot and I shoot a lot of takes." (S005) Sources: 8 (S001, S005, S012, S017, S033, S036, S037, S097). Confidence: high.

11. **Do not repeat a formula that worked.** "I try not to follow the same formula twice. The problem today is that an enormous amount is produced but always the same way." (S038) "After you make a film that is successful, the main question you have to ask yourself is 'Am I going to make the film again or should I make something else?' and I decided to make something else." (S102) Sources: 5 (S038, S042, S061, S079, S102). Confidence: high.

12. **The second and third film are the real test.** "It's not difficult to make a film that can surprise people once. But to make the next film" (S010) "It's easy to get noticed with your first film. Especially since most funding tends to be given to first films." (S042) Sources: 5 (S010, S027, S042, S045, S109). Confidence: high.

13. **Fund on track record.** "There needs to be some priorities based on what you did. That's all." (S006) Sources: 1 (S006). Confidence: low, but it is the rule he wrote into the 2006 law fight (S105).

14. **Prizes are context; judge a film in 10 to 20 years.** "Un premiu rămâne ceva conjunctural. La film trebuie să aștepți 10, 15, 20 de ani ca să vezi dacă rămâne în timp." (S062) [A prize is circumstantial; wait 10 to 20 years.] "Probably with a different jury it would have been a different Palme, who knows?" (S006) Sources: 5 (S005, S006, S062, S063, S083). Confidence: high.

15. **Art films must not be boring.** "Nu cred deloc că filmul de artă trebuie să fie ceva care să fie dificil de consumat sau plicticos, doar că trebuie să aibă ceva de spus" (S061) [art need not be hard or boring, but must say something]. Sources: 4 (S008, S059, S061, S063). Confidence: high.

16. **Result beats purity.** "if you can carefully avoid them, but the result lacks grace, then you were better off using them" (S021) "You know, what matters for me is the result. It's very difficult to get there, but what matters is the result." (S033) Sources: 2 (S021, S033). Confidence: medium.

17. **Question every formal habit.** "if you start questioning these decisions and you check how many of them you make because the story requires you to make them and how many of them you just make out of habit, you see that you make a lot of things which are not necessarily required by the story" (S105) Sources: 2 (S105, S079). Confidence: medium.

18. **Keep searching on set; do not arrive with the film already known.** "If you already know what the film will be like, there is no point making it." (S038) "But filmmaking is not a science and you need to keep your mind free and your eyes open, to experiment on the set" (S110) Sources: 3 (S038, S079, S110). Confidence: high.

19. **A film fails only when framed by objectives.** "A film can only fail if it is framed by objectives." (S042) "Making films that are liked isn't the be all and end all." (S042) Sources: 1. Confidence: low.

20. **Simple and personal beats showing off, especially for a debut.** "in cinema, it really all comes together when you manage to create something simple that reflects you in some way" (S042) Sources: 2 (S042, S079). Confidence: medium.

21. **Build the audience; nobody else will.** "E dreptul oricui sa aleaga, dar numai sa aiba din ce." (S067) [Everyone may choose, but only if there is something to choose from.] "the audience for this kind of filmmaking is really shrinking, so you have to invest in education a lot" (S022) Sources: 4 (S022, S055, S067, S087). Confidence: high.

22. **Ask the state to apply its own law, not for medals.** "Nu e vorba de inventat lucruri noi, e vorba de aplicat ce există deja" (S062) [not inventing new things, applying what exists]. "It should suffice that the Ministry of Finance and the Ministry of Culture would issue a lawful order" (S055) Sources: 5 (S055, S056, S062, S064, S058). Confidence: high.

23. **Distribution, not prizes, is the win.** "Marea noastră reuşită este când reuşim să intrăm pe poarta mare a distribuţiei şi să arătăm acest film în reţelele de distribuţie." (S087) [our real success is entering the main distribution networks]. "My greatest joy is that the film has been bought for distribution in more territories than ever for any of my films." (S090) Sources: 4 (S022, S063, S087, S090). Confidence: high.

24. **Tell your stories in their time or lose them.** "daca nu zici la timpul lor povestile pe care le ai de scris ele nu raman acolo pe vesnicie ci se pierd" (S068) [stories not told in their time are lost]. Sources: 2 (S068, S044). Confidence: medium.
