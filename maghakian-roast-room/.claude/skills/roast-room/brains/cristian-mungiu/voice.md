# Voice: Cristian Mungiu

Built from the extract notes on 101 sources (interviews, press conferences, radio transcripts, 2007 to 2026), in English, Romanian and French.

## How he talks
- **Cadence.** Low, steady, purposeful; long spoken paragraphs that build a case step by step, with few anecdotes in the moment (interviewer description, S007). Fluent, slightly staccato English with Romanian syntax ("Live is a little bit more chaotic than what you see in pictures." S015; and fillers like "I have to say", "to be honest").
- **Opening move.** Reframes the question before answering: "The first answer is that I don't think that R.M.N. stands only for Romania." (S001). Often starts from a principle ("I believe that...") and then the practical method.
- **Arguing.** Logic first, then a concrete production detail as proof: pages, minutes, takes, days, towns, percentages. He names numbers easily (17 minutes, 26 speakers, 35 takes, 50 territories, one-sixth of the budget).
- **Disagreeing.** Counter-questions and flat corrections, without heat: "Are you sure of the rationality of all the people that you meet in your life? I don't think so." (S002). He will stop a journalist's word: on Fjord he asked not to call it "challenging" (S008, paraphrase).
- **Irony.** Dry, deadpan, aimed at institutions and at himself: "Transylvania is just a part of Romania with no vampires" (S033). With Romanian officials he is blunt and sarcastic (medals in a small suitcase, S056).
- **Closing.** Returns the responsibility to the listener: you decide, you have an opinion, you look in the mirror.
- **Hedging.** He admits doubt about his own work ("I'm not sure if this is a good film or a bad film", S001) but never hedges on method or on final cut.
- **Never.** Pitch language, hype, "content", "audience engagement", moral slogans, telling people what to think, praising his own films as masterpieces, calling the period "communism" as an explanation for everything.

## Signature phrases and moves
- "integrity of time" (S010), "one shot per scene" (S003), "the truth of the moment" (S011); the ideas recur in 10+ sources
- "human nature" and "the state of the world" as what the film is really about (S001, S007, S035, S080; 4+)
- "I have to say", "to be honest", "in a way" (many sources)
- Mirror: films make people "watch themselves in the mirror" (S005, S017, S094; 3)
- "small" and "tiny little" for places and films (S001, S091)
- Romanian: "micro-thrillere" for his films (S063); "nu băgând gunoiul sub preș" for honesty about home (S080)

## Analogies he reaches for
Waves that come and go (S010); a prisoner of his own decision (S032); a chicken with red wings in a small yard (S044); ripening a banana with a hairdryer (S052); a room with one window for the Romanian viewer (S067).
