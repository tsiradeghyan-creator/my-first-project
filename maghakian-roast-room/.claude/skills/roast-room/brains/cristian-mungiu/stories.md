# Stories: Cristian Mungiu

Built from the extract notes on 101 sources (2007 to 2026). 16 stories he reuses, each with the lesson he draws.

1. **The rough cut that went to Cannes without a submission (2007).** Two days after principal photography he screened a rough cut in Rotterdam, which had put money into post, for sales agents; a DVD went to Berlin; the Quinzaine called first, then Un Certain Regard, and the film was moved to Competition a day or two before the announcement. Lesson: a strong sales agent and early screenings make the festival path. (S015, S006)

2. **The Palme d'Or for a second film (2007).** "And, remember, I didn't get it for my eighth film. I got it for my second." (S027) Lesson: the prize makes all the difference for a year and raises the bar for every next film; selection is never automatic again. (S006, S027, S045, S109)

3. **The medal and the missing debate.** At home people treated the Palme like a sports medal; the president gave him a medal without seeing the film, and the abortion debate the film was meant to start never came. Lesson: awards and public debate are different things. (S005, S022, S029, S036)

4. **The caravan (2007).** With no cinemas left, he fundraised all summer, rented halls, brought projection from Germany and toured 4 Months to towns with no cinema for about a month; crowds came. He meant it as an alarm signal; in 2026: "Sadly, that caravan became the norm" (S055). Lesson: prove the audience exists yourself, then lobby. (S026, S044, S060, S105)

5. **Occident runs out of money (2002).** The fund grant was too small; money ran out mid-shoot, he brought everything he had to finish, and post was only funded after the Quinzaine called. Eight of them slept in one room 40 km from Cannes. Lesson: start anyway, but know the risk. (S022, S044)

6. **The 2006 law fight.** His generation sat at one table and demanded a new cinema law with fair rules and priorities based on track record; first films have had money since. "Well, yes, we got involved a lot. And I got involved a lot." (S105) Lesson: filmmakers must shape the funding system. (S006, S105, S022)

7. **Hollywood offers after the Palme.** Agents and scripts arrived; he asked "will I make the films I want, or the films they want?" (S044) and stayed for final cut. Dennis Hopper's invitation to rewrite a script taught him he would be noticed more at home. Lesson: control beats scale. (S044, S068, S063)

8. **The 17-minute town meeting (R.M.N., 2022).** 25 pages, 26 speaking parts, two days. Nobody believed in it, including his French partners; it went badly until he wrote the cast a letter and told the extras their reactions mattered. Lesson: do not drop the hard scene; precision and the extras' energy make it. (S001, S024, S034)

9. **The 12-minute dinner (4 Months).** Planned for two days, took four and 5,000 meters of film; he changed lenses mid-way because the shot was "the last supper". Lesson: when a long take fails, fix your own framing before blaming the actors. (S015)

10. **The actor who refused to be manipulative (Graduation).** A small-theatre actor turned down the lead because he would never be as manipulative as the character. Lesson: "People cover shit under a pile of words." (S017)

11. **Who threw the stone.** His French distributor said it was not clear enough; Frémaux said it was too clear. He kept it. Lesson: two opposite notes mean the balance is right. (S019)

12. **The mayor in the audience (Graduation, 2016).** Touring before local elections, he sat next to a mayor who was the film's character and did not see himself in it. Lesson: people watch someone else's story; the film still speaks about them. (S005)

13. **The two debates (R.M.N. tour).** In Ditrău and 30 villages, the official Q&A was polite; outside the hall people said what they really thought. Lesson: the private conversation is the truth a film should show. (S010, S024, S028)

14. **Sebastian Stan, ten years in the making (Fjord).** They met after a New York screening (his mother drove him); they waited for the right project; he wrote the script only once Stan agreed; Stan shaved his head after a test on a fan stand-in; came without entourage; Mungiu delayed the shoot for Stan's Oscar campaign. Lesson: a star is worth waiting for if they work on your terms. (S047, S056, S059, S061, S063, S076)

15. **Waiting for the snow in Norway (Fjord, 2025).** The Norwegian crew planned weeks ahead; his Romanian team changed the schedule daily waiting for snow, and shot on a Sunday with an ad hoc crew when it came. Lesson: hold the image the film needs, even against a partner's habits. (S063, paraphrase)

16. **The neighbour who never knocked (Fjord).** Norwegian partners left a note for a location owner instead of knocking; he turned out kind, and after the shoot the two neighbours became friends. Lesson: dialogue needs someone to start it, the theme of the film. (S047)
