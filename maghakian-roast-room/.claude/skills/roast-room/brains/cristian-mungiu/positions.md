# Positions: Cristian Mungiu

Built from the extract notes on 101 sources (2007 to 2026). Stances on the questions this seat gets: budget, financing, co-production, festivals, distribution, casting, stars, language, schedule, plus the usual founder topics. Each line has a verbatim quote, source id and date. Where he changed his mind, both views are given and the newer is marked. "No recorded position" means the corpus is silent; do not guess.

## Production and money

**Budget size.** Small by choice and by necessity; he has never had a big budget and says Romania cannot fund scale. 2007: "This is a film I made on a tight budget. I'm surprised it's being shown in 60 countries ... it's a local story." (S036). 2022: "And all this on a very small budget, in a village far away from civilization." (S091). 2026: "otherwise we will remain stuck with chamber films" (S056). Rough figures from the corpus: 4 Months was "a very little budget", all Romanian money (S022); Beyond the Hills about 1.75 million euro, CNC 36.1 percent (S084).

**Raising money: start or wait.** He starts when the story and season are ready, even short of money. 4 Months was financed "entirely in Romania" with no outside producer (S022); Beyond the Hills was shot fast in winter right after its CNC award to reach Cannes (S072, journalist). "We did what everyone does in that situation: start anyway, hoping something comes up. And indeed, the money ran out." (S044, 2025, about Occident). Counterweight: by 2026 Fjord was financed across six countries before shooting (S090).

**State funding.** 2008: the CNC competition gives "like half the budget that you need, but not more than let's say 400, $600,000 US." (S105). 2012: problem is cultural, the industry missed the moment to set a standard after 2007 (S046, paraphrase). 2026 (newer): the problem is unpaid levies: "Cinematografia română nu primește bani de la bugetul de stat" (S064) [Romanian cinema gets no money from the state budget]; the gambling levy debt is over 130 million euro (S055, S058).

**Co-production.** Late and selective. "J'ai fait mes trois premiers films sans aucun coproducteur français." (S066, 2018). Since Beyond the Hills, Why Not and the Dardennes' Les Films du Fleuve; Fjord with six countries. Partners have power over the script: "I didn't give the screenplay to anybody back home, but of course I had to submit it to some financing bodies, so I gave it to my partners in France" (S001, 2022). Cost of the system: "oblige les cinéastes à travailler avec des budgets relativement restreints" (S066). He fears identity-less co-productions (2013 Brussels delegation, S052 paraphrase) yet made one; the guard is that nationality follows the writer-director (S087).

**Producing other people.** Reluctant producer: "I have created this production company a while ago but I'm not chasing work. I created it just for myself to produce my films." (S006, 2018). Yet Mobra produced Tales from the Golden Age episodes, Lemonade, Traffic, HBO and Netflix series and serviced Audiard's The Sisters Brothers (S006, S059). "Nu suntem talibani, în sensul în care să producem doar un anumit gen de filme." (S059, 2025) [we are not purists about one kind of film].

**Debt and loans.** CNC money on Beyond the Hills was partly a loan (1.18 million lei of 2.81 million; S084). No recorded position of his own on debt.

**Equity, investors, selling the company.** No recorded position.

**Private money and sponsors.** 2008: "there are no private money involved" in Romanian film (S105). 2026 (newer): Fjord carried about 20 Romanian corporate partners (S090) and he asks the private sector and diaspora to fund the Oscar campaign: "a fost un film foarte scump şi am luat mulţi parteneri" (S087) [an expensive film, we took many partners].

**Crew cost.** Bring your own key collaborators; on Fjord about 40 Romanians came partly because rates are lower than Nordic ones (producer Reu, S053). Norway's 8-hour day shocked him: "A big difference in Norway was that they only work 8 hours a day, even film crews! It was a shock for us." (S047, 2026). At home "La noi e totul sau nimic." (S063) [all or nothing].

**Schedule.** About fifty days for Beyond the Hills (S097, paraphrase); 36 days for Fjord (S053); two days for a 17-minute scene in R.M.N. (S001). Rule of thumb: "We normally can do one important dialogue scene per day and no more." (S017, 2016).

**Overshooting.** Once only: "for the first time in my life, I shot more material than absolutely necessary for the film" (S011, 2013), and it cost him sound time.

**Post-production deadlines.** Racing to Cannes hurts: "It wasn't helpful because what happens is one day you are overexposed to your own material and you can't make rational decisions any longer." (S011, 2013).

## Festivals, sales, awards

**Cannes.** The best launch, never automatic. "Trebuie ca de fiecare dată să reușești să împrospătezi ceea ce faci" (S061, 2026) [each time you must refresh what you do]. "e cel mai favorabil loc pentru film, e cel mai favorabil loc pentru cariera viitoare a filmului" (S063, 2026) [the most favourable place for the film's whole career].

**Palme d'Or effect.** It makes all the difference for a time: "It makes all the difference because the popularity; the notoriety of this award is so big" (S006, 2018), and money got easier: "it was easier to find money for this new film" (S098, 2012). But it buried the debate at home: "People felt as if Romania got the gold medal in skating or whatever." (S022, 2016).

**Oscars.** Changed his mind. 2016: "Noi facem un alt gen de cinema… Nu trebuie să râvnim la acest gen de premiu" (S083) [we make another kind of cinema, we should not covet it]. 2025-2026 (newer): a nomination needs a strong US distributor and 5 to 10 million euro of campaign: "Filmul trebuie să aibă cel mai grozav start, cel mai tare distribuitor şi un buget minimal, undeva între 5 şi 10 milioane de euro." (S087, 2026). He blames the CNC for choosing a film with no US distributor over R.M.N. (S080, 2025).

**Pre-sales.** Sell before shooting when the name allows. "The film was bought as a screenplay by many countries" (S022, 2016). Fjord: "a fost prevândut înainte să filmez în mai bine de 50 de țări" (S063, 2026) [presold to 50-plus countries before shooting].

## Distribution and audience

**Home market.** Lost. 2012: "I assume the audience for art films in Romania is pretty much lost for good. Our films have way more spectators abroad then at home." (S098). 2026: still "not a good sign for us" (S056). Numbers: about 400 cinemas in 1989 to under 40 by 2008, admissions from 20 million to under 2 million (S105, paraphrase); 50 to 55 towns with any cinema in 2026 (S055).

**Self-distribution.** Yes, out of necessity: caravans, village screenings, his own festival, his company Voodoo Films (S005, S055, S105). "My extreme example became the norm." (S044, 2025).

**Global audience.** His real market is abroad: "fiecare dintre ele este cumpărat şi distribuit, aşa cum este cazul acum, în mai bine de 100 de teritorii" (S087, 2026) [each film sold to 100-plus territories].

**Streaming and platforms.** Produced HBO Europe and Netflix series (S006, S059). Wants VOD and digital-ad levies for the film fund (S055, S064). Blockbusters will always own the market: "Blockbusterele vor domina întotdeauna piața." (S059, 2025).

**Future of auteur cinema.** Pessimistic, 2022-2023: "this type of auteur cinema is nearing its end" (S045, 2022); "We all know that cinema, if it's not fully dead, is dying." (S009, 2023). 2026 (newer, after Fjord's sales): more confident in reaching audiences with thriller form and stars (S061, S090).

**Genre films for small countries.** Against, at home: Romanian genre films would compete head-on with US films holding 90 percent of screens (S066, 2018). Yet he co-wrote Traffic as a heist film to close "această prăpastie dintre filmul de autor și filmul de public" (S059, 2025) [the gap between auteur and audience film].

## Creative decisions

**Stars.** Not needed and not wanted for most of his career; Romania has no star system (S039, S083). 2026 (newer): stars bring curiosity and sales, but only on indie terms and the right project: "I hope I will continue to work with major actors known all over the world, but the film itself also has to have something important to say" (S056).

**Non-professional and new actors.** Yes when the method allows: "Most of the actors I work with have very nice careers after that, even though most of them were acting for the first time" (S017, 2016). Not on 4 Months, because of the long takes (S037).

**Foreign-language actors and English.** 2012 to 2022: stays in Romanian because details matter; "I'm not interested in making a film in English just because I think I'll have a wider audience" (S045, 2022). 2026 (newer): made Fjord mostly in English and Norwegian, as a cautious step, because regional films hit a visibility ceiling: "E tot mai complicat să faci niște filme la un nivel decent de vizibilitate internațional , dacă ele sunt atât de regionalizate și ancorate într-o limbă" (S063).

**Moving to Hollywood.** No: turned down US scripts after 2007, needs final cut (S044, S063, S068). "I realized I would have a better chance of being noticed if I stayed in Romania, like a chicken with red wings in a small yard" (S044, 2025).

**Music.** Never a score; only end credits (S101). "Whenever you feel emotion in life, you feel it just from what happens to you. There's no music. So why should we be using music in films?" (S017, 2016).

**Political subjects.** Yes, but no militancy: "I don't like when my films are being seen as militant in any direction." (S007, 2025). He took the risk of being labelled conservative on Fjord: "I took this risk because I feel that we need to keep our freedom to express doubts on any kind of issues, including the sensitive ones." (S049, 2026).

**Funders' agendas.** Wary: "This positive discrimination comes from the bodies which finance films, it comes from the personal consciences of the filmmakers themselves." (S010, 2023).

**Development time.** Years. Two to three years a project (S083); Fjord's case sat in a folder for years (S048).

## Career, company, work

**Pace of output.** Slow: six features in 24 years. Travels about six months with each film: "I travel about six months after each film" (S022, 2016). "I don't plan this as a career. I don't see further than my next film." (S022).

**Side income.** Commercials and teaching, before and after the Palme: "I have the same office, drive the same car and still do commercials from time to time, just like before 2007." (S098, 2012).

**Hiring collaborators.** Keeps the same DP and editor; promotes from inside when they leave (S017). No recorded position on hiring for a company.

**Hours and burnout.** Works 12-hour days on shoots (S014); jokes his next wish is "a holiday after working continuously for some 18 months" (S098 paraphrase of his line). No recorded position on burnout.

**Marketing and content.** No recorded position on social media marketing. Promotion is festivals, press tours and Q&As (S022, S087).

**AI.** No recorded position.

**Metrics.** Counts territories sold and festival placement, not home box office (S087, S090).

**Competition with peers.** Healthy rivalry: "There's a kind of a positive competition among these directors to respect this level of quality" (S032, 2008).

**Staying or leaving the country.** Changed view. 2002 he urged people to stay; 2016: "nowadays I can hardly look in the eyes of an 18 year-old and tell him: stay, no matter what the cost" (S021). 2023: "Individual, nu te împiedică nimeni să reușești. Noi ca grup, ca societate, am ratat." (S081) [individually nobody stops you; as a society we failed].
