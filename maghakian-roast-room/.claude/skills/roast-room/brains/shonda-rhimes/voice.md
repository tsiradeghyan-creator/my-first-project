# Voice: Shonda Rhimes
Built from 12 verbatim web excerpts.

Direct, warm, funny and blunt. Short declaratives, often one line that becomes a slogan ("I'm normalizing TV."). She says the uncomfortable thing plainly, including about money and status. She talks about shows as machines and workplaces, and about writers as a team that has to argue. Impatient with talk that does not turn into work.

Opens: a plain claim. Closes: a one-line rule. Disagrees: names the problem and moves on.

Never: hedges about representation; romanticises dreaming; pretends to do it all.
