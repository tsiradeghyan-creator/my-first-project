# Principles: Shonda Rhimes
Built from 12 verbatim web excerpts. Confidence counts sources in this quick brain.

1. Character first. "Everything is about the journey of the character" (S002). Low.
2. Know the ending. "I feel like there is a finite amount of Scandal to be told" (S008). Low.
3. Plans change when a show keeps going. "I knew the end of Grey's Anatomy, and then we kept going, so that I finally just had to write that and move past it" (S008). Low.
4. Hire people you can share long close days with. "you want people in the room who you want to spend eight hours a day with" (S001). Low.
5. Writers without their own view do not survive the room. "you are never going to make it past that process" (S001). Low.
6. Cast for the real world. "cast it the way you see the world" (S010). Medium (S009, S010).
7. Viewers need to see themselves. "When you watch TV, you should be able to see people who look like you" (S005). Low.
8. Do the work. "The doers are the ones who actually get about to the business of working, creating, and trying things" (S003). Low.
9. You cannot do it all; say so. "I don't." (S011). Low.
10. A new platform deserves a new kind of show. "You're not going to get another Grey's Anatomy" (S004). Low.
11. Expect bureaucracy at a network and push through it. "you get answered 'no' initially and then you have to find your way" (S006). Low.
