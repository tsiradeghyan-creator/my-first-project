# Books: Shonda Rhimes

One memoir (with a 10th anniversary edition) and one co-written novel. Book text was not fetched; summaries are in my own words from how she teaches the books in talks and interviews. Her craft teaching otherwise lives in her MasterClass (summarized by a reporter in S087) and her talks.

## Year of Yes: How to Dance It Out, Stand in the Sun and Be Your Own Person (2015)
- **Core thesis:** A hugely successful, deeply introverted showrunner had let her life shrink to work and home. For one year she said yes to everything that scared her, and the act of doing each feared thing removed the fear.
- **Key frameworks:** say yes to what scares you for a set period (a rule that removes the choice); doing the thing undoes the fear; say yes to difficult conversations because peace is on the other side; say yes to your body and health as work, not as a feeling; accept compliments with "thank you"; admit you have help; "the hum" of work and why play restores it; learn to say no ("I'm sorry, I can't do that", no explanation); people who liked you unhappy may leave when you change.
- **Taught in sources:** S015, S019, S020, S026, S028, S039, S073, S077, S093, S101.

## Year of Yes, 10th anniversary edition (2025)
- **Core thesis:** Ten years on, the yeses changed her personality and her life; she added seven chapters on what came after.
- **Key frameworks:** say yes to finding home (she moved from Los Angeles to Connecticut in the pandemic); say yes to a hobby (golf, being bad at something effort can fix); say yes to mentors you choose (books and memoirs, not waiting to be picked); "you can have it all, but not all at once"; fear as information, not an enemy; saying yes builds the clarity to say no; openness to marriage after years of refusing it.
- **Taught in sources:** S015, S040, S074, S077, S098.

## Queen Charlotte: Before Bridgerton Came an Epic Love Story (2023, with Julia Quinn)
- **Core thesis:** A novel built backward from the series she wrote, in the voice of the Bridgerton books.
- **Key frameworks:** honor the source's tone; work with the original author; fiction inspired by fact.
- **Taught in sources:** S076, S095.
