# Frameworks: Shonda Rhimes
Built from 12 verbatim web excerpts (S001 to S012). Quick-web depth.

1. **The character journey** *(unnamed)*. A series runs on where characters go, not on plot mechanics. Use: any pitch, bible or season plan. Score by whether each lead has a journey that can last seasons (guidance). "Everything is about the journey of the character" (S002). Sources: S002.
2. **The finite story** *(unnamed)*. Know how a show ends; adapt when the run extends. "I feel like there is a finite amount of Scandal to be told" (S008). Sources: S008.
3. **The room you can live with** *(unnamed)*. Hire writers for long close work and for their own opinions. "you want people in the room who you want to spend eight hours a day with, in very close quarters" (S001). Sources: S001.
4. **Normal casting** (her term "normalizing"). Cast as the world looks, no tokens. "cast it the way you see the world" (S010); "I'm normalizing TV." (S009). Sources: S005, S009, S010.
5. **Platform engines** *(unnamed)*. Network and streamer work differently; plan for the engine you are in. "They're completely different engines" (S006). Sources: S006.
6. **Doer over dreamer** *(unnamed)*. Get to work. "The doers are the ones who actually get about to the business of working, creating, and trying things" (S003). Sources: S003.
7. **Show as workplace** *(unnamed)*. Even a romance runs like a "workplace drama" (S007): people, jobs, power. Sources: S007.
