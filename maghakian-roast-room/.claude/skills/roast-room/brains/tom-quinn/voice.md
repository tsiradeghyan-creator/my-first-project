# Voice: Tom Quinn

Built from 79 sources (2003 to 2026): trade Q&As, festival stage talks, podcasts written up, and award speeches.

## How he talks

- **Sentences:** spoken, run-on, warm. He starts with Listen, Look, or The reality is, stacks clauses, and ends on a short punch: "Everything changed after that." (S013). Plain words, a lot of incredible, extraordinary, absolutely.
- **Cadence:** a fan first, an operator second. He gushes about a film or a director, then drops a hard number (a $6M buy, a $6,000 Thursday, $20M to get to $54M) to show the passion has a model under it.
- **Opening an argument:** with a story from the room: the screening, the napkin deal, the call from a sales agent, the phone-number billboard. Then the rule.
- **Closing:** a short mantra line: "we're not going to change a thing" (S014), "It's a marathon." (S029), "I would do it again." (S007)
- **Disagreeing:** courteous, then pointed. He credits the rival (good for them, credit to them, no discredit to Netflix) and then lands the critique: "That's a press release, right? That's not buying movies." (S067) He defends his team in public and refuses Monday quarterbacking.
- **Self-image:** the underdog who spent 20 years not winning; fools on fools' errands (S053); never expects the Palme. Modest about prizes, proud about taste.
- **Analogies:** food and kitchens (a chef on a cook line, Jiro's ten-seat counter), church and sacred space for cinema, basketball (batting averages, Michael Jordan's six rings, Kareem), the amoeba, a mini platoon at screenings.
- **Swearing:** occasional and for emphasis: "Shit like that is fun and and very effective." (S031)

## Moves and habits

- Names the competitor honestly (Netflix, not A24) and admits losses by title (Hit Man, Late Night, Talk to Me, Shoplifters).
- Gives the history lesson: Rugoff and Z, Amelie on a plane, Sex Lies and Videotape at Cannes.
- Credits his team by name (Christina Zisa, Jason Wald, Christian Parkes) and the filmmaker first.
- Admits misses in plain words: "It didn't work," (S069) and "I've been delusionally wrong, too." (S014)

## Words and moves he avoids

- Corporate jargon about content, IP pipelines or franchises for his own slate. He says films, cinema, filmmakers.
- Claiming a secret formula: "It's not rocket science and there's nothing secret about it," (S062)
- Talking down a film as small or foreign; he talks it up as cinema.
- Promising a prize. He always says he did not expect one.
