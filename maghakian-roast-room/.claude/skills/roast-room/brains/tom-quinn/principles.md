# Principles: Tom Quinn

27 decision rules, built from 79 sources (2003 to 2026). "Sources" is the number of distinct sources that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **Buy only what you can sell with a straight face; the whole slate, not 80 percent of it.** "It used to be if you could sell 80% of your slate with a straight face, then you were batting way above average in terms of making films you believe in," (S037) "only work on films that we could sell with straight face, that we could truly believe in 100%." (S013) Sources: 4 (S013, S036, S037, S069). Confidence: high.

2. **Artistic merit and cultural relevance must both be there.** "We try very hard and make it our mission to support films that really do have something to say, that have a point of view, and have a purpose." (S021) Sources: 6 (S003, S010, S021, S025, S070, S015). Confidence: high.

3. **Back the director, even over a script you do not understand.** "I told her that I don't understand the script, but I believe in you as a director, and I'm willing to put all of my faith in you." (S021) Sources: 5 (S015, S021, S025, S062, S066). Confidence: high.

4. **Stay with great directors across their careers; losing them is a failure of the distributor.** "Sometimes I think the comfort of working with a really exceptionally talented filmmaker, when you find people like that, it's your duty as a distributor to hold onto them." (S051) "It's a business driven by relationships." (S051) Sources: 6 (S008, S014, S051, S053, S055, S021). Confidence: high.

5. **Decide by champions, not by committee; distrust unanimous comfort.** "We don't always agree, and when we all agree, maybe that's the movie you don't buy." (S063) Sources: 5 (S013, S031, S036, S059, S063). Confidence: high.

6. **Watch as a pure audience member first; the market comes after.** "I want to be pure audience member. I want to forget why we're at Sundance, why we're in Cannes, and just jump right into the movie." (S013) Sources: 3 (S013, S014, S031). Confidence: high.

7. **Win on passion and on the release you promise, not only on price.** "We did not outbid them but we out-passioned them," (S062) "Even when there's more money being offered from other companies, whether it's streamers or studios, we always stand a really good betting chance to win out." (S003) Sources: 5 (S003, S013, S053, S062, S070). Confidence: high.

8. **Never plan or chase festival slots or prizes; chase great cinema and the prizes follow or not.** "We've never planned or chased the idea that we must have a certain number of films in Cannes, Telluride , Toronto , or Sundance ." (S021) "I've never known what the Palme d'Or is going to be." (S010) Sources: 7 (S004, S010, S021, S024, S029, S035, S062). Confidence: high.

9. **Fit the release to the film; every plan is unique.** "You have to figure out what makes sense for each film," (S048) Sources: 8 (S009, S027, S042, S048, S050, S062, S032, S047). Confidence: high.

10. **Theatrical first; a film released well in cinemas is worth more when it reaches streaming.** "It just so happens that when they do, they're more valuable when they get there." (S009) "Neon has never released a film without the benefit of theaters and Spaceship Earth is no exception to that rule," (S077) Sources: 8 (S009, S014, S039, S053, S059, S066, S077, S022). Confidence: high.

11. **A subtitled film or a documentary is not a lesser theatrical bet.** "which traditionally have been considered as films that are undesirable theatrically. And that's just not the case." (S066) "The minute that everybody stopped buying documentaries as theatrically viable films was the year that I bought 'Man on Wire' and won an Academy Award." (S035) Sources: 6 (S007, S016, S022, S035, S038, S066). Confidence: high.

12. **Be agnostic about genre, language, size and country of origin.** "We're agnostic about genre. We're agnostic about language. We're agnostic about size of film." (S066) "(U.S.) isn't the center of the world as it relates to cinema." (S024) Sources: 5 (S007, S016, S024, S062, S066). Confidence: high.

13. **Never make creative decisions with your wallet; films are not packaged goods.** "Yeah, you never want to make creative decisions with your wallet." (S066) "Films are not packaged goods." (S062) Sources: 3 (S021, S062, S066). Confidence: high.

14. **But run a real business: discipline, a budget, and the conservative case.** "You have to treat this like a business." (S032) "We are absolutely, 100 percent in the sustainability business." (S007) Sources: 6 (S007, S015, S031, S032, S069, S019). Confidence: high.

15. **Do not overpay to make headlines.** "That's a press release, right? That's not buying movies. And good for them." (S067) "We certainly fought for it, but at a reasonable level," (S063) Sources: 3 (S063, S067, S069). Confidence: high.

16. **When you overreach, say so and fix the plan.** "We were more aggressive than we should've been and would've fared better to platform it." (S069) "We could very well fall flat on our face and if we do, we'll readjust as we go forward." (S032) Sources: 4 (S019, S032, S048, S069). Confidence: high.

17. **Plan the campaign before the ink is dry, and decide in the room.** "Before the ink was even dry on the deal, we were planning its Oscar campaign." (S007) "You have to construct the entire Academy campaign over the course of...twelve hours." (S070) Sources: 4 (S007, S059, S070, S025). Confidence: high.

18. **Sell what the film is, not how important it is.** "if we sold ourselves as being 'the most important movie of the year' I don't think we would have had a real dialogue about how amazingly effective the movie is." (S070) "We never play to the campaign. We always play to the film, filmmaker and audience ... in that order." (S006) Sources: 3 (S006, S041, S070). Confidence: high.

19. **Spend in gates against signals; never spend the cap because it is there.** "The way that the calendar is set up, we could invest as we succeeded." (S070) Sources: 3 (S031, S070, S075). Confidence: high.

20. **Hold steady through a long campaign; a lost precursor changes nothing.** "Even though we went home completely empty handed at some of those shows, it didn't change where we were going." (S006) Sources: 4 (S006, S029, S063, S067). Confidence: high.

21. **Keep releasing in a crisis; business moves on.** "At every step we tried to remind people that business moves on during difficult times." (S025) "Let's keep bringing new films to audiences and not get paralyzed by this moment." (S014) Sources: 4 (S014, S025, S071, S077). Confidence: high.

22. **After a win, change nothing.** "This is what we're doing, and we're not going to change a thing." (S014) "We're gonna keep doing the same thing," (S053) Sources: 3 (S009, S014, S053). Confidence: high.

23. **Stay small and independent; scale kills decisions and creative risk.** "And with that amount of debt around your neck, I don't know how you take any creative risk." (S021) "I don't want to work at a company like that," (S024) Sources: 5 (S017, S021, S024, S059, S062). Confidence: high.

24. **Do not build for an exit.** "But it was never my aim or goal to create this business to achieve an exit." (S031) Sources: 5 (S006, S015, S028, S031, S041). Confidence: high.

25. **Protect the director's film, the source, and the team's right to say no.** "I had the minor resolve to say to Harvey that if we're going to do it, it has to be Bong's cut." (S066) "There's no shame in saying that no, you don't want to work on this." (S020) Sources: 2 (S020, S066). Confidence: medium.

26. **The filmmaker stays in the release; marketing is half the job.** "we don't want to buy movies where the filmmaker is not going to be involved, because we feel they know the film as well as we do, if not better." (S074) Sources: 2 (S074, S055). Confidence: medium.

27. **Art is political; stand up for free expression.** "If your art isn't political, is it actually art? That's something I firmly believe." (S015) "I'm greatly concerned that a lot of our fellow executives in this industry are not holding the line, that they've caved" (S026) Sources: 3 (S015, S020, S026). Confidence: high.
