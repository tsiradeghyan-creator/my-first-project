# Books: Tom Quinn

Tom Quinn has written no books. His thinking reaches the public through interviews and stage talks, so this file lists the recurring bodies of work the brain is built from instead.

- **Festival and summit stage talks (2013 to 2026).** Cannes American Pavilion and Marche du Film panels (2013, 2024), IndieWire Screen Talk live at NYFF and Cannes (2021, 2023, 2025), CinemaCon (2019), the Zurich Summit Game Changer talk and speech (2025), the Produced By Conference and the SXSW London keynote (2026). Core ideas: Cannes as the beacon, the Palme as a ticket driver, no groupthink, no ego marketing, a cap on size, the algorithm as the enemy of taste. Sources: S011, S013, S015, S016, S023, S024, S026, S031, S033, S035, S036, S045, S048, S049, S067.
- **The Radius-TWC interviews (2011 to 2015).** Deadline, THR, TheWrap and IndieWire Q&As on multiplatform release, premium VOD as a first window, reporting VOD grosses, the dead zone between platform and wide, and protecting Snowpiercer and Citizenfour. Sources: S018, S019, S020, S032, S042, S050, S051.
- **The Parasite record (2019 to 2025).** Variety's conversation with Bong Joon Ho, IndieWire's Oscar-night Q&A, THR's Awards Chatter page and the 2025 oral history. Core ideas: follow the director, the Rugoff release of Z as a model, sell subtitled cinema as cinema, spend-to-gross discipline. Sources: S008, S009, S040, S053, S054, S055, S065, S066.
- **The Neon-building interviews (2017 to 2026).** Variety on the decalogue, No Film School's I, Tonya case study, Deadline on the pandemic model, THR on the banner year, The Ankler, The Ringer, Creative Screenwriting, Carolina Alumni Review. Core ideas: the mantra, straight-face slates, the amoeba, the conservative model, Jiro's subway counter. Sources: S003, S004, S007, S014, S021, S025, S037, S059, S070.
- **The Goldwyn years (2003).** The Independent's Q&A: specialised film as 600 prints and under, no quota, filmmakers involved in the release, what makes a film sell at a festival. Source: S074.
