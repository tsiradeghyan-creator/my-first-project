| Day | Slot | Tactic (Doctrine) | Goal | Hook category |

## 📅 Daily Posting Schedule (Detailed)

### Monday — Iconic Line Reveal (Reel)
**Format**: Reel, 20-30 sec
**Construction**:
- Hook: «Հիշո՞ւմ ես այս phrase-ը» (HOOKS_LIBRARY A.1)
- Visual: Script page close-up + lamp light (REELS_EDITING + IMAGE_PROMPTS Template 3)
- Voice: Personal Maghakian, reveal 4-rewrite backstory
- CTA: Comment bait #12 — «Ո՞ր line-ն է քո սիրելին»
- Hashtags: HASHTAGS Set 1 (Project showcase)

### Tuesday — Writers' Room BTS (Carousel)
**Format**: 5-slide carousel
**Construction**:
- Slide 1: Hook + writers' room scene image (IMAGE_PROMPTS Template 7)
- Slides 2-4: Process explanation (FRAMEWORKS — Pixar or BAB)
- Slide 5: CTA — Save (CTA_LIBRARY #1)
- Voice: Personal Maghakian (mentor mode)
- Hashtags: Set 2 (BTS)

### Wednesday — Deleted Scene OR Character Confession (Reel)
**Format**: Reel, 25-40 sec
**Cadence**: Alternate weeks (Wk 1 = Deleted, Wk 2 = Character, Wk 3 = Deleted, Wk 4 = Character)
**Construction**:
- Hook: Curiosity Gap (HOOKS B.3)
- Pattern Interrupt: #5 (wrong-color) or #10 (mistake reveal)
- Visual: IMAGE_PROMPTS Template 4 or 5
- CTA: DM trigger #28 («ԿԱՐԴԱ») or Save #2
- Hashtags: Set 1 or 2

### Thursday — Numbers OR Cross-Project Resurrection (Carousel/Reel)
**Format**: Alternate – Wk 1 = Carousel (Numbers), Wk 2 = Reel (Cross-Project), Wk 3 = Carousel, Wk 4 = Reel
**Construction**:
- Hook: Authority / Nostalgia
- Visual: IMAGE_PROMPTS Template 2 (Numbers) or BTS combo
- Framework: AIDA (for numbers) or Pixar (for nostalgia)
- CTA: Follow #19 or Save #1
- Hashtags: Set 1 mostly

### Friday — Cliffhanger Carousel (7 slides, kicks off weekend)
**Format**: 7-slide carousel
**Construction**:
- Slide 1: Mystery hook
- Slides 2-6: Build-up (no reveal yet)
- Slide 7: Partial reveal + «Մաս 2՝ վաղը»
- Visual: IMAGE_PROMPTS Template 6 (per slide)
- CTA: Profile visit + Follow combined
- Saturday gets the «Մաս 2»

### Saturday — Predict-Reveal Part 1 + Cliffhanger Part 2
**Two posts**:
1. Continue Friday's carousel (Part 2) — reel/single post at 11:00
2. New Predict post — at 18:00:
   - Hook: «Կռահեք – ո՞ր ժանր/character/scene»
   - Engagement mechanic #6 (Predictions)
   - CTA: Comment bait + reveal next Wednesday

### Sunday — Personal Diary (Single post)
**Format**: Single post (image + caption) OR short reel (15 sec)
**Construction**:
- First-person Maghakian voice
- Vulnerability / craft confession
- Image: writer at desk, late night, lamp glow
- No CTA OR soft Save
- Hashtags: Set 3 (Educational/Behind-the-scenes mix)

## 🚀 14-Day Series Launch Playbook

Use when launching a new project (Hotel Grand 4, new series, etc.)։

### Pre-Launch (Days 1-7)

#### Day 1 — Mystery Tease
- **Format**: Reel
- **Content**: Cold open scene fragment, no context
- **Visual**: Single moody image, IMAGE_PROMPTS Template 6
- **Hook**: «Մի բան գալիս է...»
- **No project name**

#### Day 2 — Universe Connection
- **Format**: Carousel
- **Content**: Easter egg from previous Maghakian projects + hint at new
- **Tactic**: T10 (Universe Map)

#### Day 3 — Writers' Room Sneak Peek
- **Format**: Story sequence (7 stories)
- **Content**: Script page close-ups, post-it notes, coffee, breakdown bulletin

#### Day 4 — Character Tease
- **Format**: Reel
- **Content**: Character silhouette + monologue line, no name
- **Visual**: IMAGE_PROMPTS Template 5

#### Day 5 — «Engineer's» Cut
- **Format**: Reel
- **Content**: Script page with red-pen edits, voice-over of process
- **Tactic**: T9 (Show vs Reality split)

#### Day 6 — Big Question
- **Format**: Carousel + Stories
- **Content**: «Ո՞վ է [character name silhouette]» – ask audience to predict
- **Engagement mechanic**: #6 (Predictions)

#### Day 7 — Title Reveal
- **Format**: Carousel
- **Content**: Title in gold serif on black + tagline
- **Visual**: IMAGE_PROMPTS Template 2
- **CTA**: Save + Follow for premiere details

### Launch Week (Days 8-14)

#### Day 8 — Premiere Date + Cast Reveal
- **Format**: Carousel, tag all cast
- **Engagement mechanic**: #13 (Cast Tag Reveal)
- **CTA**: Save reminder

#### Day 9 — Director / Writer Statement
- **Format**: Reel, talking head OR voice-over
- **Voice**: Personal Maghakian
- **Content**: «Ինչու գրեցինք այս series-ը»

#### Day 10 — Behind-the-Scenes Carousel
- **Format**: 5-slide carousel
- **Content**: Set photos, script pages, casting decisions

#### Day 11 — Character Spotlight (Lead character)
- **Format**: Reel
- **Tactic**: T4 (Character Confession)

#### Day 12 — Iconic Line Tease
- **Format**: Reel
- **Tactic**: T2 (Iconic Line - upcoming)
- **CTA**: «Տես premiere-ին այս phrase-ը»

#### Day 13 — Final Countdown
- **Format**: Story countdown sticker + Reel hype
- **CTA**: «Մեկ օր մնաց»

#### Day 14 — Premiere Day
- **Format**: Story takeover throughout day
- **Plus**: Reel at premiere time with live-watch CTA
- **Engagement mechanic**: #7 (Live AMA post-premiere)

### Post-Launch (Days 15+)
- Cliffhanger carousels per episode
- Character Confessions weekly
- Deleted scenes monthly
