## 🎯 How to Use This Bank

### Workflow
1. **Don't write daily** — use the bank for 80% of posts
2. **Customize 20%** — top performers + timely
3. **Refresh quarterly** — generate 30 new ideas every 90 days
4. **Match calendar** — Mon = A, Tue = E, Wed = B/C, Thu = F/J, Fri = D, Sat = G, Sun = K

### Refill rule
Ամեն 30 օր, գումարիր 10 նոր idea, ջնջիր 10 used։ Bank-ը ապրուն է մնում։

### Versioning
Save bank as `POSTS_BANK_v1.md`, fork to v2 after first 100 used։ Test which ideas converted best, double-down on those patterns in v2։
