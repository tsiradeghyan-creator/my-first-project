# REELS_EDITING.md – Cinematic Reels Editing Rules

Reels-ը Maghakian-ի #1 amplification channel է (5,260% reach/follower ratio)։ Editing-ը պետք է լինի *cinematic*, ոչ trendy։

## Core Specs

| Parameter | Default | Range |
|-----------|---------|-------|
| Aspect ratio | 9:16 vertical | Always |
| Duration | 15-30 sec | 7-90 sec |
| Frame rate | 30 fps | 24 (cinematic) - 60 (smooth) |
| Resolution | 1080×1920 min | 4K downscale OK |
| Color grade | Warm cinematic | Maghakian palette |
| Audio | Low-volume background | Or original VO |

---

## 🎬 The 7-Second Rule

Hook-ի առաջին 7 վրկ-ը որոշում է, թե reel-ը կդիտվի լրիվ։ Algorithm-ի retention curve-ը decays exponentially after second 7։

### Allocate
- **0-0.5 sec**: Visual + text hook simultaneously
- **0.5-2 sec**: Audio hook begins
- **2-7 sec**: Reveal teaser (don't pay off yet)
- **7-20 sec**: Build / story / value
- **20-25 sec**: Payoff
- **25-30 sec**: CTA + loop-ready ending

## 🎨 Maghakian Visual Identity

### Color Grade
- **Shadows**: Deep, slightly warm black (`#0a0805`)
- **Midtones**: Warm amber tint
- **Highlights**: Slight gold push (`#d4a857` tint)
- **Saturation**: -10 from default (cinematic restraint)
- **Contrast**: +15 (filmic look)

### B-roll Library (default)
- Script pages (close-up, page turns, ink writing)
- Vintage typewriter (keys, paper feed)
- Coffee mug, ashtray, lamp
- Pen close-ups
- Handwritten notes
- Desk close-ups (wood texture)
- Open notebook
- Single hanging bulb

### Lighting
- **Primary**: Single warm tungsten source (~2700K)
- **Falloff**: Strong, deep shadows
- **Atmosphere**: Smoke/haze for volumetric light
- **Avoid**: Flat lighting, ring lights, daylight

## ✂️ Cut Rhythm

### Default rule
- **Cut every 1-3 seconds** maintaining retention
- **Hold longer (4-6 sec)** for emotional/intimate moments
- **Faster (0.5-1 sec)** for energy spikes

### Cut types
- **Match cut** – same action across two angles
- **L-cut** – audio continues over visual change
- **J-cut** – next audio starts before visual change
- **Hard cut** – clean break (default)
- **Cross-dissolve** – emotional transitions (use sparingly)

## 📝 Caption Layer (On-Screen Text)

### 85% rule
85% reels-ը դիտվում է without sound։ Captions are non-negotiable։

### Caption format
- **Font**: Bold sans-serif Armenian (Mariam Sans, GHEA Aspet Sans)
- **Size**: Large – 6-10% of frame height
- **Position**: Lower-third or center
- **Background**: Semi-transparent black bar OR drop-shadow
- **Color**: White primary, gold accent for emphasis words

### Caption rhythm
- Sync with speech (or beat to audio if no VO)
- Break into 3-5 word chunks
- Emphasis word gets larger / gold
- Avoid full paragraph dump on-screen

### Auto-caption tools
- Instagram native captions (decent Armenian support)
- CapCut auto-captions (best Armenian accuracy)
- Premiere Pro speech-to-text (Armenian: manual cleanup needed)

---

## 🎵 Audio Strategy

### 3 audio layer options
1. **Pure VO** (Maghakian voice) – cinematic, authority
2. **Trending audio + VO** (audio low ~10-15%, VO primary)
3. **Trending audio only** (rare, only for visual-pure reels)

### Trending audio rules
- Check «trending sounds» tab in Reels Explore weekly
- Use audio that has 1K-10K reels using it (sweet spot)
- Avoid audio with >100K uses (saturation)
- Match audio mood to content tone

### Original audio
Whenever possible, upload original audio – future creators can use it → brand multiplier.

## 🔁 Loop-Ready Endings

### Why loop matters
Algorithm counts re-watches as views։ If ending → seamlessly loops to beginning → completion rate doubles, sometimes triples։

### How to create loop
- Last frame visually matches first frame
- Last audio beat matches first audio beat
- Or: end with question that beginning «answers»

### Example
- Open: «Հիշո՞ւմ ես այս phrase-ը»
- End: «Հաջորդ post-ում - ո՞ր scene-ն» → cuts to open
- Loop: viewer re-watches «Հիշո՞ւմ ես» on second pass

## 🎭 Hook On-Screen Text Templates

| Hook category | Text template |
|---------------|---------------|
| Curiosity | «Կա մի բան, որ ոչ ոք չտեսավ» |
| Numbers | «80,000 էջ» (massive) |
| Question | «Քանի՞ տարեկան էիր երբ...» |
| Reveal | «Հիմա կասեմ ճշմարտությունը» |
| Pattern | «3 բան, որ...» |

Hook text-ը պետք է լինի **standalone-meaningful** – mute-ով հանդիսատեսը պետք է հասկանա։

## 🎬 Shot Composition (Maghakian Lookbook)

### Composition rules
1. **Negative space** – brand identity, ոչ stuffed frame
2. **Rule of thirds** – default
3. **Center frame** – for hero moments (script close-up, single object)
4. **Symmetry** – for «universe» type reveals
5. **Lead room** – when subject looks off-screen

### Avoid
- Wide-open daylight outdoor scenes (off-brand)
- Bright office settings
- Group selfie energy
- Phone-held vertical with no setup

### Mobile-first

### Desktop / pro

- **CapCut auto-cut** – decent first pass

## 🚫 Anti-Patterns

❌ TikTok watermarked content cross-posted – algorithm downranks
❌ Vertical phone selfie talking head (off-brand)
❌ Bright ring-light shots
❌ Generic «stretched» horizontal video to vertical
❌ Stock footage opens (no Maghakian aesthetic)
❌ Multiple aspect ratios in same reel
❌ Audio peaking / clipping
❌ Tiny captions <5% frame height
❌ No CTA at end
❌ Hard cut to black with no callback
❌ Fast strobing cuts (no purpose, accessibility issue)

## 📊 Reel Performance Diagnostic

After publishing, check Insights → Reel performance։

| Metric | Healthy | Excellent |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| Completion rate | 50%+ | 70%+ |
| Re-watch rate | 1x | 1.5x+ |
| Average watch time | 60% duration | 80%+ |
| Reach / follower | 50-200% | 500%+ |

### Underperformer diagnosis
- Low completion → hook too slow, fix 0-7 sec
- Low reach → bad hook copy, or audio too saturated
- High completion, low engagement → no CTA, no payoff
- Low save rate → not specific/useful enough

## 🎯 Maghakian Reel Checklist (Before Publishing)

- ☐ 9:16, 30fps minimum
- ☐ 7-second rule respected (visual + text + audio in 0.5 sec)
- ☐ Maghakian color grade applied
- ☐ Captions on screen, Armenian-sharp, no broken letters
- ☐ Cut rhythm 1-3 sec
- ☐ Audio low-volume background OR original VO
- ☐ Loop-ready ending
- ☐ CTA visible last 3 seconds
- ☐ Hook text standalone-readable
- ☐ Brand DNA visible (script pages, lamp, gold, deep black)
