# BROADCAST_CHANNEL.md – Maghakian's Inner Circle Channel

🎯 **Activation strategy** (once at 5,000+ followers — strong algorithm signal for the main account too)

## What Are Broadcast Channels?

Broadcast Channel = Instagram-ի opt-in one-to-many DM-like channel։ Հանդիսատեսը subscribe-ով join է լինում, դու push-ում ես text/media/polls directly նրանց inbox-ին։

Different from:
- **Close Friends Stories** (curated list, you pick)
- **Regular DM** (one-to-one)
- **Stories** (24-hour public)

## 🔑 Why This Matters for @maghakianscripts

1. **Algorithm boost**: Active Broadcast Channel = signal of engaged audience → boosts feed reach
2. **1.5B+ messages/month** = mainstream surface
3. **Direct-to-inbox delivery** = no algorithm filter to bypass
4. **Premium «backstage» feel** = converts casual followers to superfans
5. **Maghakian's natural advantage**: scriptwriter «inside the writers' room» framing maps perfectly to broadcast channel format

## 📐 Channel Architecture

### Recommended channel name
**«Maghakian Writers' Room»** կամ **«Անիի Սցենարային սենյակը»**

### Channel description (250 chars max)
```
Writers' room-ից ուղիղ ձեզ։
Cut scenes, character backstory, scripting tips, premiere alerts.
Դուռը բաց է — բայց միայն հետաքրքրասերների համար։

— Անի Մաղաքյան & Maghakian Scripts
```

### Frequency
- **3-5 messages per week** (sweet spot — daily fatigues, weekly feels neglected)
- **Mix**: 40% exclusive content, 30% interaction (polls/questions), 20% premiere/news, 10% personal

## 📝 Content Pillars for Broadcast Channel

### 1. **Writers' Room Drops** (exclusive)
- Script page snippets (handwritten edits)
- «Today I'm working on...» moments
- Cut scenes shared as text excerpts

### 2. **Polls That Shape Content**
- «Հաջորդ post-ը — Hotel Grand-ի մասին, թե Բեկորների»
- «Ո՞ր էջի scene-ն ուզում եք իմանաք» (with 3-4 specific options)
- **Real-time content collaboration** with subscribers

### 3. **First-Access Premieres**
- Project announcements 24h before public feed
- «Subscribers see it first» principle
- Creates anticipation that public follows

### 4. **Personal Ani Notes**
- Quick voice memos (audio share)
- Reflections from current project
- Honest doubts and breakthroughs

### 5. **Recommendation Drops**
- «I'm watching X right now and learning Y»
- Book recommendations
- Industry articles
- = Authority + Liking layer

## 🎯 Channel Promotion (Cross-Surface)

### How to get subscribers
1. **Story sticker**: «Join my Broadcast Channel» (drives 80%+ of subscribers)
2. **Pinned post**: «Want backstage access? Join channel ↓»
3. **Bio link**: dedicated URL
4. **End of reels**: «For deeper content, join the writers' room channel»
5. **DM auto-response**: include channel join link

## 📊 Performance Tracking

| Metric | Healthy | Excellent |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| Subscriber growth (monthly) | 5-10% of followers | 15%+ |
| Message read rate | 40-60% | 70%+ |
| Reply rate to polls | 5-15% | 20%+ |
| Subscriber → premium customer | 1-3% | 5%+ |

## 🚫 Anti-Patterns

❌ Treating channel like another feed (post-then-forget)
❌ Sending sales pitches with high frequency
❌ Ignoring polls/questions from subscribers
❌ Over-posting (>1/day) → unsubscribe spike
❌ Under-posting (<1/week) → forgotten
❌ Cross-posting same content as feed (no exclusivity = no value)
❌ Treating subscribers as «leads» (relational > transactional)
