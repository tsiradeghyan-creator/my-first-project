## 🎯 Pinned Posts (3 slots)

Instagram-ը թույլ է տալիս 3 pinned posts։ Սրանք պետք է լինեն conversion-optimized.

### Slot 1 — Best Hook (drives reach)
- Choose top-performing reel (current: «Քանի՞ տարեկան...»)
- Sets the tone for new visitors

### Slot 2 — Brand Anchor (drives understanding)
- «47 Projects Universe Map» (Tactic 10 carousel)
- Or «Ո՞վ ենք մենք» — agency intro carousel
- Establishes credibility within 5 seconds

### Slot 3 — Lead Magnet (drives DM)
- DM trigger post («Գրիր ՍՑԵՆԱՐ ↓»)
- Or pinned «How to commission a script»

### Rotation rule
Quarterly review: is each pin still earning its slot? Swap if not.

## 📊 Conversion Funnel

Trace the path:

```
Reach (reel viewer)
   ↓ (5-15% click profile)
Profile visit
   ↓ (3-8% follow OR DM)
Follower / DM lead
   ↓ (1-3% convert to commission inquiry)
Lead
   ↓ (10-30% become client)
Client
```

### Current bottleneck (per METRICS.md)
**Reach → Profile visit** ratio is leaking։ Hooks land reach but profile doesn't have the «click» moment.

### Fixes (priority order)
1. **Pin best reel** (sets first impression)
2. **Bio rewrite** (clarifies value within 3 seconds)
3. **Highlights designed as posters** (not random)
4. **Story CTAs to profile** (story #6 in launch sequence per CONTENT_CALENDAR)
5. **Comment-to-follow conversion** (reply to every comment with subtle re-pitch)

## 🧪 Profile Audit Checklist

Quarterly check:

- ☐ Bio < 150 characters, value clear in 3 seconds
- ☐ Link in bio works, points to lead magnet
- ☐ Profile picture recognizable as thumbnail (32x32 px)
- ☐ 3 pinned posts current and on-strategy
- ☐ 9 highlights consistent design
- ☐ All highlights have content (no empty/abandoned)
- ☐ Highlights ordered by user journey (most important first)
- ☐ Account category = "Public Figure" or "Production Company"
- ☐ Action buttons enabled (Email, Call, DM)
- ☐ "Add yours" sticker prompts active (if used)

## ⚠️ Anti-Patterns

❌ Bio in Russian when audience is Armenian-primary
❌ Generic "🎬✨ Storytellers ✨🎬" emoji-heavy bio
❌ Link in bio = personal Instagram URL (waste)
❌ Inconsistent Highlight covers (each different style)
❌ Pinned post older than 6 months
❌ Profile picture too detailed for thumbnail
❌ Bio with no specific value prop
❌ 15+ highlights (overwhelming, user won't scroll)
