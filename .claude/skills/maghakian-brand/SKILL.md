# Maghakian Brand

This is the concise entrypoint. The older full playbook is preserved in `references/full-guide.md` and must be loaded only when this skill is actually routed.

## Required Reference

- `references/full-guide.md`

## Operating Contract

- Read `references/full-guide.md` before creating brand output.
- Read `portfolio.md` before any credential, filmography, episode-count, career-scope, or project-specific claim.
- Write Armenian only in proper Eastern Armenian Unicode.
- Keep the brand voice literary, warm, confident, and precise.

## Output Contract

- The requested brand asset or copy in the user's language.
- Any source assumption that affects dates, titles, totals, or claims.
- Platform-specific formatting when the target channel is known.

## Verification

- Check portfolio claims against `portfolio.md`.
- Check colors, typography, logo usage, and tone against `references/full-guide.md`.
- For current public facts, launches, or platform requirements, verify before presenting them as current.

## Safety Boundaries

- Get explicit approval before spending credits, publishing, uploading private media, or changing account/platform state.
- Do not use private likeness, voice, protected identity, or rights-sensitive material without clear authorization.
- Do not delete, overwrite, or move user files unless explicitly asked.
- Treat model names, platform features, prices, schedules, and public claims as time-sensitive.
