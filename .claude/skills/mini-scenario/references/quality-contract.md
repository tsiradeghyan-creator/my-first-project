# Mini Scenario Quality Contract

Use this reference when auditing, rewriting, or completing an Instagram serial plan.

## Output Contract

A complete `/setup` output must include:

- niche,
- one-sentence logline,
- one or two character cards,
- serial type,
- base visual style,
- target audience.

A complete `/basic` output must include:

- one-line format diagnosis,
- episode skeleton,
- 8-12 episode two-week plan,
- episode 1 script with hook, steps, mini-climax, next-episode teaser, CTA,
- 30-minute production checklist,
- 3 season KPIs,
- compact serial passport.

A complete `/pro` output must include:

- expanded serial passport,
- season map,
- episode table,
- two-week calendar,
- visual mini-bible,
- 6-8 episode A/B plan,
- crisis guide.

## Verification

Before finishing, check:

- The user has one concrete action for today and one for tomorrow.
- The first episode has a 0-3 second hook.
- The chosen cadence is realistic for the stated resources.
- KPI targets match the stated goal: reach, leads, sales, retention, or education.
- Rights-sensitive items are flagged, not silently approved.
- If Armenian text appears, it is proper Eastern Armenian Unicode.

## Safety Boundaries

- Do not recommend using copyrighted music, celebrity faces, brand logos, private
  locations, minors, medical/financial/legal claims, or paid promotion without
  explicit user approval and a rights/compliance note.
- Do not promise virality. Give testable hypotheses and A/B plans.
- Do not publish, schedule, buy ads, create accounts, or contact people.
- When the user asks for a personal/brand serial, distinguish authorial voice from
  visual identity and ask before locking a brand system.

## World-Class Upgrade Standard

Compared with generic content-plan prompts, this skill should win on:

- serial architecture, not isolated post ideas,
- character continuity and repeatable rituals,
- platform-native retention mechanics,
- measurable test plan,
- realistic production load,
- explicit rights and safety boundaries.
