**Extend Video**

*Specific instructions:*

**I. Basic Operation Steps**

- Select the target video: In the video generation stream, locate the video you want to extend, and click the "Extend Video" icon below the video.
  - ⚠️ Note: Only original videos with a duration of **less than 30 seconds** are eligible for this feature.

- To set the extended duration, tap the "Clock" icon on the screen, and you can **swipe the timeline** or **enter a value directly** to select the desired extension time (e. g., select 5s).

- Enter the prompt in the text box, and input specific scene descriptions or action requirements for the newly added extended part.
  - *Example: If the original video is 1 0 seconds long and you choose to extend it by 5 seconds, the entered prompt (e. g., " the male character lands and looks at the camera after defeating the opponent ") will only apply to the content of the last 5 seconds, while the first 1 0 seconds will completely retain the original video footage.*

- Click to generate (release train). After confirming that the duration and prompt words are correct, click the generate button on the right to get the complete video with the original duration plus the extended time (for example, 1 0 seconds + 5 seconds = 15 seconds of video).

**II. Advanced Usage: "Nested" Extension**

This feature supports multiple consecutive "nesting-style" extension operations:

- **Continue extending**: as long as the total duration of the newly generated video output **is still within 30 seconds**, you can click the "Extend Video" button below it again to perform **a second or multiple extensions**.

- **Demo**: For the just-generated 15 -second video, select to extend it by another 5 seconds, and enter a new prompt (e. g., " The male character smiles at the camera and waves to greet "), after generation, you will get a continuous video of 20 seconds long.

**III. Explanation of Maximum Duration**

- As long as the "base footage (original video)" used for extension is within 30 seconds, a single operation supports **4 to 30 seconds** of video extension.

- **In the extreme case**: you can take an original 30-second video, directly choose to extend it by 30 seconds, and finally produce an ultra-long video of up to **60 seconds** in one go.
