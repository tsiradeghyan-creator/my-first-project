# Treatment and pitch deck: structure and checklist

What buyers (platforms, channels, producers) look for in the first 3 pages: what it is, why it sells, why us.
Write each section in the language of the buyer (hy / ru / en). Facts about the studio come from
`maghakian-roast-room/business.md`; never invent numbers, ratings or partners.

## Series pitch deck (default for selling scripts), 10–14 pages
1. **Cover**: title, format line (genre · N episodes × minutes), logline, key image.
2. **Why now**: the hook for this buyer: audience gap, trend, timing, a pull quote.
3. **Logline + premise**: one sentence + one paragraph of the world and the engine of the show.
4. **Synopsis**: pilot in 1–2 paragraphs, then the season in 1–2 paragraphs. Spoil the ending.
5. **Characters**: 3–6 cards: name, age, want vs need, the wound, one telling line. Face reference image.
6. **Season arc**: episode list, one line each (or a table): what changes per episode.
7. **Tone and look**: 2–4 comps ("X meets Y"), mood images, colour, music. Use film-crew style cards.
8. **Format and production**: episode count/length, main locations (count them: from the breakdown),
   cast size, shooting days estimate, budget band if asked. Fewer locations = an argument.
9. **Audience and platform fit**: who watches, where, why this buyer.
10. **Future**: season 2 hook, spin-offs, international/adaptation potential.
11. **Team**: Maghakian Scripts track record (48 projects, 2,300+ episodes, since 2016, partners),
    Ani Maghakyan (IMDb nm9250160), Tigran as producer. Turnkey production offer if relevant.
12. **Contact**.

## Feature film: same, but replace 6 with a three-act outline (setup / confrontation / resolution, 1 paragraph each)
and add a short director's or writer's statement.

## Children's (books, animation 0–16)
Age band, learning or emotional value for parents, main character design, sample spread or episode,
series potential (books → animation → merch), Armenian-original angle.

## Classic written treatment (`--layout doc`)
Present tense, 3–10 pages, prose: title page, logline, then the story told in sequence with key scenes,
no camera directions, a few lines of dialogue at most.

## Gap audit (run before building any deck from an existing document)
Mark each item ✅ / ⚠️ weak / ❌ missing and tell Tigran before writing:
logline in one sentence · format line · why now · synopsis with an ending · characters with want/need ·
season arc · tone + comps · production scale (locations, cast) · audience/platform fit · team · contact.
(The RU deck «Влюбись в меня снова» was missing synopsis, characters, season arc, why now and tone.)
