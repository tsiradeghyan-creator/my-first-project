# Wong Kar-wai — style card

**Signature in one line:** longing in confined, neon- or lamp-lit spaces — smeared motion, slow motion, repeated
music and time folding back on itself.
**ASL:** mixed and elliptical; slow-motion holds alternate with jump-cut fragments · **Collaborators:** DP
Christopher Doyle; editor/production designer William Chang (context only).

## Composition & framing
- Frames within frames: doorways, corridors, curtains, mirrors, window bars; characters off-centre, crowded to the
  edge or half hidden; tight interiors.

## Lenses & format
- Wide lenses close in crowds (Chungking Express), longer lenses observing through obstructions (In the Mood for
  Love); rich grain.

## Camera movement
- Handheld with step-printed, low-shutter smear in action/crowds; slow-motion glides past characters; slow
  lateral moves along corridors.

## Light & colour
- Saturated neon and practical lamps; deep reds, greens, ambers; smoke; rain on glass.

## Production design & costume
- Cramped apartments, noodle stalls, stairways, clocks; for In the Mood for Love a changing series of cheongsams
  marks time.

## Editing
- Jump cuts, repetition of a moment, slow motion set against a repeated song, voice-over fragments, time stamps.

## Sound & music
- A recurring piece of music (a waltz or pop song) returning like a memory; rain, street noise, voice-over.

## Performance
- Restrained, glances that don't meet, gestures repeated (smoking, fixing hair), unspoken feeling.

## Use sparingly / avoid
- Step-printing everywhere becomes a filter. Use it for one emotional peak.

## AI translation
- "observed through a half-open doorway and a hanging curtain; she is framed small in the gap, off-centre"
- "slow motion as he passes her in a narrow stairway; their shoulders almost touch; neither looks"
- "handheld, low shutter, step-printed motion blur; neon reflections smear while the subject stays readable"
- "saturated red and green practical light, cigarette smoke, rain streaks on the window"
