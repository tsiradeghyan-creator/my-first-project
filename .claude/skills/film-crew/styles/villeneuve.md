# Denis Villeneuve — style card

**Signature in one line:** tiny humans before monumental architecture and landscapes, in fog and hard silhouette,
with slow pushes and enormous, low sound.
**ASL:** long, patient · **Collaborators:** DPs Roger Deakins, Greig Fraser; composers Jóhann Jóhannsson, Hans
Zimmer (context only).

## Composition & framing
- Extreme wide shots with small figures; strong geometric architecture (brutalist, monolithic); silhouettes
  against bright haze; negative space.

## Lenses & format
- Clean large-format look, wide frames for scale, long lenses compressing figures against structures.

## Camera movement
- Very slow push-ins and drifts; static observation; aerial shots that reveal scale; little handheld.

## Light & colour
- Monochromatic palettes per world (orange haze, cold grey-blue, desert beige); hard top light or backlight;
  fog/dust volume.

## Production design & costume
- Minimal, massive, functional spaces; muted, utilitarian costume with strong silhouette.

## Editing
- Measured; long silences; suspense from withholding; abrupt sound cuts.

## Sound & music
- Deep drones, sub-bass, horns, wind; silence used as pressure; dialogue sparse.

## Performance
- Quiet, internal, watchful.

## Use sparingly / avoid
- Fog and scale without a human anchor become wallpaper — always keep a figure for scale.

EWS, static then imperceptible push: a woman in a grey coat walks toward a monolithic doorway twenty times her
height through dust; the sun is a pale disc behind; only wind and a sub-bass rumble; 12 s.
