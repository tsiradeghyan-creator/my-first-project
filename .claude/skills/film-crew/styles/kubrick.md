# Stanley Kubrick — style card

**Signature in one line:** symmetrical, deep, wide-angle frames in which people look small inside systems they
cannot escape, watched by a camera that moves slowly and never blinks.
**ASL:** long (holds of 8-20 s are normal) · **Collaborators:** DPs John Alcott, Geoffrey Unsworth; Steadicam
operator Garrett Brown on The Shining (context only).

## Composition & framing
- One-point perspective: corridors, rooms, roads converging to a central vanishing point; subject centred.
- Symmetry and strong geometry (doorways, tables, rows of uniforms, carpets); people placed like pieces.
- The "Kubrick stare": head tilted down, eyes looking up at the lens, unblinking, in close-up.

## Lenses & format
- Wide angles used close (around 18-25mm and wider) for depth and slight unease; very fast lenses for candlelight
  in Barry Lyndon.

## Camera movement
- Slow, steady forward/backward tracking along corridors and trenches; low Steadicam following a child; slow
  optical zoom-outs from a detail to a painterly tableau. Moves are smooth, unhurried, relentless.

## Light & colour
- Motivated, often practical sources (candles, overhead fluorescents, light panels in walls/floors); clean,
  even, cold institutional light; saturated accent colours (red in bathrooms/rooms, orange, white).

## Production design & costume
- Clean, controlled, often vast interiors; patterns (carpets, tiles); uniforms and repetition in costume.

## Editing
- Long holds, measured rhythm, abrupt intertitles or time jumps; cuts often between symmetrical compositions.

## Sound & music
- Classical and pre-existing music as counterpoint (Strauss, Ligeti, Beethoven, Handel); long silences; loud
  ambience (machines, breathing).

## Performance
- Restrained or deliberately stylised; long, uncomfortable pauses; sudden eruptions after stillness.

## Use sparingly / avoid
- The stare and the twins-in-a-corridor image are clichés; use the method (symmetry, distance, inevitability),
  not the quotations.

## AI translation
- "symmetrical one-point-perspective composition, subject dead centre, vanishing point behind the head"
- "18mm wide-angle lens close to the subject, deep focus, slight wide-angle distortion at the frame edges"
- "Steadicam glides forward at hip height at a slow constant pace down the corridor"
- "slow optical zoom out from a close detail to a wide painterly tableau; camera position fixed"
- "even cold overhead light, clean institutional surfaces, one saturated red accent"

## Example shot
WS, 18mm, Steadicam at hip height glides down a symmetrical white corridor toward a closed red door; the
character walks ahead of us, centred, never looking back; ambience of humming ventilation; 12 s.
