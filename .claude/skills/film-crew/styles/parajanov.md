# Sergei Parajanov — style card

**Signature in one line:** the film as a sequence of living icons and illuminated manuscripts — frontal, static
tableaux in which symbolic objects and ritual gestures replace dialogue and plot.
**ASL:** very long static tableaux in The Color of Pomegranates (Sayat Nova); more mobile, colourful folk energy in
Shadows of Forgotten Ancestors (verify per film). Context: Armenian, born in Tbilisi; worked in Ukraine, Armenia and Georgia.

## Composition & framing
- Frontal, flat, planimetric staging; figures face the lens or are in strict profile, like medieval miniatures and
  icons.
- Symmetry, repetition, objects arranged as still lifes (pomegranates, books drying on a roof, lace, fish,
  daggers, carpets); frames within frames (arches, doorways, walls).

## Lenses & format
- Academy-like near-square frame in The Color of Pomegranates; deep, even focus; painterly flatness.

## Camera movement
- Almost none: the camera is still and the image changes by gesture, entrance or cut. Movement belongs to people
  and fabric (a cloth unfurling, a hand turning a page).

## Light & colour
- Soft, even, frontal light without dramatic shadows; rich symbolic colour — pomegranate red, ochre, ivory,
  indigo, gold, black; textiles and walls as colour fields.

## Production design & costume
- Armenian church architecture, monastery walls, carpets, embroidered and ceremonial costume, headdresses;
  ritual objects (candles, censers, bells); repeated symbolic props.
- One performer may play several roles (Sofiko Chiaureli plays multiple parts in Pomegranates) — doubling as style.

## Editing
- Chapters with poetic titles; cut from tableau to tableau like turning pages; repetition and visual rhymes rather
  than continuity editing; deliberate breaks of the 180° rule are irrelevant because space is flat.

## Sound & music
- Chant, liturgical music, folk instruments, bells, layered natural sounds; very little or no spoken dialogue;
  poetry read or shown as text.

## Performance
- Slow, ritual, choreographed gestures; direct gaze into the lens; stillness as presence.

## Use sparingly / avoid
- Do not fake "ethnic decoration": every object must mean something in the scene. Avoid camera moves and
  dramatic lighting that break the icon logic.
