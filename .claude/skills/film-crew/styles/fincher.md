# David Fincher — style card

**Signature in one line:** controlled, precise, quietly menacing images — the camera moves only when the actor
moves, the light is low and sickly-green/amber, and nothing in frame is accidental.
**ASL:** medium-fast, crisp · **Collaborators:** DPs Jeff Cronenweth, Erik Messerschmidt; composers Trent Reznor
& Atticus Ross (context only).

## Composition & framing
- Locked-off or perfectly smooth frames; centred or rigorously balanced; characters in clean, dark space;
  top-down inserts of procedures (typing, evidence, hands).

## Lenses & format
- Digital capture, clean image, controlled depth of field; widescreen.

## Camera movement
- Motivated micro-moves: dolly/pan exactly matching an actor's step or turn, stopping when they stop; no handheld
  wobble; occasional "impossible" glides through space (Panic Room).

## Light & colour
- Low-key, underexposed, practicals; desaturated palette leaning green-yellow (urban) or teal-amber; cool office
  fluorescence.

## Production design & costume
- Clean, functional, slightly decayed institutions; muted clothing; screens and documents as props.

## Editing
- Precise, fast, information-dense; cross-cutting procedures; cuts on small actions; no wasted frames.

## Sound & music
- Industrial/electronic textures, low pulses; detailed foley of keyboards, paper, breath.

## Performance
- Understated, many takes for naturalness; flat delivery hiding menace.

## Use sparingly / avoid
- The look is easy to fake with a green grade; the essence is control and motivation of every move.

## AI translation
- "the camera dollies exactly with his step forward and stops the instant he stops; the move is almost invisible"
- "low-key practical light, underexposed, desaturated green-yellow palette, clean digital image"
- "top-down insert of hands sorting photographs on a dark desk"
- "locked-off symmetrical medium shot, dark office, single desk lamp as key light from frame-right"

## Example shot
MS, locked-off: an investigator sits under a single desk lamp in a dark office; he leans forward 10 cm and the
camera pushes in exactly 10 cm with him, then holds; keyboard clicks, low hum; 6 s.
