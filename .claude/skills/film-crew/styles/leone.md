# Sergio Leone — style card

**Signature in one line:** the ritual standoff — extreme close-ups of eyes, hands and holsters cut against
extreme wide landscapes, stretched in time by silence and an operatic score.
**ASL:** long builds then accelerating cuts at the climax · **Collaborators:** composer Ennio Morricone; DP Tonino
Delli Colli (context only).

## Composition & framing
- Extreme close-up of eyes filling the widescreen frame; very wide landscapes with tiny figures; faces in the
  foreground with action deep in the background.

## Lenses & format
- Widescreen (Techniscope); long lenses on faces; deep focus in wides.

## Camera movement
- Slow crane rises revealing a vast space; slow pans across faces; mostly static during the standoff.

## Light & colour
- Harsh sun, dust, sweat, weathered skin; warm desert palette.

## Production design & costume
- Dusty towns, train stations, arenas; weathered leather, ponchos, dusters; strong silhouettes.

## Editing
- The standoff accelerates: cuts between eyes, hands and wide shots get shorter and shorter until the shot is
  fired; long silences before music swells.

## Sound & music
- Operatic themes with whistles, choirs, electric guitar (plan for the edit); amplified natural sounds (spurs,
  wind, flies, creaking).

## Performance
- Near-silent, faces as landscapes; minimal dialogue, maximum stare.

## Use sparingly / avoid
- Cutting eyes-hands-eyes without building geography first loses the tension. Establish the triangle wide.

## AI translation
- "extreme close-up of a man's narrowed eyes filling the widescreen frame, sweat and dust on weathered skin"
- "extreme wide shot of a circular dusty arena, three tiny figures standing far apart"
- "long lens close-up of a hand hovering above a holster, fingers twitching"
- "harsh midday sun, warm desert palette, wind and creaking leather only"

## Example shot
ECU, static, 3 s: eyes flick left. Cut to EWS, static, 5 s: the three figures in the arena, wind. Cut to insert,
2 s: a hand opens and closes. (The editor shortens each cycle as the duel approaches.)
