# Quentin Tarantino — style card

**Signature in one line:** long, funny, menacing conversations held in patient frames, then violence or a
reversal arrives as a hard cut — structured in chapters, scored with eclectic needle drops.
**ASL:** long for dialogue scenes, fast bursts for action · **Collaborators:** DP Robert Richardson (later
films), editor Sally Menke (through Inglourious Basterds), then Fred Raskin (context only).

## Composition & framing
- Low-angle "trunk shot" looking up at characters leaning into a car trunk.
- Overhead god's-eye shots; tight inserts of hands, objects, feet; wide two- and three-shots for standoffs.
- Mexican standoff geometry: several characters pointing at each other, framed so the tension reads in one shot.

## Lenses & format
- Shoots on film; widescreen anamorphic frequently (and 70mm for The Hateful Eight); saturated, filmic grain.

## Camera movement
- Slow circling track around a table during conversation; long Steadicam/dolly follows through locations;
  crash zooms and snap zooms in genre moments (Kill Bill); otherwise patient and static during talk.

## Light & colour
- Rich, saturated colour; strong primary accents (yellow, red); warm practical-motivated interiors; stylised
  colour or black-and-white switches inside a film are possible (verify per film).

## Production design & costume
- Pop-culture textures (diners, cars, posters, vintage props); iconic graphic costumes (black suits with thin ties;
  a yellow tracksuit) that read as silhouettes.

## Editing
- Non-linear structure, chapter title cards, flashbacks with labels; split screen; long dialogue holds that make
  the audience wait; sudden hard cut or smash cut into violence or consequence.

## Sound & music
- Pre-existing songs from surf rock, soul, spaghetti-western scores, 60s-70s pop; music often enters loud on a
  cut. For AI generation: no songs in the clip — plan needle drops for the edit.

## Performance
- Verbose, rhythmic dialogue about seemingly trivial things that builds threat; characters perform for each other;
  sudden tonal shifts.

## Use sparingly / avoid
- Gratuitous gore (also triggers moderation); copying famous lines. Use the structure: delay, talk, rupture.

## AI translation

- "slow continuous circling track at table height around the seated group while they talk, saturated warm diner light"
- "overhead top-down shot, the figure centred in frame"
- "sudden fast zoom in to an extreme close-up of her eyes, then hold"
- "anamorphic widescreen, 35mm film grain, saturated yellow and red accents"

## Example shot
MWS three-shot, anamorphic, three characters in a triangle each aiming at another; locked-off camera holds 10 s
of silence; then a hard cut to an overhead shot of the table after it is over.
