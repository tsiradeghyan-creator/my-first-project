# Wes Anderson — style card

**Signature in one line:** dollhouse worlds shot flat-on and symmetrical, with deadpan people, pastel palettes and
precise whip pans and lateral tracks.
**ASL:** medium-fast; brisk comic timing · **Collaborators:** DP Robert Yeoman (context only).

## Composition & framing
- Centred, symmetrical, planimetric frames (camera perpendicular to the back wall); characters face camera;
  overhead inserts of hands and objects arranged neatly.

## Lenses & format
- Wide lenses with flat staging; aspect ratio changes to mark eras (Grand Budapest Hotel) (verify per film).

## Camera movement
- Lateral tracks past rooms like a cross-section; whip pans between two characters; snap zooms; static otherwise.

## Light & colour
- Even, bright, soft light; strict pastel palettes per location (pink, mint, mustard, powder blue).

## Production design & costume
- Miniature-like, handcrafted sets; signage and props (keep on-set text minimal for AI); uniforms and matching
  outfits for groups.

## Editing
- Chapter cards, quick cutaways for jokes, symmetrical cut from one centred frame to another.

## Sound & music
- Baroque/folk/60s pop and composer scores (Alexandre Desplat in later films); dry, deadpan dialogue timing.

## Performance
- Deadpan delivery, rapid speech, characters looking straight into the lens.

## Use sparingly / avoid
- Symmetry alone is not the style — the comic precision and melancholy are. Avoid readable signs (AI garbles text).
