# Andrei Tarkovsky — style card

**Signature in one line:** time made visible — very long takes moving slowly through wind, water, fire and
memory, where the image is allowed to breathe until it becomes spiritual.
**ASL:** very long (takes of a minute or more are common) · **Collaborators:** DPs Vadim Yusov, Georgy Rerberg,
Alexander Knyazhinsky, Sven Nykvist (context only).

## Composition & framing
- Deep, layered frames with nature in the foreground; figures often small or seen from behind; mirrors and
  reflections; rooms opening onto landscape.

## Lenses & format
- Moderate lenses, deep focus; texture of film; shifts between colour, sepia and monochrome for memory/dream.

## Camera movement
- Extremely slow lateral tracks and push-ins, sometimes over objects in water; slow pans following a character
  across a field; levitation and dream moves rendered calmly.

## Light & colour
- Natural light, overcast skies, candle and fire light; desaturated greens and browns; sudden fire or rain as
  events.

## Production design & costume
- Weathered houses, ruins, flooded floors, dripping water, wind in grass and trees; simple, worn clothing.

## Editing
- Few cuts; time flows inside the shot; dream logic between scenes; no rush to explain.

## Sound & music
- Wind, water dripping, birds, distant trains, fire crackling; sparse classical (Bach) and electronic textures
  (Artemyev).

## Performance
- Internal, quiet, contemplative; characters look, wait, remember.

## Use sparingly / avoid
- Slowness without content is empty: every long take needs an event inside it (wind arriving, a candle carried,
  rain starting).

## AI translation
- "very slow lateral dolly over a flooded floor; water reflects grey light; objects lie submerged; one continuous
  take"
- "wind moves through tall grass in waves; a lone figure stands far away with their back to camera"
- "overcast natural light, desaturated green and brown palette, fine film grain"
- "dripping water, wind and distant bird sounds; no music"

## Example shot
EWS, very slow push-in over 20 s toward a wooden house in a field; a gust of wind rolls through the grass from
left to right; a woman sitting on the fence turns her head away; only wind.
