# Martin Scorsese — style card

**Signature in one line:** kinetic, confessional cinema — voice-over, long Steadicam oners through worlds,
whip pans, freeze frames and rock-and-roll driving the cut.
**ASL:** fast montage alternating with long oners · **Collaborators:** editor Thelma Schoonmaker; DPs Michael
Ballhaus, Robert Richardson, Rodrigo Prieto (context only).

## Composition & framing
- Characters dominate the frame in confrontation; slow-motion close-ups for intensity; insert montages of
  objects and money.

## Lenses & format
- Range from wide in oners to long lenses in slow motion; film texture.

## Camera movement
- Long Steadicam journeys (backstage into a club); whip pans; snap zooms; fast push-ins on a look; tracking along
  bars and tables.

## Light & colour
- Saturated reds in bars and nightlife; neon; stark contrast in violence; warmer nostalgia for the past.

## Production design & costume
- Period detail, clubs, kitchens, cars; costume that shows status rising and falling.

## Editing
- Montage set to songs; freeze frames with voice-over commentary; jump cuts in frenzy; abrupt violence; the
  narrator addressing the audience.

## Sound & music
- Pop and rock needle drops (plan for the edit); voice-over narration; overlapping dialogue.

## Performance
- Volatile, improvisational energy; charm turning into threat.

## Use sparingly / avoid
- Freeze frame + voice-over every minute becomes a tic; save for turning points.

- "fast whip pan left with motion blur to his face"
