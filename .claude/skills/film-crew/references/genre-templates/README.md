# Genre templates and the five-stage method (moved from shortfilm-prompt, 2026-11-26)

The `shortfilm-prompt` skill was retired on 2026-11-26. Its useful parts live here, inside film-crew:

- **`five-stage-method.md`**: the five-stage short-film structure.
  1. Core theme;
  2. Character and scene;
  3. Atmosphere and quality;
  4. Camera rules;
  5. Storyboard.
  
  It also has the emotional-narrative adaptation (family, pet, farewell), the seven self-check rules and the
  30-second checklist. Use it for one-idea shorts (transformation, emotional, combat) after film-crew's plan.
- **Genre templates** (27 files): ready structures for a 15 s transformation, animal vlog, car commercial, claymation,
  cyberpunk city, dance, drone FPV, fashion film, food ASMR, found-footage horror, micro-drama, movie trailer,
  multi-shot narrative, music video, nature timelapse, product commercial, sci-fi space, sports slow motion, travel
  vlog, and others, plus `camera-move-library.md`, `atmosphere-prefabs.md`, `genre-camera-sop.md` and
  `negative-prompts.md`.

**How to use:** in Step 0 (producer), if the brief matches a genre, read the matching template and take its beats
and camera habits into the plan. The final prompt still goes through the model skill (seedance-25-director,
cinema-worldbuilder-pro-2.0) and `prompt-craft.md`. The templates were written for older engines (Seedance 2.0,
Sora); their limits and model names are out of date, and the model skills' limits win.
