## What is new (Cloud, 2026-11-26)

1. **An opening by intrusion.** A macro 6 cm above the mud holds a quiet rack focus (grass, then soil, ash and droplets) for 3.5 s. In the last second her armoured boot rushes past the lens and throws mud onto it. The stillness is set up so the intrusion lands.
2. **A speed ramp inside one shot, on purpose.** The first half of the charge is real time and the second half is about 40% slow motion, with "the handheld camera remains intensely organic even in slow motion". The John school bans slow motion; here it is a deliberate dramatic tool with a stated percentage. Genre decides.
3. **Physical operator motion vs digital shake.** It asks for "strong natural camera sway, footstep vibrations… imperfect framing" but "no artificial camera shake, no gimbal, no digital floating". Real operator motion has causes (steps, reframing); added shake is noise. The prompt asks for the first and bans the second.
4. **Occlusion by the world.** The tracking camera passes behind spears, broken structures, smoke and duelling soldiers, so depth and danger come from the foreground.
5. **A recognition beat as micro-expressions.** "Eyes widen slightly, jaw tightens, nostrils flare, breathing momentarily suspended… composed and battle-ready." It is fear held down, and the camera reacts to her eyeline shift with an upward correction and a brief focus struggle.
6. **Imperfect acquisition as the reveal.** Over the shoulder, then a rack focus to the distant dragon, then a handheld telephoto push with "slight framing overshoot, momentary focus hunting, then a precise manual refocus". The camera searches like a person seeing something huge, which sells the scale and the shock.
7. **Screen direction and hand locks.** She runs "consistently toward screen-right", the sword stays in her right hand, and "nobody accompanies Mira".

**Weak spots to watch:**
- **Speed ramps inside a generated shot are unreliable.** Models often ignore the "40%" or apply it to the whole shot. If it matters, generate in real time and ramp in the edit.
- **A zoom on a prime package.** "Panavision optics" and specific primes (35, 65, 75, 100 mm macro), then a "telephoto zoom… 135–200mm equivalent". On a real set that is a lens change or a zoom lens. For the model it is fine; just don't copy it into a DP plan as one lens.
- **Shot 3 is technically hard.** A 75 mm tight close-up with the camera moving backward ahead of a sprinting subject is where AI faces wobble. A 50 mm lens, or a slower moment (she slows as she sees it), is safer.
- **The medieval Mira face reference (`3259a8c6…`) matches 21**, which is good consistency within the medieval world, though the identity-drift note from 21–23 still applies across worlds.

**What transfers to our skills:**
- openings by intrusion after a held stillness;
- speed ramps used deliberately, with a stated percentage, or done in post;
- physical operator motion described by its causes, with digital shake banned;
- reveals by imperfect acquisition, where the camera searches.
