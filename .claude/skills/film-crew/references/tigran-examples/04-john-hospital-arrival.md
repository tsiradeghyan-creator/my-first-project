# 04: John arrives at the abandoned hospital (12 s, 4 shots, reverse angle)

Shared by Tigran on 2026-11-26, the fourth in the John series. The prompt is verbatim below.

```text
12-second sequence, 4 shots, 3 hard cuts. The abandoned hospital forecourt: John's pickup arrives and stops near the entrance; he gets out with his rifle, closes the door, and walks toward the entrance. 16:9, 24 fps. Photoreal live action, natural light.
CUT POLICY: hard cuts only, on the action, no transitions, no dissolves, no fades, no wipes. Sound runs continuously across the cuts.
NO BGM — no background music of any kind. No score, no soundtrack, no instrumental, no underscore, no ambient musical pad, no drone, no tone bed, no swell, no sting, no humming, no singing, no whistling, no lyrics. Diegetic sound effects and room tone only. Nothing musical anywhere at any point.

<<<ef9bc5de-ebac-4d9f-bd5c-f515838a4148>>> — JOHN'S PICKUP TRUCK. Its model, colour, condition, cab and interior match its reference exactly and stay unchanged in every shot. Do not add anything to it that is not in its reference.
<<<24c1a8a4-aaa6-4035-a778-89f8a210b59c>>> — THE ABANDONED HOSPITAL AND ITS FORECOURT. SETTING:
<<<24c1a8a4-aaa6-4035-a778-89f8a210b59c>>>, the forecourt of a large abandoned hospital, under an overcast sky, light haze in the air. The pickup <<<ef9bc5de-ebac-4d9f-bd5c-f515838a4148>>> rolls in from the right and stops in front of the entrance, in its fixed position: parallel to the façade, its nose pointing left, its driver's side toward the camera. Behind it, the canopy, the sign and the dark gap of the sliding doors.
SHOT 2 — 4.0–6.5s — MEDIUM SHOT, JOHN GETS OUT.
Static, on the forecourt side of the truck, a few metres from the driver's door, at chest height, the truck in profile, its nose pointing left, the entrance behind it. The driver's door opens; <<<43e71247-4ba7-4118-8c8d-da583c401aa8>>> steps out onto the asphalt with the rifle <<<d1541ec5-f49a-4071-8ca2-88d589df4a6f>>> in his hand. John comes round the back of the truck and walks slowly toward the entrance, toward the camera, the rifle held low in both hands, his eyes on the dark gap. He gets closer and closer to the doors. Shot 3: static, close.
Shot 4: static, low, from inside the dark lobby, through the gap between the sliding doors.
Real camera with real weight. No zoom, no slow motion, no speed ramps, never smooth or weightless, never a game-engine look.

Realistic film acting, like a live-action drama. John moves like a man who has done this before in dead places: efficient, quiet, controlled. No fear acted, no heavy breathing, no frowning. What is alive is the work behind his eyes.
THE EVENT (read from the ending): going in. The sequence ends with John about to step into the dark.
PHYSICAL ACTION — the channel:

ACTING TASK — <<<43e71247-4ba7-4118-8c8d-da583c401aa8>>> (he is fully invested in his tactic; the work happens in his eyes; his partner is the dark entrance and whatever may be inside):

MOTIVE (his fuel): he needs something that is inside this building, and he needs it now.

OBSTACLE: the building is dark and unknown; anything could be inside; the engine he leaves running can be heard.
```

## What is new compared with 01–03 (Cloud, 2026-11-26)

1. **Geography lock block ("THE TRUCK'S POSITION — FIXED IN EVERY SHOT").** It states where the truck is, which way its nose points, and which side faces the camera. It even works out the reverse angle ahead of time: from the lobby, the nose points right. This is the 180° rule written for the model. It is the single most useful technique so far for multi-shot geography.
2. **Reference viewpoint ban, made specific.** It names the reference photo's own viewpoints so the model can't copy them ("no three-quarter view from the left corner, no chest-high view across an upright ambulance"). 01 only said "don't reproduce the framing".
3. **Reveal by foreground wipe.** A low lateral dolly past the overturned ambulance; the forecourt opens as the ambulance slides out of frame.
4. **Threat point of view without a threat.** Shot 4 is from inside the dark lobby, framed by the door gap and almost black. The audience feels watched; nothing is shown.
5. **Film stock fitted to the scene.** Daylight-balanced, 250-speed, fine grain for an overcast day, where 01–03 used tungsten 500. This fixes the stock-vs-light note on 01.
6. **Text policy with one exception.** "The only text in the image is the hospital sign CEDAR RIDGE MEMORIAL CLINIC", spelled out exactly.
7. **Moment-to-moment beats tied to shot numbers** (Shot 2: eyes to the gap; Shot 4: checks with every step).
8. **Sound perspective flips with the reverse angle.** Shot 4 has the hollow enclosed silence inside, his steps getting closer outside, and the idling engine behind him. The obstacle "the engine he leaves running can be heard" is kept in the sound.

**Weak spots to watch:**
- **Four shots with a reverse angle in 12 s is the hardest yet.** If the model flips the truck in Shot 4, generate Shot 4 separately with the geography block, and use a frame from Shot 2 as the start-frame reference.
- **Shot 3 is 1.5 s.** That is short for a door to close with weight; 2 s is safer.
- **Rendered sign text** can garble; the exact spelling in the prompt is the right move. Check it in the output.

**Pattern so far (01–04):** everything from 01–03, plus a geography and axis lock block for multi-shot scenes, specific reference-viewpoint bans, and film stock fitted to the light.
