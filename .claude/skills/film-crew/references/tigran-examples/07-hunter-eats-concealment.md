## What is new compared with 01–06 (Cloud, 2026-11-26)

1. **A video reference that governs the look only, with what it shows written out.** "In <<<video_1>>> the camera is a DISTANT WIDE … WITH FIVE MEN AND AN ANIMAL. HERE … a tight shot of one man." Naming the reference's content stops the model from copying its framing and its crowd.
2. **Horror by concealment.** The carcass is below frame, and what he eats is a dark shapeless mass, never in focus, never readable. The terror is implied, which is stronger on screen. It also keeps the shot within platform content filters.
3. **One unbroken gesture (overlapping action).** "The hand is already going down as the knee is still dropping… no plateau." This works against the AI habit of splitting an action into separate steps with pauses. It is the animation principle of overlapping action, written for a video model.
4. **Performance by contrast.** "The madness is in the hands, not in the eyes. The hands are frantic and the face is almost empty." The eyes are narrowed and private: down to the hands, then nowhere, then back. It names the two clichés to avoid: the staring madman and savouring.
5. **A state that changes during the shot.** The clay smear around his mouth builds over six seconds, and the blood state at the start is given ("already heavily marked from the killing"), so it continues from the previous shot.
6. **The angle changes through the action.** It starts as a steep low angle and ends almost level, because he kneels toward the lens and the camera tilts down to keep him waist-up.
7. **Backlight without a silhouette.** A faint rim from the hidden sun, but "his face… clearly visible throughout": a known failure of low angles against a bright sky, locked by name.
8. **The world goes on off screen.** Machete impacts "never evenly spaced and never in a beat" (so they never turn into music), and the others still working around him. Sound widens the world past the frame.

**Weak spots to watch:**
- **The reference description drifts between shots.** The same character id (`04b0605c…`) was "carved rune-like marks, dried blood around the mouth" in 06. Here it is "stitched-mouth line, spiral scarification, stacked military belts". If the reference image did not change, keep one fixed description per id across the series. A drifting description fights the reference.
- **"Tribal calls" contradicts 06.** 06 said the calls copy no real culture. "Tribal" pulls the model toward real-world stereotypes. Use the same wording as 06 ("their own invented calls").
- **Timecodes are seconds:frames again** (0:10, 1:14, 2:12). Write decimal seconds.
- **Content.** Eating from a kill with blood is likely to trigger moderation on some engines even with concealment. Keep the concealment lines, and have a fallback with less blood on the face.
