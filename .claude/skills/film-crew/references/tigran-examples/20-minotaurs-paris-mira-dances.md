## What is new compared with 01–19 (Cloud, 2026-11-26)

1. **Calm in the foreground, chaos in the background, as the joke.** Mira dances, sharp and unbothered, while the minotaurs scare pedestrians in the soft background. It is the comedy version of 16 (a still bison against a flowing herd). The contrast carries the gag, so the shot needs no dialogue.
2. **A named performance reference, followed by its mechanics.** "The relaxed, blissfully nonchalant energy of Jon Hamm's famous nightclub dance in *Your Friends & Neighbors*", then exactly what the body does: arms loosely at chest or shoulder height, soft wrists, swaying shoulders, small head moves, eyes narrowing, "not a rehearsed choreography, not theatrical". The name tells a human the vibe; the mechanics tell the model.
3. **A documentary-reporter camera in Shot 1.** Moving through pedestrians, a whip pan to one minotaur, a rack focus to the second, people crossing the lens. The camera is a startled witness, like the prey camera in 06 but played for laughs.
4. **An "intent" note for the creatures.** "Like mischievous oversized children who find frightening people hilarious, while the pedestrians are genuinely terrified." It gives two opposite emotional states in one frame, each stated.
5. **Anti-sync again** ("one slightly ahead, the other weaving behind… never perfectly synchronized"), with "exactly two identical" for identity. Identity is shared and motion is independent.
6. **Specific failure locks:** "NO tongues sticking out" (the minotaurs' roaring faces likely went cartoonish before), and "No physical attacks or injuries" (safety in a public panic scene).
7. **The same school's structure:** header line, camera package, CHARACTERS and LOCATION blocks, shots with mm:ss times, a CAMERA & EDITING recap, a STRICT recap.

**Weak spots to watch:**
- **Naming a real actor and a real show.** The video model has not "seen" the scene, and some engines filter real-person names. The mechanics that follow already do the work, so keep the name in our notes, not in the prompt.
- **Fast crowd motion in Shot 1 is the highest morph risk in AI video** (whip pan, running creatures, many panicking people, a rack focus) in 5 s. Expect warped faces in the crowd. Fewer pedestrians, larger in frame, and one clear path for the minotaurs reduce it.
- **A real, recognisable city in panic.** It is a fantasy comedy, but "genuine fear and panic" on a real Paris street may trip moderation on some engines. Keep the comedy cue visible (the minotaurs' playfulness) and the "no attacks" line.
- **Camera brand names and generic praise** ("beautifully composed"), as in 19.

**What transfers to our skills:**
- foreground calm against background chaos;
- a named reference always followed by its mechanics (and only the mechanics in the prompt);
- shared identity with independent motion;
- an intent line when two opposite emotions share a frame.
