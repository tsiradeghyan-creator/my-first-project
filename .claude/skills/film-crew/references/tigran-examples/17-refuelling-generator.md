## What is new compared with 01–16 (Cloud, 2026-11-26)

1. **Cut points pinned in seconds.** "Hard cuts only at 4.5 s, 9 s and 15.5 s… no extra cuts." Earlier sequences gave shot ranges; this states the cut times directly in the CUT POLICY, which is easier for a model to obey.
2. **Start late.** Shot 3 "opens with the petrol ALREADY pouring". Setup beats (unscrewing, lifting, positioning) are skipped, which saves seconds and avoids the hand gymnastics AI handles badly.
3. **Liquid physics in phases, with matching sound.**
   - The stream goes steady, then gurgles and bursts as air goes back in, then a trickle, then the last drops.
   - The sound follows the same phases: glug, gurgle, air into the can, drips.
   - "He holds it there a moment to let the last drop fall." The physics carries the character (nothing is wasted).
4. **Weight shown in the body.** The arm is straight and pulled down, the shoulder dropped on that side, the body leaning the other way, the can swinging. "The effort is in his body, not his face."
5. **A global viewpoint ban for a location used across several shots.** "Every shot is a new camera position inside this place."
6. **A frame within a frame for an arrival** (the tower's legs and braces) and "the calm of someone who is home". The driving itself is the performance in an unseen-driver shot.
7. **A motive chain to the story's stakes.** "Without fuel there is no power, no light, no radio: no signal." A small chore is tied to the larger plot, so the event, "a small, practical victory", has weight.
8. **Series continuity.** In 14 the cans were empty; here the can is full. The off-screen filling is implied by continuity alone.

**Weak spots to watch:**
- **20 s with 4 shots in one generation** is at or past the limit of most engines' single-clip length and consistency. Seedance 2.5 accepts up to 30 s, but character consistency drops across long clips. Consider two generations (shots 1–2, shots 3–4) and cut on the hands.
- **The phases in Shot 3 are dense** (pouring, weakening, drops, lift-out, reaching for the cap in 6.5 s). That is fine, but check that the stream doesn't stay continuous to the end; models like constant flows.
- **Fuel vapour** ("a faint shimmer") can read as heat haze across the whole frame. Keep it "just above the filler neck".
- **"Aspect ratio 16:9" appears twice**, the same note as 15.
