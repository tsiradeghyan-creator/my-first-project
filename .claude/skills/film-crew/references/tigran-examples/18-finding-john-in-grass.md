## What is new compared with 01–17 (Cloud, 2026-11-26)

1. **The camera physically touches the world.** It "pushes its way through": stalks press against the lens, bend, slide across it and spring back behind it. "The camera never passes through the grass like a ghost: every stalk it meets reacts." AI cameras usually float through foliage as if it were not there; this makes the camera an object in the scene.
2. **MOTION SPEED becomes MOTION SPEED AND QUALITY.** It covers the grass (gentle, "never whipping") and the man ("never jerky, twitchy or robotic; no frame-skipping"). Both speed and smoothness are locked.
3. **Part of the reference, with an explicit exclusion.** "Use only the grass and the land. Do NOT reproduce its sky or its sun." The prairie plate (the dawn fog with the pale sun disc in 01 and 06) is reused under a different sky. A reference supplies texture and vegetation, while the weather is rewritten.
4. **Fixes that point to earlier failures.**
   - "He WEARS HIS HAT ON HIS HEAD… the hat staying on his head";
   - the sun banned eleven ways;
   - "no golden hour".
   
   These read as lessons from earlier generations (a missing hat, the reference's sun leaking in). Locking a repeat failure by name, more than once, is the series method.
5. **A reveal by approach.** The shape is barely readable through the gaps, then clearer, then the body, the hands, the eye. The camera's movement is the reveal.
6. **Real marksman behaviour as tactic, plus weapon safety staging.** He controls his breath "timed to keep the crosshair still", the finger rests along the trigger guard rather than on it, the muzzle points past the lens and never into it, and "never fired". It is truthful craft, and it keeps clear of moderation filters.
7. **The orbit ends at a designed final frame**: low, at face level, slightly off the barrel's axis, his face framed between stalks, a thin strip of grey sky at most.

**Weak spots to watch:**
- **The rifle changed type.** In 01–04 it is a "LEVER-ACTION RIFLE WITH SCOPE"; here the same reference id (`d1541ec5…`) is a "bolt-action rifle". The text and the reference now disagree. Pick the one the reference image shows and fix it in the series bible.
- **The sun is banned eleven times.** A long run of "no sun / no sun disc / no glow / no sunbeams…" can prime the very thing it bans. One positive line ("a thick, uniform grey cloud ceiling, light from the whole sky, no single direction") plus one "no sun anywhere" usually works better.
- **Camera–grass interaction for 15 s is hard for video models.** Stalks may melt or morph where they touch the lens. If it fails, shorten the push to 4–5 s and let the orbit happen in grass that is only brushed, not flattened.
