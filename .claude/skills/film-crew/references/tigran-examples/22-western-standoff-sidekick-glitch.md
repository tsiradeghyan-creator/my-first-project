## What is new (Cloud, 2026-11-26)

1. **SPATIAL LOCK for three parties.** Mira and the sheriff are at opposite ends of the street, and the orc is at a "THIRD, SEPARATE POSITION… NEVER beside Mira". It is the geography block of 04, applied to a standoff with an interrupter.
2. **Dialogue timing rules.** "Her line ENDS COMPLETELY. Hold approximately 1–2 seconds of tense silence." Then the orc's line starts off-screen, and the camera whip-pans to him **mid-line**, inside the same shot. Overlaps and silences are written out, which is where comic timing lives.
3. **Off-screen sound motivates the camera.** The voice comes "noticeably farther away" with outdoor acoustics. The whip-pan follows the sound and ends in a snap zoom with telephoto compression. The zoom is used once, on purpose, as a comic beat; elsewhere zooms are banned.
4. **A minimal costume override.** The orc keeps his medieval armour and "ONLY a classic cowboy hat is added". The smallest change makes the gag.
5. **A meta "reality glitch" kept at image level.** Two glitch pulses: frame duplication, horizontal displacement, digital tearing. "The glitch affects the IMAGE, not his body. NO morphing." The line between image distortion (wanted) and anatomical morphing (unwanted) is drawn explicitly, with matching sound (two low-frequency pulses, audio instability).
6. **A character arc across the series.** Mira's final smile is "growing control over reality", which follows on from "And… CUT!" in 19. She is the one who bends the film.
7. **A genre cliché allowed on purpose.** Tumbleweed is banned in 14 (a realistic desert) and required here (a Western parody). Clichés are tools that depend on the genre.

**Weak spots to watch:**
- **Asking for glitches invites uncontrolled artefacts.** The model may glitch the whole clip or morph faces anyway. Keep the glitch to the 3-second sheriff shot, as written, and expect retries. A cleaner option is to add the glitch in post.
- **No SHOT labels or cut list, only time ranges and "HARD CUT" in two places.** The whip-pan section is one shot, the rest are cuts. A short CUT POLICY ("hard cuts at 4 s, 8 s, 18 s, 21 s; no cut at 13 s") would remove doubt.
- **"SALOON" is the one sign that must read**, so spell it out, which this prompt does. Other signage should stay unreadable.
