## What is new compared with 01–12 (Cloud, 2026-11-26)

1. **A MOTION SPEED block.** "Every movement happens in real time… never sped up, never fast-forward, no time-lapse." Vehicles and crowds in video models often move too fast or jerky, so this closes a known artefact with its own block.
2. **The plate-to-shot handoff in action.** The plate from 11 governs the buildings, cars, leaves, glass and skid marks. Its own viewpoint is banned by description ("no low, central, symmetrical view straight down the middle of the street"), and one element is moved on purpose as a declared delta: the bus is "much further away than in the location reference", at the vanishing point. This is the plate-first workflow (05, 09–11) working as designed.
3. **A reveal by crane, timed in three phases.** Rooftops (0–3 s), the descent revealing the street (3–6 s), settling at head height while the truck approaches (6–10 s). The truck enters during the descent, so the reveal and the arrival overlap.
4. **The final camera position is set off the plate's axis.** The camera ends "slightly to the right of the street's centre line… at a gentle angle", which honours the ban on the plate's symmetrical view.
5. **The acoustics match the space.** The engine is "echoing off the empty shopfronts". The fog shots had a dead acoustic with no reverb; a street of hard façades gets an echo.
6. **Text comes only from the reference** ("the only text… is the faded signs already on the shopfronts"), the same method as the bottles in 12.
7. **A small hero.** John is "seen only small through the windscreen", so his reference is used lightly. The shot belongs to the place.

**Weak spots to watch:**
- **The side street is not in the plate.** Plate 11 has a cross-intersection at the far end, where the bus is, but no side street "a little way down from the camera". Since the reference is told to "match exactly", declare the side street as a delta ("a side street on the right that the reference does not show"), the same way the bus distance is declared.
- **The plate is altered twice** (the bus moved, a side street added) **while it must "match exactly".** Two deltas are fine if both are declared. Undeclared ones fight the reference.
- **The geography is right; keep it in the bible.** The truck comes out of a side street on the right and turns left to drive toward the camera, which is correct. Write the screen direction down (it enters frame right, then moves toward the lens) for the next shot's continuity.

**Pattern so far (01–13):** everything from 01–12, plus:
- a motion-speed lock;
- shots built from plates with the viewpoint banned and the changes declared as deltas;
- reveals timed to overlap with the action;
- acoustics matched to the space (dead in fog, echo in streets);
- text carried only by references.
