## What is new compared with 01–02 (Cloud, 2026-11-26)

1. **Four shots in one generation, with a CUT POLICY block.** Hard cuts on the action, no transitions, and sound running continuously across the cuts. The edit is written into the prompt itself.
2. **Size jump as the punctuation.** Three static extreme close-up inserts (key, gear, wheel) cut to an extreme wide aerial. This is a classic accent cut (step 6 to step 1 on the film-crew size ladder), used on purpose for the release.
3. **The obstacle becomes a physical beat.** "The engine may not start" is played as two coughs and then a catch. Suspense comes from an object, not a face.
4. **Sound mapped shot by shot**, with perspective matching the frame: in the cab, every click and clunk; from the drone, the engine distant and small across the valley. Silence before the engine makes the roar land.
5. **Consequence physics.** When the engine catches, the key, dashboard and wheel shiver; when the gear engages, the truck lurches; the wheel turns with weight and resistance.
6. **Series bible.** John's motive is the same as in 01 ("stayed alive this long by not being seen"), so the character's want carries across shots. The truck reference lists every part that must not change (ignition, keys, selector, wheel).

**Weak spots to watch:**
- **"Drone" means two things in one prompt.** Shot 4 is a drone camera, while the NO BGM line forbids "drone" (a musical drone). A model may read the ban as being about the aerial, or the camera as being about the sound. Say "aerial shot from high above" for the camera, or "drone note" in the music ban.
- **The prairie reference id changed** (`84afa6fd…` here, `9863bc64…` in 01–02). If that is not a deliberate new plate, the location may not match across the series.
- **The last frame is a state** ("heading toward the valley"), not a composition. Name what is in frame at 12.0 s.
- **12 s with 4 shots and 3 cuts is a lot for one generation.** Check that the model holds every cut point; if it drifts, split into 2 generations at the cut to the aerial.

**Pattern so far (01–03):**
- the emotion lives in one body channel (feet and eyes, the finger, the hands);
- the acting task is Goal / Obstacle / Tactic, with the obstacle turned into a physical beat;
- each reference has a role, props list their locked parts, and known artefacts are locked by name;
- sound is diegetic, mapped per shot and matched in perspective;
- the template and the character's motive stay fixed across the series.
