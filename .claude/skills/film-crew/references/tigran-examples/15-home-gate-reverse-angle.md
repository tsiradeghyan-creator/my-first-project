## What is new compared with 01–14 (Cloud, 2026-11-26)

1. **A reference used for its reverse angle.** "Same gate, same fence, same road… but seen in REVERSE ANGLE: from the inside of the fence, looking out." It says what is now behind the camera and out of frame (the station, the tower, the mast) and what must not exist outside the fence. One location reference can serve both sides of a scene when the reverse is declared.
2. **Symmetry with a meaning.** This shot is centred, square and one-point perspective, the opposite of 11's "never centred or squared". The padlock sits on the centre line in the foreground, between the camera and John. The composition says what the scene means: the threshold, the lock, the home that must stay untouched. Rules like "never centred" are defaults, broken on purpose when the meaning asks for it.
3. **A story prop checked by a look.** John stays seated, "looking at the closed gate and the chain… checking that nothing has touched it". The whole performance is one look at one object.
4. **Sound that tells you where you are.** "The faint, regular tick of the fence energiser close to the camera" says electrified fence, home and safety, without a word or a sign.
5. **A compact form.** Each block is a single line (CUT POLICY, NO BGM, MOTION SPEED, REFERENCES, SETTING, TIME, REALISM, SHOT, PERFORMANCE, PHYSICAL ACTION, PHYSICS, LIGHTING, AUDIO, stock). Without the long recap of 06–08 and 14, it holds the same discipline in about a quarter of the length. This is probably the best default length for most engines.

**Weak spots to watch:**
- **The speed is physically impossible.** The truck comes "from the horizon line" to three metres from the gate in about 3 s. That is hundreds of km/h, which directly contradicts the MOTION SPEED block ("slowly and steadily"). The model will choose one, usually by time-lapsing the truck. Start it in the middle distance (already a few hundred metres away, growing), or make the shot 8–10 s.
- **A chain-link fence across the whole frame** is a moiré and aliasing risk in AI video: the mesh may shimmer or crawl. "The mesh perfectly still" helps. Keeping the mesh slightly soft (it is near the camera, and focus is on the road) reduces it further.
- **"Aspect ratio 16:9" is written twice** (first line and last line). It is harmless here, but keep one.

**Pattern so far (01–15):** everything from 01–14, plus:
- reverse angles taken from a single reference and declared;
- symmetry and centring used on purpose when the meaning asks for it;
- a performance made of one look at one prop;
- ambient sound that identifies the place;
- a compact one-line-per-block template as a lean default.
- A watch item: speed against distance.
