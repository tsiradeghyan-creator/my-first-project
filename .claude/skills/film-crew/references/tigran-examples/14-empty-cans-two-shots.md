## What is new compared with 01–13 (Cloud, 2026-11-26)

1. **Selective use of a multi-object reference.** "Only the cans are used from this reference — the small white plastic bottle beside them is not in these shots and is never seen." A reference image often holds more than you want; name what to take and what to leave.
2. **Concrete costume and vehicle descriptions next to the references.** The jacket (olive waxed canvas, corduroy collar, flap pockets), the hat, the hair and beard; and the truck panels (brown and tan two-tone, chrome trim, red reflector). Text and reference now agree in detail. Keep exactly this description fixed for the rest of the series.
3. **Drama from an object's physics.** Empty steel cans "boom like bells", so the obstacle is the noise itself. The audio has one precise exception: "no echo and no reverb except inside the empty cans themselves". The second boom is "identical to the first", a sound callback.
4. **An anonymous insert that limits what the model must render.** Head cropped by the top edge, feet and ground below the bottom edge, and "every object… leaves it downward, out of the bottom edge". Less to generate means less to go wrong, and the shot stays about hands and objects.
5. **A classic suspense beat.** The noise he cannot help, then stop, eyes over the land, listening, nothing, a slow breath out. The threat exists only as a possibility, and the audience fills it in.
6. **A precise performance count:** "he blinks once in Shot B". It also says "a man listening, not a man posing".
7. **Two shots with their own framing, optics, camera and timing blocks,** separated visually (═══ lines), and a cut on the action: Shot A ends reaching for the second can, Shot B starts lifting it. "Identical film look and grade in both shots" locks the match.
8. **Lens data that is correct.** 85 mm has a diagonal field of view of 29°, which shows the writer computes it rather than guesses.
9. **The cliché ban fits the location.** "No tumbleweed rolling through frame" is the desert default, banned by name.

**Weak spots to watch:**
- **Shot A's geometry.** From a "side-on" view of the truck's rear corner, a tailgate dropped flat is seen edge-on, so "its inner face and latch hardware visible" is not possible unless the camera is slightly behind the truck. Write "from just behind the rear corner, at three-quarters" or drop the inner-face detail, or the model will bend the truck to satisfy both.
- **The location changed to an open desert gas station.** The series so far was a foggy prairie and a small town under overcast light. That is fine for a new scene, but the bible should record the change (fog gone, desert scrub) so the grade stays consistent.
- **Timecodes are seconds:frames again** (0:12, 2:12, 3:12).
- **Length and capitals.** The same notes as 06.
