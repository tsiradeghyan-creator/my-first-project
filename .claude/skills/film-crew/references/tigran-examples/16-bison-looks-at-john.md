## What is new compared with 01–15 (Cloud, 2026-11-26)

1. **Animal acting, written as behaviour.**
   - Its state is "not aggressive, not afraid. Calm, heavy, curious, utterly unbothered."
   - The behaviours are precise: steps getting shorter before the stop, all four hooves planted, a slow head turn, two clouds of breath, one ear flick.
   
   The same "one physical channel" method as John's shots, applied to an animal.
2. **The look into the lens is the event.** The series bans looking into the lens elsewhere. Here the bull faces the lens on purpose, because the lens is John. "The event: being seen." As in 15, the default rule is broken when the meaning calls for it.
3. **A POV "but not through the glass".** The camera sits just in front of the windscreen, which gives John's point of view without windscreen reflections and dirt.
4. **Stillness in front of flow.** One still animal in sharp focus, with the herd an unbroken blurred stream behind it. The contrast carries the moment.
5. **Screen direction stated and repeated** ("RIGHT to LEFT", four times), so the herd's direction holds across this shot and its neighbours.
6. **Variety against cloning in a crowd of animals.** "Each animal different in size, shape, fur and posture", the same guard as the varied packaging in plate 10.

**Weak spots to watch:**
- **John's reference is attached while he is off-screen.** An attached character reference pulls the model to show that person. In 06 an absent character was declared with "NEVER APPEARS". Either remove John's reference from this shot (the gaze target can be described in words), or add "he is never visible in the frame".
- **The head turn may be anatomically forced.** A bison's neck turns only a little; "faces the lens almost straight on" from side-on may give an unnatural twist. Let the head and forequarters turn together, a quarter turn of the body, so the look is physically possible.
- **The distance and the lens.** "A few metres away" on a "long lens" fills the frame with more than head, hump and shoulders unless the lens is moderate. Pick a focal length (e.g. 100–135 mm at 15–20 m) and make the distance match.

**Pattern so far (01–16):** everything from 01–15, plus:
- animal performance as precise behaviours with a stated state of mind;
- the look into the lens used as the event;
- a POV in front of the glass;
- stillness against flow;
- repeated screen direction.
- A watch item: a reference attached for an off-screen character.
