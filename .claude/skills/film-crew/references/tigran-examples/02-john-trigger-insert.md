## What is new compared with 01 (Cloud, 2026-11-26)

1. **It is a series template.** It uses the same blocks in the same order and the same reference ids for John, the rifle and the prairie, with shot codes (1S, 1G). The format is a production system that keeps the whole sequence consistent, not a one-off prompt.
2. **Insert grammar.** The shot is a static extreme close-up with timed sub-beats (0.0–1.5 s settle, 1.5–5.0 s squeeze) and anatomical precision: the pad of the finger on the centre of the trigger, and the trigger moving "a hair, then a hair more".
3. **Defined last frame.** "Hold on the finger, mid-squeeze, to the final frame." This fixes the weakness of 01.
4. **An event that must not happen is locked in picture and sound.** "The rifle does not fire" in the action, and "No gunshot" in the audio.
5. **A recurring artefact gets locked three times.** The rifle is completely dry: in its reference line, in the shot and in the physics. This suggests earlier generations showed dew on the metal, because the prairie is wet.
6. **Expert behavioural detail.** "Squeeze so the shot surprises even him" is the real marksman's surprise break, a tactic that gives the hand something truthful to do.
7. **The sound shrinks with the frame.** It keeps only the tiny sounds the frame can hear (finger on metal, trigger tension) and drops breathing.

**Pattern so far (01–02):**
- the emotion goes into one body channel (feet and eyes, then the finger);
- the acting task follows Goal / Obstacle / Tactic;
- each reference has a role, and props are locked;
- known artefacts are locked by name;
- the template is fixed across a series.
