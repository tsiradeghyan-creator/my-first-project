## What is new compared with 01–18 (Cloud, 2026-11-26)

1. **Dialogue with delivery notes and a voice reference.** Each line carries a delivery note ("deep, gently cheerful", "disappointed, mildly scolding"), and Mira's lines are tied to `<<<audio_1>>>`. This is Seedance 2.5's voice (tone) reference used inside a multi-shot scene.
2. **Comedy built as an escalating reveal.**
   - a huge orc, then his innocent "Hi";
   - the faint;
   - Mira peeking out;
   - two minotaurs and a dragon's eye;
   - the twist "And… CUT!", which reframes everything as a staged prank.
   
   Every shot raises the stakes one step, and the pauses are written in ("Brief silence", "A brief awkward beat").
3. **Costume overrides on character references.** The orc and Mira keep their identity from their references but wear a new courier uniform written in a COSTUMES block ("Original hair visible behind cap"). This is identity from the reference and wardrobe from the text, the right split.
4. **Count locks and scale by fragment.** "EXACTLY TWO" minotaurs from one reference. The dragon is "only ONE COLOSSAL EYE and part of its scaled head", so scale comes from what you don't see.
5. **Rack focus as a comic reveal.** Mira peeks out from behind the orc's shoulder and the focus jumps from his jacket to her face. The camera move is the punchline.
6. **Physical comedy with mechanics.** In the faint, the eyes lose focus, the knees buckle, a shoulder lands first, then the torso and legs, and the glasses shift. "Genuinely loses consciousness", not a pratfall.
7. **An eyeline just past the lens.** Mira looks "just past the lens" in the final close-up: intimate, without breaking the fourth wall until the "CUT" gesture does it on purpose.
8. **A geography block for an interior** (door opens inward, the corridor's depth, a side hallway, a window at the far end). Each shot states which way the camera faces.
9. **A per-shot recap** at the end (CAMERA & CONTINUITY), like the POSITIVE CONSTRAINTS of the long template, but one line per shot.

**Weak spots to watch:**
- **Very ambitious for one generation.** 25 s, 7 shots, 4 spoken lines, 6 image references plus a voice. Expect drift in the creatures and the orc's lip-sync (tusks make mouth shapes hard). Split at the corridor reveal (shots 1–4, then 5–7) and carry a frame across as a start reference.
- **Camera brand names** (ARRI ALEXA 35, Panavision) are not used in our skills, which describe the look instead. Some engines respond to them, others ignore them or render a camera in frame. Test both ways before adopting it.
- **"MUSIC: FALSE"** reads like code. "No music; diegetic sound only" is clearer to a video model.
- **The small embroidered "NPC"** will likely garble. It is fine as an easter egg; don't rely on it reading.
- **Generic quality words** ("Premium cinematic photorealism", "beautifully framed") carry no instruction. The John series replaces them with physical specifics.
