# 09: Looted pharmacy, environment plate (still image)

Shared by Tigran on 2026-11-26. This is a still-image world plate, the interior counterpart of 05. The prompt is verbatim below.

```text
Cinematic film still. Interior of a looted pharmacy inside an abandoned small-town clinic, late autumn day. No people, environment plate.

COMPOSITION: 35mm, camera at chest height just inside the pharmacy entrance, looking in along the length of the room; a heavy roll-down steel security grate fills the foreground, pulled down to the floor but levered and bent upward at one corner to leave a forced gap roughly a metre wide — the camera looks through and past this gap into the room; beyond it, two long parallel shelving units run away from the camera with a narrow aisle between them; a dispensing counter along the right wall; the back wall at the far end carrying a single small high window; the ceiling low with a suspended acoustic grid.

THE FORCED GRATE: galvanised steel roll-down shutter, its slats buckled and torn at the lower left corner where a bar has been worked under and levered repeatedly, the metal peeled back and the edges bright and scratched where the paint and oxide have been scraped away — the only recently disturbed surface in the whole building; the floor guide rail bent out of true; the padlock hasp twisted and hanging; above the forced corner the slats are still intact and dusty.

THE SHELVING: two freestanding metal pharmacy shelf units running the length of the room, five shelves high, forming a narrow aisle between them roughly eighty centimetres wide; the shelves lined with plastic dividers and small bins, each bin with a printed label strip on its front edge; the stock heavily picked over — whole sections swept bare with only the clean rectangles in the dust where boxes stood, other sections still holding rows of white pill bottles and small cardboard cartons standing upright and untouched; one bin pulled out and dropped on the floor with its contents spilled; loose white pills scattered across the vinyl and crushed to powder in places where feet have passed.

THE COUNTER: a laminate dispensing counter along the right wall with a dead computer terminal, a label printer with a curl of blank labels hanging from it, a pill counting tray and spatula, a tipped basket; behind the counter a wall-mounted hanging rack of white paper prescription bags filed alphabetically by surname, each stapled shut with a printed label, perhaps forty of them still hanging exactly where they were left, never collected, grey with dust.

THE REST: a glass-fronted refrigerator against the far wall, dark and dead, its door standing ajar and its racks empty; a narcotics safe set into the wall below the counter, its door hanging open and the interior stripped bare, the surrounding wall gouged where it was attacked; a tipped stool; a waste bin overflowing with crushed boxes; hand-written paper notices taped to the end of each shelf unit; a thick, even, undisturbed layer of pale dust over every horizontal surface except the forced grate itself.

THE LIGHT: two weak sources only — a small high window in the back wall, frosted and fitted with security bars, throwing a soft grey slab down the far end of the aisle and across the upper shelves; and grey corridor daylight leaking through the forced gap in the grate behind the camera, raking low across the floor for a couple of metres and dying; the middle of the room sits in deep cool shadow that still holds detail; the tops of the shelves and the ceiling are dark; no artificial light, no emergency lighting, no flashlight.

THE AIR: still and cold; fine dust suspended, clearly visible where the window slab falls.

THE GRADE: cold and desaturated; both daylight sources read grey-blue and neutral; shadows cool and open with detail retained throughout, nothing crushed to pure black; the white pill bottles and the paper prescription bags the lightest elements, dulled by dust; low to moderate contrast; almost no saturated colour anywhere.

PALETTE: 45% cold grey-blue — the two light sources, the dust in the air, the lit shelf faces; 30% deep cool shadow — the middle of the room, the shelf tops, the ceiling; 25% drained off-white and galvanised grey — the pill bottles, the paper bags, the steel grate and shelving.

LIGHTING restated: a barred high window at the far end and corridor light through the forced grate behind camera as the only sources; no artificial light, no practical, no fill; the window slab the brightest value, the ceiling and shelf tops the darkest.

Focus: deep focus, the torn edge of the grate in the foreground, the shelf contents, the prescription bag rack and the far window all sharp and readable; anamorphic elongated vertical oval bokeh on the dust motes crossing the window slab.

Shot on real 35mm film, tungsten-balanced 500-speed colour negative film, visible grain, natural halation on the window slab; anamorphic 35mm lens, rectilinear, straight verticals; suspended dust; tech tail palette: cold grey-blue 45%, deep cool shadow 30%, drained off-white and galvanised grey 25%; gritty documentary realism, available light only; absolutely no CGI, no render look, no colour grade, no dramatic lighting, no teal-and-orange, no video-game styling, no blood, no bodies, no vegetation indoors. Clean environment plate, no people, no flashlight in frame. No text overlay, no watermark. Aspect ratio 2.39:1.
```

## What is new compared with 05, the other plate (Cloud, 2026-11-26)

1. **Labelled sections for a still.** COMPOSITION, then one block per set area (THE FORCED GRATE, THE SHELVING, THE COUNTER, THE REST), then LIGHT, AIR, GRADE, PALETTE and Focus. Each area of the set gets its own inventory, so nothing is forgotten and the model reads the room in zones.
2. **Set dressing as forensic evidence, with a timeline.**
   - The grate's bright scratched edges are "the only recently disturbed surface in the whole building", against thick old dust everywhere.
   - Clean rectangles in the dust show where boxes stood.
   - The narcotics safe was attacked.
   - Pills are crushed where feet passed.
   
   The plate tells who came, what they took and how long ago, without a person.
3. **One emotional detail.** About forty prescription bags, filed by surname, "never collected, grey with dust". It is a single humane prop that says everyone left. A plate needs one of these.
4. **Frame-in-frame foreground that is also a route.** The forced gap in the grate frames the aisle, and it is the way in for the next shot. The plate is designed for the scene that will use it.
5. **Two named sources, brightest and darkest values named, and "shadows… still hold detail, nothing crushed to pure black".** This keeps the plate usable later as a reference or for grading.
6. **Deep focus** for a plate meant to be read, where 05 was wide open for mood. The optics are chosen for the plate's job.
7. **Style bans by name:** "no teal-and-orange, no video-game styling, no dramatic lighting". These are the default AI looks for a "post-apocalyptic interior".

**Weak spots to watch:**
- **Contradictions inside the prompt:**
  - "THE GRADE: cold and desaturated" against "no colour grade" at the end;
  - "deep focus, all sharp" against "anamorphic oval bokeh" (bokeh needs out-of-focus areas; limit it to the dust motes nearest the lens or drop it);
  - "undisturbed dust everywhere except the grate" against "crushed… where feet have passed" (feet disturb floor dust; say "footprints through the dust on the floor" — which is also a story clue).
- **"tech tail palette"** looks like a typo, and the palette is written twice. Keep it once.
- **In-scene text** (label strips, printed bag labels, hand-written notices) will come out as gibberish. That is fine as an unreadable texture, but a later close-up will expose it. Say "labels too small and dusty to read", or plan an insert with real text added in post.
- **The aspect ratio is in the prompt text,** the same note as 05.

**Pattern so far (01–09):** everything from 01–08. For stills: labelled zones, set dressing as forensic evidence with a timeline, one humane prop, a foreground that is also the route for the next shot, shadows that keep detail, and default AI looks banned by name.
