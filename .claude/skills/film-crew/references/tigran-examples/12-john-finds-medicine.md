## What is new compared with 01–11 (Cloud, 2026-11-26)

1. **A product prop has its own reference, and its text comes from that reference.** The bottles are a separate reference: "Do not invent other kinds of medicine packaging", and "the only text in the image is the labels on the bottles, exactly as in their reference". Readable text is carried by an image reference instead of being spelled in the prompt. This is the reliable way to get labels right.
2. **A pre-emptive ban on an AI default.** "Nothing on his back, no bag." A scavenger tends to get a backpack by default. Here the pockets must do the job, so the bulging pockets become the visual proof of the haul.
3. **The label is kept out of the face close-up.** In Shot 2 the bottle is "just below the frame", so the close-up is about his eyes reading, not about rendering text next to a face.
4. **A repeated glance as the rhythm of the scene.** "One bottle, the door; another, the door." Tension comes from a repeated action, not from music or a face.
5. **A prop sound signature.** The pills rattle when a bottle is lifted, and the rattle is muffled once the bottle is in a pocket. The sound tells the story of the haul.
6. **The weapon's position is locked for continuity.** The rifle leans against the end of the shelf, "within reach", and "stays where he leaned it".

**Weak spots to watch:**
- **Too much action for Shot 3.** Pocketing one bottle, then taking three more with a door check between each, all in 4 s, is about one second per bottle-and-glance. The model will rush or morph hands. Give Shot 3 5–6 s, or make it three bottles in all.
- **Story logic against plates 09–10.** The pharmacy was "thoroughly looted" with the useful sections "bare board", yet here the needed medicine is still stocked. If this is a different, hidden storage room that looters missed, say so in SETTING (a locked back room, a door that was never forced). The audience will ask.
- **One light source, not two.** "From a dirty window or the open door" leaves the choice to the model. The plates name exact sources; do the same here.
- **The stock is back to tungsten 500** after 04 moved to daylight 250 for grey daylight. If the blue cast is wanted, say "cool"; if not, match 04.

**Pattern so far (01–12):** everything from 01–11, plus:
- product props with their own reference that carries their text;
- pre-emptive bans on the model's default additions (backpack);
- text kept out of face close-ups;
- a repeated action as the rhythm of tension;
- a sound signature for the key prop.
