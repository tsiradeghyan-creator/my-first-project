## What is new compared with 01–07 (Cloud, 2026-11-26)

1. **Point of view without a watcher, from exactly three signals, "and there is no fourth".**
   - foreground grass that partly blocks the view;
   - the frame rising and falling with a breath, about four times;
   - a braced, almost still camera with one small correction.
   
   The clichéd POV signals are banned by name: no scope reticle, no binocular mask, no HUD, no visible rifle. Subjective state is carried by camera behaviour alone.
2. **Scale defined by what can be read, not by "far".** "You can read arms rising and falling, and you can read nothing else… If more than that is readable, the camera is too close." This is a measurable rule for the model.
3. **Action designed for legibility at distance.** A blow is read as a change of silhouette height, so each one has four visible phases: load (taller), hold, strike (folded), recovery. This is anticipation and staging from animation, applied to an extreme wide shot.
4. **Anti-synchronisation.** The others arrive one by one at uneven intervals, so "no two arms ever rise or fall together; no loop, no choreography, no dance". AI crowds tend to move in sync and in loops; this breaks it with causality (different arrival times) instead of a bare ban.
5. **Spacing justified by physics.** "Each is swinging a long blade… men with machetes keep their spacing." A reason makes a constraint stick better than a rule alone.
6. **Atmospheric perspective done right.** It asks for 300 mm compression and an image "soft because of the air, not because it is out of focus", with "no heat shimmer — it is cold". It adds a visible fog layer with a top, and the ridge in slightly cleaner, warmer light.
7. **Sound from the watcher's place.** Wind and grass by the lens dominate, plus one controlled breath that matches the frame. The distant impacts are thin, stripped of low end, and **arrive late** (the speed of sound over distance). It is an expert detail, even if models may not honour the delay.
8. **Pacing by events.** It follows the animal's escape attempts, one man committing, then a staggered cascade with a deliberately late fifth arrival. The beats are designed as a sequence of readable events.

**Weak spots to watch:**
- **Too many events for 8 s at this scale.** Three stages, five separate arrivals, one camera correction, and figures only a few pixels tall. Video models often merge or duplicate tiny figures and lose the count. Consider cutting to three arrivals, or splitting stage one (the escape attempts) into its own shot.
- **The placeholder `<<<video_1>>>` means a different file in each prompt** (07: a distant wide; 08: a close, low shot). That is fine for one generation, but in a series bible give each reference video a unique name so notes do not mix them up.
- **Timecodes are seconds:frames again** (3:08, 3:18, 3:22). "No timecode, no numbers" in the image is the right guard against burn-in.
- **Length and capitals.** The same notes as 06 apply.
