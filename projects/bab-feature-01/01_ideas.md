# Stage 1: ideas (2026-11-03)

Six original ideas were pitched by Rhimes, Villeneuve and Blum (BAB seats, simulations). Quinn, Bajaria, Bloys and Tigran's seat scored them. Then all seven heads debated every idea over two rounds (D1, D2), and the buyers joined the final round.

## The six ideas (round 1 and round 2 scores)

| | Idea | Logline (short) | R2 score |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | «Քառասունքը» (The Fortieth Day) | 40 days of mourning around a Gyumri bakery; on the seventh day a woman says she is the fourth child | 6.4 |
| B | «Թարգմանչուհին» (The Interpreter) | a village interpreter in Syunik has seven days before a vote to decide which words to leave untranslated between the village and a foreign mining company | 7.0 |
| C | «Ջրի տակի տունը» (The House Under the Water) | a drained reservoir; a 78-year-old woman returns to her drowned village to find what she left in the wall | 5.4 |
| D | «Լեռան ականջը» (The Mountain's Ear) | winter 1988, a mountain seismic station records a repeating rumble | 5.6 |
| E | «Հոգեճաշ» (The Memorial Table) | at the grandfather's memorial dinner his recorded toast exposes everyone's secrets, until one of them turns out to be his killer | 6.8 |
| F | «Վերջին երթուղի» (Last Route) | a night minibus in a blizzard always has one passenger more than it should | 5.1 |

## Debate D1: every head judges every idea

- Five of seven heads proposed merging A and E (Rhimes, Blum, Deakins, Vermette, Tigran).
- C and F were dropped: Villeneuve conceded C (the reservoir is not ours to schedule), and no head picked F.

## The three finalists

- **B′ «Թարգմանչուհին» (lean):**
  - One Syunik village, with the club and the company guesthouse in the same village.
  - The two foreign actors work a 3–4 day block, and the road gets one day.
  - Villeneuve's layer: a low rumble from the mine that nobody wants to translate.
  - Murch's layer: one long table on the eve of the vote.
- **AE «Քառասունքը / Հոգեճաշ» (merged):**
  - Gyumri, one house plus a working bakery.
  - The memorial tables on days 3, 7 and 40 are the clock.
  - On day 7 a woman sits at the table and says she is the fourth child.
  - The patriarch's recorded toast opens the secrets.
  - Araksya (52) is the lead.
- **D′ «Լեռան ականջը» (built):**
  - The 1988 station is built in Yerevan, with a few exterior snow days.
  - Three actors, plus voices on the radio.
  - The disaster is never on screen.

## Board verdict

**B′ «Թարգմանչուհին», GO WITH CONDITIONS (7.2).** The margin over AE is 0.2, so the conditions decide.

Conditions:
1. Two foreign-language actors from Tigran's circle, for 3–4 days.
2. The rumble changes the interpreter's decision: it is a plot fact, not atmosphere.
3. The eve-of-vote table is cut with silences, and untranslated lines stay without subtitles.
4. The village and the company are fictional, the political care is solved in writing, and there is a legal check.
5. One village and one road day.

**Fallback: AE (7.0).** We switch to it without a new council if, by Gate 2:
- condition 1 fails, or
- the rumble is not a plot fact in the concept.

If AE is chosen:
- Keep the murder (7 of 10 seats), and tie it to Araksya's choice. The killer must not be guessed before the day-40 table.
- Use a real Gyumri house and a working bakery in one neighbourhood, with rent and closed days agreed in writing.
- Light the three tables three different ways.
- Gyumri readers check the script.
