## STABLE — safe to apply without re-verifying

Held across multiple years and sources:

- **SEO beats hashtags for discovery.** Keywords in the caption's first line, on-screen text, spoken audio (Instagram transcribes it), and the name field drive reach more than tags. Hashtags are categorization labels now; following hashtags was removed in late 2024.
- **Per-surface ranking + per-account trust score.** Feed, Reels, Stories, Explore each rank differently; a run of weak posts suppresses the next several, strong ones recover fast. Quality-consistency > volume.
- **First ~3 seconds decide a reel.** Hook visually + verbally + on-screen at once. Most viewers watch without sound, so on-screen text is mandatory.
- **Saves and DM-sends are the strongest distribution signals.** Profile-visit-rate is a fast-rising feed/carousel signal — one post that makes someone tap the handle widens the next post's reach.
- **Originality is scored.** External watermarks (TikTok/CapCut) and verbatim-recycled content get downranked. Re-record natively.
- **Carousels for saves/retention, Reels for cold reach.** Single images underperform both.
- **Niche consistency compounds.** A visitor who taps the profile and sees the same kind of work is far likelier to follow — don't dilute the grid.
- **Early-window comment activity feeds the ranker.** Replying inside the first day or two — and getting replies back — is itself a quality signal. The in-app "first two days" prompt is Instagram telling on its own ranker.

## Step 1 — Frame before writing

Determine, asking the user if unclear:

1. **Account** — agency (default) or confirmed personal account
2. **Audience layer** — *industry* (producers/directors/writers/festivals), *craft* (aspiring writers/students), or *general* (viewers/readers). One post serves one layer; content aimed at all three reaches none.
3. **Goal** — saves, profile visits, follows, DM conversations, or industry visibility
4. **Language** — Armenian / Russian / English / which mix, matched to the layer

Generic captions fail in this niche. Without these four, ask first.

-----

## Step 2 — Content pillars

Generic 60/20/10 splits don't fit a creative-industry account. Use:

- **35% Work showcase** — produced/ongoing projects, milestones (no spoilers)
- **25% Craft education** — structural breakdowns, principles, lessons
- **20% Industry commentary** — Armenian film/TV landscape, shifts, honest takes
- **15% Behind-the-scenes** — writing room, process, collaborations
- **5% Direct CTA** — services, collaboration, hiring

(If a personal account is confirmed, shift its mix toward reflection/opinion/recommendations, away from showcase/CTA.)

-----

## Step 3 — Caption frameworks

**A. Industry insight** — counterintuitive opening (1–2 lines) → reasoning with one concrete example (4–8) → practical takeaway (1–2) → soft question CTA. For craft/industry layers.

**B. Project announcement** — tease (1) → context: type/stage/who's involved (2–4) → personal angle: why it matters (2–3) → hard CTA (follow/save/date).

**C. Personal essay** (personal account only) — opens on a *specific image or moment*, not abstraction; reads as short-form essay, not marketing; ≤2 emoji embedded in prose, never as bullets. Armenian: «դու» not «Դուք». Russian: «ты» in personal register, «вы» when formal. English: always «you».

**D. Educational carousel** — cover (hook + "Swipe →") → problem framing → one idea per slide → concrete example → recap → CTA (save + follow + comment). Highest-save format.

**E. Dialogue post** (personal account, rare) — anonymized question → substantial 4–8 line answer → open invitation. Doesn't work on the agency account.

## Step 4 — Reels & Stories execution

**Reels** — front-load the hook (see STABLE). Verify current length guidance before specifying any duration. Strong formats here: "scenes I rewrote N times", "why most [genre] scripts fail", "how to start a screenplay", walking-and-talking reflection, desk-cam process with voice-over. Speak target keywords aloud (transcribed for search). No external watermarks.

**Stories** — priority order for this niche: process moments (desk/notebook/screen) → recommendations with personal take → BTS → polls/quizzes testing Ani's actual opinions (not "which color") → monthly Q&A → permissioned DM reshares → collaboration reposts. Highlights: 5–7 max — Projects, Process, Books, Press, Q&A — more dilutes focus.

## Step 5 — Hashtags & profile

**Hashtags** — selection matters more than count; verify the current recommended count before finalizing. Niche/specific over generic; never #love/#instagood. Relevant pools: industry (`#screenwriter` `#screenwriting` `#showrunner`), Armenian (`#armeniancinema` `#armenianfilm` `#yerevan`), Russian (`#сценарист` `#кино` `#сериал`), branded (`#maghakianscripts`). Add geo-tag (Yerevan) and tag collaborators.

**Profile** — the **name field** (not username) is what Instagram search indexes: keep it keyword-rich, e.g. "Maghakian | Screenwriting & Production". Don't change the username (breaks legacy links). Pin 3 posts: best portfolio piece / identity-establishing / current focus. Re-check pins every ~2 months.

## Step 6 — Industry networking through content

The screenwriting industry is small; treat Instagram as professional networking, not just audience-building.

- **Tagging** — tag every involved professional on project posts; tag real creators when referencing influences.
- **Comments on others' posts** — to industry peers, write substantive 2–3 line replies, not "thanks ❤️" — peers notice how you show up in others' comments.
- **DMs** — short and specific; never pitch in a cold DM, just request an email exchange. For mentor-figures, thoughtful public comments outperform cold DMs.

## Step 7 — Replying to comments on owned posts

The in-app prompt that says replying in the early window boosts reach is correct — early-window comment activity is a ranking input (see STABLE). But identical "Շնորհակալություն 🤍" pasted under every comment is read by both viewers and the ranker as noise. Tier the reply by comment depth.

### Before drafting, ask if unclear

1. **Account voice** — agency "we/մենք/мы" (default) vs personal "I/ես/я". Stay consistent across the whole comment thread of one post.
2. **Post topic** — needed to decide whether substantive comments deserve a topical reply or just warmth (e.g., a launch tease invites only warmth; an essay/BTS post invites a back-and-forth).
3. **Language priority** — reply in the language the commenter used. If they wrote in Armenian, reply in Armenian; same for Russian/English. Don't switch languages on them.

### Comment tiers and response shape

**Tier 1 — Emoji-only or 1–3 word reactions** (❤️, 🔥🔥, 🥺😍, "սիրուն ա", "огонь")
- One short warm line, ≤6 words, one or two emoji max.
- Vary the wording across the thread — never paste the same line under five comments in a row. The ranker and human readers both notice.
- Don't escalate: a heart deserves a heart back, not a paragraph.
- Examples (agency voice):
  - HY: «Շնորհակալ ենք սիրո համար 🤍» / «🤍 շնորհակալություն» / «Միասին ենք զգում 🔥»
  - RU: «Спасибо за тепло 🤍» / «🤍 благодарим»
  - EN: "Thank you 🤍" / "Means a lot 🤍"

**Tier 2 — Short emotional comments** ("ամեն դետալ նիշում եմ 🥺🤍", "до мурашек", "this hit me")
- One sentence that *names the thing they noticed* — details, mood, a specific element — then a short thanks.
- Show you actually read the comment.
- Examples:
  - HY: «Ամենաթանկ արձագանքն է, երբ դետալները նկատվում են 🤍 Շնորհակալություն։»
  - RU: «Самое ценное — когда замечают детали. Спасибо 🤍»

**Tier 3 — Substantive comments** (paragraph-length, opinion, craft observation, regional/cultural anchor)
- 2–3 lines. Pick up the *specific* thing they said (a name, a place, a craft point), affirm it, add one line of your own perspective, then thank.
- This is where the agency builds reputation — peers and casual visitors both scroll comments.
- Never copy-paste; each Tier-3 reply is bespoke.
- If they raise a regional/cultural anchor (Gyumri, Spitak, diaspora, a specific era), acknowledge it by name; don't generalize it away.
- Example pattern (agency, HY):
  - «Շատ ճիշտ եք նկատել 🤍 Յուրաքանչյուր մանրուք իր տեղն ու իմաստն ունի, իսկ Գյումրին՝ առանձնահատուկ տեղ մեր սրտում։ Շնորհակալ ենք, որ զգում եք խորությունը։»

**Tier 4 — Industry/peer comments** (another writer, director, producer, festival account)
- Treat as Step 6 networking — 2–3 substantive lines, no fan-tone. If a public reply isn't enough, take it to DM and say so («գրենք մեյլով»/«в личке»).
- Tag back only if it's natural; don't tag-bomb peers.

**Tier 5 — Critical / cold / off-topic**
- Don't ignore a substantive critique, but don't argue in the thread. One short, non-defensive reply («արժեքավոր է նկատառումը, կմտածենք»), then move on.
- For spam / unrelated promo — hide, don't reply. Replying boosts it.
- For dogpile or hostile threads — stop replying after one calm message; the ranker reads long argument threads as low quality.

### Timing and rhythm

- Try to handle replies inside the window Instagram itself prompts for (verify the current wording in-app; historically "first two days"). After that the marginal ranking lift drops.
- Reply in *waves*, not all at once — a burst of identical-timed replies looks automated. Two or three batched passes across the early window is fine.
- Heart-react every comment you don't reply to in text. Silence reads as inattention.

### Anti-patterns specific to comment replies

- Pasting the same line under every comment.
- Replying in English when the commenter wrote in Armenian / Russian.
- Writing a paragraph under a single-emoji comment (over-eager, off-voice).
- Slipping into "I" on the agency account, or "we" on the personal one, in replies.
- Tagging the commenter back when they're already in-thread (clutter, no benefit).
- Auto-translations — read the comment first; machine translation misses tone in Armenian/Russian especially.

## Step 8 — Diagnosing growth

Track in priority order: saves/reach → DM-sends/reach → profile-visits/reach → follows-from-post → reel watch-through → DM conversations → reply-rate on owned posts within the early window. **Do not quote fixed ER benchmarks from memory** — verify current benchmarks for the account's size band.

Review every 2 weeks: which 3 posts earned the highest saves/reach, which format won, what pattern to double down on. Reach volatility is the trust score recalibrating, not a shadowban — strong posts pull it back.
